2 · Neuron browser: both poles

In SmolLM2's MLP, a neuron's value is silu(gate)·up, the number down_proj reads. It can be strongly positive or strongly negative, so each neuron has two poles. Below are the ten strongest contexts for each pole in one million tokens of Wikipedia text. The highlighted token is where the neuron fired; the bar is its activation as a share of the pole's maximum.
