Scaling laws

Module 19 · Why they work ← All explorables

Six small character-level GPTs, the same recipe, one pass over the same 4 million characters of Wikipedia. Only the width changes. A scaling law is a guess that the loss follows a simple curve in the number of parameters N and the number of training tokens D. Below: the six real curves first, then a surface fitted to them that you can push past the data, then the compute question: for a fixed budget, a big model trained briefly or a small one trained long?
