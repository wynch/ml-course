Retrieval and memory

A language model only knows what its weights hold and what its prompt shows it. Retrieval fills the prompt: search a pile of text for the passages that best match the question, then paste them in. Below, the pile is 200 Wikipedia paragraphs from SQuAD. Type any question to rank them with BM25, which counts shared words, computed live in this page. Pick one of the 200 SQuAD questions to also see the cosine ranking from MiniLM's vectors (precomputed: the page cannot run MiniLM) and what SmolLM2-360M answered with and without a passage.

    2 · Why BM25 ranked its first paragraph there

    score = Σ over query words of idf × tf·(k1 + 1) / (tf + k1·(1 − b + b·len/avglen)). A word in every paragraph has an idf near zero; a rare word near ln(200) ≈ 5.3. Repeats help less and less (tf saturates), and a long paragraph needs more hits for the same score. Over these 200 paragraphs only, so the numbers differ from the module's 2,067-paragraph run.
