In-context learning

Module 20 · Why they work ← All explorables

A transformer that never updates its weights can still fit a rule to examples you put in its prompt. That is in-context learning. Below, a small transformer trained only on prompts of points from random lines runs in your browser: place points and watch its guess and the least-squares line move together. Then the module's measured numbers: the 5-dimensional net against four classic rules, a hedged look at whether a layer acts like one gradient step, and few-shot sentiment on a 360M-parameter language model.
