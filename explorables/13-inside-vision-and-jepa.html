Inside vision and JEPA models

Module 13 · Looking inside ← All explorables

Four ways to look inside vision models. A channel browser shows what 21 channels of InceptionV1 do on six sets of images, among them one channel said to detect V shapes. Feature visualisation paints the image a channel wants. A probe and a neuron browser look inside CLIP's image tower. A last panel asks what a small JEPA encoder keeps about its tile world. Every number here comes from the module's own runs.

1 · Channel browser: InceptionV1 layer 4e, channels 45 to 65

Each dot is one image: the channel's strongest response anywhere in the picture (before the ReLU). Six sets of 200 images each. The black tick is the median. The gap is the median on Vs minus the larger of the cat and car medians, in units of the pooled spread; a channel that preferred drawn Vs would have a large positive gap. The claim tested here is about pointed shapes in photos, so drawn Vs may not match it: a miss of this test, not a refutation.
