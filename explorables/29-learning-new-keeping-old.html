Learning something new without losing the old

Module 29 · How learning works ← All explorables

A small model that knows Shakespeare learns a second text, GSM8K's worked maths solutions. While it learns, its loss on Shakespeare rises: it forgets. This page replays the module's 18 runs: six ways of learning, 3 seeds each, scored every 25 steps. Pick what goes on the horizontal axis and ask whether one number, the KL divergence from the start model (how far its predictions moved), tells how much each run forgot. Then watch a network lose the ability to learn at all, and a real chat model do the same trade.

1 · Learn, forget, and the one number

Each dot is one run at one step; the large dot is where it stands at the step you choose, and the thin line is its path so far. Vertical axis: forgetting, the rise of the Shakespeare loss since step 0. Click an arm to hide or show it. The chips give the Spearman rank correlation between forgetting and the chosen axis over the 18 runs at that step: 1 means the axis puts the runs in exactly the order of their forgetting.
