ML course quizzes

10 quizzes · 100 questions Explorables ← Course home

Ten self-graded quizzes, one per module — ten questions each, every one grounded in that module's own code, figures and measured numbers.

Answer in any order; a question locks as soon as you answer it and reveals why the right answer is right and why the tempting one is not. Nothing is uploaded: your answers and scores live in this browser's localStorage, and each quiz has a Reset button. Take one right after you finish a module — or after playing with its explorable.
