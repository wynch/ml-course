Whisper — Weak Supervision at 680k Hours Beats Curated SSL

source created 2026-05-06 updated 2026-05-06 speech audio asr weak-supervision multimodal openai whisper

Radford, Kim, Xu, Brockman, McLeavey, Sutskever (OpenAI) — arXiv 2212.04356, December 2022

The paper that re-orients speech recognition around scaling weak supervision rather than scaling self-supervised pretraining. Whisper trains a vanilla encoder–decoder Transformer on 680,000 hours of (audio, transcript) pairs scraped from the internet — multilingual, multi-domain, multi-task — and shows the resulting model transfers zero-shot to most ASR benchmarks at parity with fully supervised specialists.

Recipe

  • Architecture. Off-the-shelf encoder–decoder Transformer. Audio → log-Mel spectrogram → conv stem → encoder. Decoder generates text autoregressively conditioned on encoder output and special tokens that select the task: <|transcribe|> vs <|translate|>, language ID, timestamps on/off.
  • Data. 680k hours total: 117k hours covering 96 non-English languages; 125k hours of X→English translation; the rest English transcription. Sourced from internet audio + paired transcripts; aggressive heuristic filtering removes machine-translated and machine-generated transcripts.
  • Training. Standard cross-entropy next-token prediction. No self-supervised pretraining stage; no curated labelled corpus; no fine-tuning per benchmark.

Headline empirical claim

Whisper-large is competitive with or better than fully supervised baselines on most standard ASR benchmarks (LibriSpeech, CommonVoice, TED-LIUM, etc.) without ever fine-tuning on the benchmark training set. The argument: at 680k hours, weak supervision substitutes for both curated labels and SSL.

Significance

Whisper is the canonical counter-example to the Wav2Vec 2.0 thesis that contrastive SSL is the right substrate for speech. Both routes work; Whisper says scale of weakly-supervised paired data dominates carefully designed self-supervised objectives once the data is available. The same tension recurs across modalities — see source-clip-radford (paired data + contrastive) vs source-dino-lineage (pure SSL) for the vision analogue.

The multitask interface (task-selecting special tokens) prefigures the general unified-token discipline now standard in concept-multimodal systems and in the RT-2-style VLA models that emit robot actions as tokens.
