Beyond Sparse Denoising In Frames

source created 2026-05-14 updated 2026-05-14 wavelets scattering-transform denoising robustness computer-vision stephane-mallat

Nathanaël Cuvelle-Magar and Stéphane Mallat — arXiv 2025

This recent paper connects wavelet scattering to denoising and geometric regularity. It argues that sparse frame estimators are not rich enough for complex image regularities and proposes a denoising estimator based on different subsets of scattering coefficients.

Main Claim

Scattering coefficients can capture geometric image regularities that are hard to adapt to with ordinary sparse frame thresholding. Numerical experiments suggest minimax behavior for cartoon images across a range of Lipschitz exponents, stated by the authors as a mathematical conjecture.

Why It Matters

This is one of the most direct supports for the wavelet-substrate hypothesis:

  • noise robustness should be part of the substrate, not only learned by augmentation
  • residual learning can focus on what scattering fails to explain
  • image regularity can be represented by structured coefficient families rather than a generic learned early network

The proposed experiment-wavelet-substrate-network should therefore include noise and blur stress tests from the beginning.

Caveat

The paper is about denoising and geometric regularity, not small-device classification or continual learning. It motivates the substrate but does not prove the edge-intelligence claim.
