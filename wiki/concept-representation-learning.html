Representation Learning

concept created 2026-04-05 updated 2026-10-09 representations embeddings features learning

How models learn internal representations of data, and what makes good representations.

Key Results

  • source-ijepa-self-supervised-learning — I-JEPA: predicting in representation space (not pixel space) without hand-crafted augmentations. Part of LeCun's JEPA vision.
  • source-platonic-representation-aristotelian — challenges representation convergence metrics; representations converge to shared local neighbourhoods, not global statistical models.
  • source-drifting-model-representation — Drifting VQ-VAE fixes codebook collapse in discrete representations.
  • source-from-latent-to-weight-space — exploring the relationship between latent space and weight space.
  • source-unified-latents-diffusion — Google DeepMind's UL framework jointly trains encoder + diffusion prior + diffusion decoder via noise-level alignment; SOTA ImageNet/Kinetics.
  • source-contrastive-learning-gaussian-representations — contrastive objectives provably yield Gaussian marginals via spherical uniformity + Maxwell-Poincaré.
  • source-infoldm-predictive-ssl-identifiability — identifiability for predictive SSL: with a random signal and nuisance together, maximising predictive mutual information while matching the latents to a Gaussian predictor recovers the signal up to an affine map, given the optimum (raw/arxiv-2609-37789-predictive-ssl-identifies-stochastic-signals-under-nuisance.md, §3.2, Corollary 1). Synthetic R² is 0.99 at 5 signal dimensions and 0.68 at 20 (same file, Table 1).
  • source-weak-sigreg — regularizes sketched covariance toward identity (isotropy) to prevent dimensional collapse; ports the VICReg/SIGReg anti-collapse move into supervised ViT/MLP training.
  • source-neural-low-degree-filtering — 2026 theory paper: layers as iterative spectral filters that select accessible low-degree label-correlated directions, giving a tractable model of hierarchical feature learning beyond lazy random features.
  • concept-wavelet-scattering — fixed multiscale wavelet geometry as an alternative to fully learned early representations; tied to analysis-2026-wavelet-scattering-update and experiment-wavelet-substrate-network.

Tools for Analysis

  • source-manifold-discovery-analysis-mda — Stanford method for visualising regression-DNN features by reconstructing the label manifold (Nature Comms).

Biological Parallels

  • source-visual-cortex-dimensionality — human visual cortex representations may be much higher-dimensional than thought, and those dimensions are behaviourally relevant.

Physics-Informed ML (formerly its own concept page)

Incorporating physical laws, symmetries, or constraints into ML architectures or training to improve generalization, especially for scientific applications. A specific inductive-bias-as-representation story: hard-coding conservation laws or symmetries shapes what representations the model can learn.

  • source-physics-informed-gnn-newtons-laws — GNN that learns Newton's laws from data and extrapolates to 35x larger systems. Published in Nature Communications.

Connection to Interpretability

Representation learning and concept-mechanistic-interpretability are deeply intertwined — understanding what representations models learn is central to interpreting them. See source-nonlinear-feature-representations for theoretical analysis of feature geometry in transformers, and source-tensorlens-attention-tensors for end-to-end aggregation of transformer representations into a single tensor.
