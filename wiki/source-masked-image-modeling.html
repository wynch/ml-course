Masked Image Modeling — MAE and iBOT

source created 2026-05-07 updated 2026-05-07 self-supervised-learning masked-image-modeling vision vit representation-learning foundational

Two papers four days apart, both November 2021, both adapting BERT-style masked-token reconstruction to Vision Transformers. They define the two flavours of "BERT for vision" and disagree on what to predict: pixel space (MAE) vs EMA-teacher embedding space (iBOT). The split foreshadows the larger debate that produced concept-jepa.

MAE — He et al. (FAIR, Nov 11 2021)

arXiv: 2111.06377. He, Chen, Xie, Li, Dollár, Girshick.

Three asymmetries that made masked-pixel reconstruction finally work for vision:

  1. 75% mask — counter-intuitive vs language modeling's ~15%, but necessary because images are spatially redundant; masking less leaves the task too easy.
  2. Encoder sees only visible patches — encoder cost shrinks ~4×; the throughput trick that makes MAE genuinely scalable.
  3. Shallow decoder reconstructs raw pixels of masked patches under MSE. Discarded after pretraining; only the encoder transfers.

ViT-Huge fine-tuned reaches 87.8% ImageNet top-1 (SOTA for ImageNet-only training at the time). Pixel-space target was a sharp departure from the prior consensus that pixel targets were "too low-level" (cf. BEiT's discrete-token prediction).

iBOT — Zhou et al. (Nov 15 2021)

arXiv: 2111.07832. Zhou, Wei, Wang, Shen, Xie, Yuille, Kong.

Masked image modeling without an offline tokenizer — instead, a momentum-EMA teacher network produces patch-level target representations on the fly, combined with a DINO-style image-level self-distillation objective. A single ViT pretraining recipe that learns both global semantic (CLS-token) and local patch features.

Two losses jointly applied:

  1. Image-level self-distillation (DINO-style): student CLS-token ↔ teacher CLS-token cross-view, encouraging globally consistent representations.
  2. Patch-level MIM: at each masked position, the student must match the teacher's representation of the same patch (taken from the unmasked view). BERT-style local objective, but the "vocabulary" is the teacher's evolving embedding space, not a fixed discrete codebook.

Strong linear/fine-tune ImageNet results, especially strong dense / segmentation transfer (because the patch-level objective forces locally meaningful features).

iBOT's teacher → DINOv2 lineage is direct: the iBOT objective scaled and tuned becomes part of source-dino-lineage. MAE's pixel targets remained influential in video MAE, audio MAE, and modality variants.

Connection to LLM masked language modeling

MAE is the cleanest vision analogue of BERT-style MLM: a fixed fraction of input tokens replaced with mask tokens; reconstruct under cross-entropy/MSE. The conceptual difference is the mask ratio (75% vs ~15%), forced by the redundancy structure of natural images vs natural language. This same modality-driven gap is one motivation source-ijepa-self-supervised-learning gives for predicting in representation space rather than pixel space — a reaction against MAE that puts it at the centre of the concept-jepa debate.

Why this pair matters

  • They unified the mask-and-reconstruct paradigm across modalities — a conceptual bridge between LLM and vision SSL canons.
  • Reset the prior assumption that generative pixel-level objectives can't compete with joint-embedding methods (MAE's contribution).
  • Established that EMA-teacher representation prediction could replace fixed tokenizers (iBOT's contribution).
  • Together they form the direct precursors of DINOv2 (source-dino-lineage), which combines the iBOT objective with DINO image-level self-distillation.
  • Frame the debate that produced JEPA: pixel space vs representation space, generative vs joint-embedding.
