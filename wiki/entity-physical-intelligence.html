Physical Intelligence

entity created 2026-10-20 updated 2026-10-20 organization robotics vla flow-matching

A robot-learning company in San Francisco (raw/arxiv-2410-24164-0-a-vision-language-action-flow.md, title note) that builds vision-language-action (VLA) models: one network that takes camera images and a spoken or written task and outputs motor commands. Its papers list Sergey Levine, Chelsea Finn and Karol Hausman among the authors (raw/arxiv-2504-16054-pi-0-5-a-vision-language-action.md, title block; source-pi-0-5).

Findings that matter here

  • Training on 104 locations matches training on the test homes themselves; removing other robots' data hurts most; web data helps with unseen objects, not with the four scored tasks (raw/arxiv-2504-16054-pi-0-5-a-vision-language-action.md, §V-B, §V-C).
  • Contradiction (partial), carried from source-pi-0-5: OpenVLA found no need to co-train on web data; π0.5 finds other robots' data and web data drive generalisation to new homes and objects (source-openvla).

Caveat: the company tests its own models on its own robots; no outside group has rerun the comparisons (raw/arxiv-2504-16054-pi-0-5-a-vision-language-action.md; source-pi-0-5, "Caveats").
