3D Generation Foundations

source created 2026-05-07 updated 2026-05-07 3d-generation 3d-vision autoregressive gaussian-splatting transformers shape-abstraction unsupervised

A pair of 3D-generation works that apply transformer architectures to two complementary parts of the 3D pipeline: autoregressive generation of 3D Gaussian scenes (GaussianGPT), and hierarchical unsupervised shape abstraction (HiT). Together they sketch how transformer machinery — autoregression, hierarchy — transfers from language and 2D vision into 3D.

GaussianGPT: Autoregressive 3D Gaussian Scene Generation — von Lützow, Rössle, Schmid, Nießner

Technical University of Munich. Project page.

Transformer-based approach to generating 3D scenes using autoregressive, token-by-token prediction of 3D Gaussians. Treats 3D scene construction as a sequential process rather than holistic refinement.

Mechanism

  • Compresses 3D Gaussians into discrete tokens via a sparse 3D convolutional autoencoder with vector quantisation (VQ).
  • GPT-style causal transformer with 3D rotary positional embeddings generates token sequences.
  • Enables: unconditional generation, scene completion/outpainting, controllable sampling via temperature.

Distinctive contribution: the autoregressive paradigm as alternative to diffusion / flow-matching for 3D. Imports the GPT recipe — discrete tokens, causal next-token prediction, temperature-controlled sampling — into 3D Gaussian splatting. Tokenisation via sparse 3D conv + VQ is the load-bearing step that makes the language-modelling analogy work.

HiT: Hierarchical Transformers for Unsupervised 3D Shape Abstraction — Vora

arXiv 2510.27088. Code. Presented at 3DV.

Hierarchical transformer approach for unsupervised 3D shape abstraction — learning compact part-level structural representations of 3D shapes without supervision.

Distinctive contribution: brings the hierarchical transformer pattern to the shape-abstraction problem, where prior unsupervised work largely used non-transformer architectures. Targets the "explain a shape as a small number of structured parts" objective rather than dense reconstruction.

Why this grouping matters

3D generation in the transformer era is splitting along two axes:

AxisGaussianGPTHiT
GoalGenerate full scenesAbstract shapes into parts
ArchitectureCausal / autoregressiveHierarchical
Representation3D Gaussians + VQ tokensPart-level / structural
SupervisionGenerative pretrainingUnsupervised
InspirationGPT / language modellingHierarchical vision transformers

Together they sketch the method space for applying transformer architectures to 3D: take a tokenisation that maps 3D geometry to discrete units, then pick autoregression (for generation) or hierarchy (for structure). Both are early signals that transformer recipes from text/2D transfer non-trivially into 3D — though each has its own bridging mechanism (VQ tokens for one, hierarchical attention for the other).
