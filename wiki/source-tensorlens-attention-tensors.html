TensorLens

source created 2026-05-06 updated 2026-05-06 mechanistic-interpretability transformer-architecture attention tensor-methods 2026

Atad, Zimerman, Katz, Wolf (Tel Aviv U / IBM Research) — ACL 2026, arXiv 2601.17958

Reformulates a transformer as a single input-dependent high-order attention tensor that jointly encodes attention, FFNs, activations, normalisations, and residuals. Where conventional interpretability stops at attention matrices (a shallow per-head/per-layer view), TensorLens treats the whole forward pass as one composable tensor object that can be projected back to any subset of heads, layers, or paths.

Key Claims

  • Attention matrices ignore embeddings/FFNs/values and proliferate per (head, layer) — TensorLens collapses these into one object.
  • Tensor formulation is modular: aggregate any subset of circuits into a unified operator.
  • Empirically yields richer representations than prior attention-aggregation methods; useful for linear relation decoding from embeddings (source-rome-memit-factual-editing-style readouts).
  • Provides theoretical grounding for end-to-end mechanistic interpretability.
