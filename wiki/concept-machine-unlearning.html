Machine Unlearning

concept created 2026-06-18 updated 2026-06-18 machine-unlearning privacy ai-alignment architecture

Machine unlearning is the problem of removing the influence of specific training data (a document, a user, a source, a fact) from a trained model — ideally as if it had never been trained on — while preserving everything else the model learned. It is driven by privacy ("right to be forgotten"), copyright/licensing, and safety (removing harmful or sensitive knowledge).

The core tension

Unlearning pits two goals against each other:

  • Isolation — a source's contribution must be separable so it can be removed.
  • Joint learning — but training jointly across all sources is what gives the model its general capability and cross-source transfer.

Naive approaches sit at the extremes: full retraining without the data (correct but prohibitively expensive) or gradient-based "forgetting" fine-tunes (cheap but leaky, and prone to damaging unrelated capabilities or being undone by relearning attacks).

Approaches in the wiki

  • Architectural / native unlearning — source-natively-unlearnable-llms (NULLs): bake separability into the architecture. Shared backbone neurons carry general knowledge; sparsely activated per-source "sinks" absorb source-specific information. Unlearning becomes a deployment-time switch (disable the sinks) with no gradient updates and no access to retained data. Scales to ~6M Wikipedia-article sources and resists relearning/extraction attacks.
  • Post-hoc knowledge editing — source-rome-memit-factual-editing: locate and edit specific factual associations in the weights. A removal/modification tool, but surgical and weight-level rather than a clean deployment switch.

Related ideas

  • Conditional computation as the enabling mechanism. Per-source sinks are a routing/sparsity pattern adjacent to concept-mixture-of-experts and the addressable-memory idea in source-engram-deepseek — here used for removability rather than capacity.
  • Privacy filtering at the architecture level — compare the encoder-MoE source-openai-privacy-filter.
  • A safety/governance lever, so it touches concept-ai-alignment.

Open questions

  • Does deployment-time sink disabling fully erase influence, or only suppress easy retrieval? (NULLs argues robustness against extraction; the general guarantee is unsettled.)
  • How does native unlearnability trade off against peak capability versus a jointly trained dense model?
  • Can the source/provenance granularity be made finer than whole documents (e.g. per-fact) without an explosion of sink parameters?
