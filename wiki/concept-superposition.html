Superposition

concept created 2026-10-17 updated 2026-10-17 mechanistic-interpretability superposition features neurons stub

Stub. A proposed reason why a neuron seems to mean several unrelated things (concept-polysemanticity). The word has more than one meaning, and the meanings make different claims.

Definitions

SenseClaimWhere in this wiki
More features than dimensionsA layer stores more sparse features than it has dimensions, as directions that are not orthogonalToy Models of Superposition (2022), in source-anthropic-mi-foundations
Arbitrary packingA network short of room puts unrelated things in one neuron because they seldom collideThe sense source-goodfire-already-clean tests (R, "Superposition")
Composition against superpositionTwo rival ways to lay features out across neuronsOlah's 2023 note Distributed Representations: Composition & Superposition, as the Goodfire essay reports it (R, App. "A Note on 'Superposition'")

R is raw/goodfire-already-clean-2026-10-15.md. The essay counts "five or six" uses of the word in papers and agents' replies (R, App. "A Note on Features and Definitions").

How it changed

  • Zoom In (2020) brought the word into circuits work with one example, a car feature that seemed to spread into dog neurons (R, App. "A Note on 'Superposition'"; source-olah-2020-zoom-in-circuits).
  • Toy Models of Superposition (2022) gave the "more features than dimensions" definition and a phase diagram for when features share directions (source-anthropic-mi-foundations).
  • The SAE programme took that as its reason to read features from dictionaries, not from neurons (source-sae-monosemanticity-2023, source-sae-scaling-trilogy).
  • The 2026 Goodfire essay says the Zoom In example was a misreading (shared pose plus feature visualization drawn toward dogs), and that no one has published a second circuit of that shape (R, "The Short Version"; "Cars and Animals"). It finds no arbitrary packing in the vision models it trained and calls "more features than dimensions" ordinary composition, "good news" (R, App. "A Note on Features and Definitions").

Contradiction: source-anthropic-mi-foundations says Toy Models "links superposition to polysemanticity" and that neurons are the wrong unit "when features outnumber dimensions". source-goodfire-already-clean holds that the neuron polysemanticity people reported came from weak tools, weak architecture and "recursive generalization", not packing (R, "Superposition of the Gaps"; App. "Relation to Conjoined Features"). It finds "no problems" with the toy experiments and agrees the toy packs; it disputes that real networks live in the toy's world, where every feature is "an end in itself" and nothing reuses what features share (R, App. "Toy Models"). Partial: the two disagree on real networks, not on the toy.

Open questions

  • Does arbitrary packing ever appear in a real network? The essay's test: a rare fact should ride on neurons related to its topic, not on unrelated ones (R, App. "Does Scale Help or Hurt Superposition?").
  • Is attention in superposition? The essay calls Anthropic's attention-superposition note possibly "a terminology issue" and says no one yet understands heads well enough to judge (R, App. "Relation to Attention Superposition").
  • The residual stream has no privileged basis. Is that superposition? The essay says no by itself, citing Olah's 2023 note calling a rotated representation "trivial" (R, App. "A Note on Features and Definitions").
