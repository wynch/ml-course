Knowledge Distillation

concept created 2026-10-20 updated 2026-10-20 distillation self-distillation model-compression on-policy continual-learning hub

Distillation trains a "student" model to match a "teacher" model's outputs instead of, or beside, the true labels. Hinton, Vinyals and Dean (2015) made the student match the teacher's full output distribution, softened by a temperature, so that it also learns which wrong answers the teacher thinks are close (arXiv 1503.02531; not in raw; source-hinton-knowledge-distillation).

analysis-self-distillation-as-universal-lever argues that one mechanism, a model or a copy of it acting as teacher, runs through SSL, preference tuning, RL and retrofitting. This page is the hub for the method itself: who teaches, on what text, and what the wiki's sources measured.

What the sources measured

  • Big to small, off-policy. DeepSeek fine-tuned six open models (Qwen2.5 1.5B to 32B, Llama 3.1-8B and 3.3-70B) on 800k samples from DeepSeek-R1, with SFT only and no RL (raw/arxiv-2501-12948-deepseek-r1-incentivizing-reasoning-capability-in.md, Appendix B.4.3, Appendix F). R1-Distill-Qwen-32B scores 72.6 on AIME 2024 against 47.0 for the same base model after more than 10k steps of large-scale RL; the authors conclude that distilling a stronger model beats RL on a small one, though pushing past human level may still need bigger bases and RL (same file, Appendix F.1, Table 16).
  • Big to small, features. DINOv2 distils its smaller ViTs from its largest, ViT-g/14, instead of training them from scratch; a distilled ViT-L/14 beats one trained from scratch on all 12 benchmarks tried (raw/arxiv-2304-07193-dinov2-learning-robust-visual-features-without.md, §6.5).
  • On-policy, from a stronger teacher. The student writes its own answers and the teacher scores each token by reverse KL. On Qwen3-8B-Base after SFT, about 150 steps lift AIME'24 from 60% to 70%; matching an RL-trained teacher takes 7–10× fewer gradient steps than RL, about 50–100× less compute (raw/web-2025-thinkingmachines-on-policy-distillation.md, "On-policy distillation", "Dense supervision greatly improves compute efficiency"; source-on-policy-distillation). Company blog.
  • On-policy, from the model's own earlier self. After fine-tuning Qwen3-8B on company documents cut IF-eval from 85% to 79%, distilling from the original model on chat prompts brought it back to 83% (same file, "On-policy distillation recovers post-training behavior").
  • On-policy, from the same model with a hint. SDFT makes the teacher the model itself with one demonstration in its context. On Qwen2.5-7B it learns new skills while keeping its old-benchmark average at 64.5–65.4 against a base of 65.5, where SFT falls to 53.4–60.2; it fails at 3B, where in-context learning is too weak to make a good teacher (raw/arxiv-2601-19897-self-distillation-enables-continual-learning.md, §4.3, §4.4, Table 5; source-sdft-self-distillation-continual). SDPO does the same with feedback in context in place of a demonstration (arXiv 2601.20802, abstract; not in raw; source-sdpo-self-distillation-rl).

Where it is used

  • Shipping small models: source-lacy-small-language-models lets a small model hand hard tokens to a bigger one through a <CALL> token, a cascade rather than distillation (arXiv 2602.12005, abstract; not in raw).
  • Retrofitting: changing a model's architecture or tokenizer and using the old model as teacher (concept-retrofitting).
  • Continual learning: distillation as a repair after, or a guard during, fine-tuning (concept-continual-learning).

Open questions

  • Does on-policy distillation repair forgetting at small scale? Vincent's 1.7B run found it worked only on mild damage and needed clipping plus averaging over several samples (experiment-weights-vs-context, as source-inkling-thinking-machines reports it).
  • R1's distilled models had no RL stage; the authors leave open how much RL on top would add (raw/arxiv-2501-12948-deepseek-r1-incentivizing-reasoning-capability-in.md, Appendix F).
