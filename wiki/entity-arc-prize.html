ARC Prize

entity created 2026-10-20 updated 2026-10-20 benchmarks arc-agi competitions chollet organisations

The ARC Prize Foundation runs the ARC-AGI benchmarks and a yearly open contest on Kaggle. Its team includes François Chollet, Mike Knoop, Gregory Kamradt and Bryan Landers, the authors of its 2025 report (raw/arxiv-2601-10904-arc-prize-2025-technical-report.md, title page). Chollet published the first ARC in 2019, with a definition of intelligence as skill-acquisition efficiency on new problems; it was later renamed ARC-AGI, and that set is now ARC-AGI-1 (same file, §1). This page covers Chollet too; the wiki has no separate page for him.

What the benchmark tests, and the debate around it, sit on concept-arc-agi. This page keeps one dated table of results, with the set and the checker named, because ARC scores are often quoted without either.

What it did

  • ARC Prize 2025. Ran 2025-03-26 to 2025-11-03; 1,455 teams, 15,154 entries; 90 papers against 47 in 2024. Paper awards: TRM first, SOAR second, CompressARC third (raw/arxiv-2601-10904-arc-prize-2025-technical-report.md, §2.1, §2.3, Table 2). The report names the year's theme the refinement loop (concept-program-synthesis, concept-test-time-training).
  • A warning about its own sets. The organisers judge ARC-AGI-1 and -2 now overfit through knowledge, "accidentally or intentionally". Their evidence is one trace, in which Gemini 3 Deep Think used ARC's colour names unprompted; they cannot size the effect (same file, §4.1, §4.2).
  • ARC-AGI-3 and ARC Prize 2026. Launched 2026-03-25: hundreds of turn-based environments with no instructions, rules or stated goals; over $2 million in prizes, split between an ARC-AGI-3 Kaggle contest for agents and a guaranteed ARC-AGI-2 grand prize for the best open-source solution (raw/web-2026-arcprize-arc-agi-3-launch.md, opening).

Caveats

  • The 2025 report grades the organisers' own contest; its results come from tables, not reruns (source-arc-prize-2025-report).
  • The ARC-AGI-3 headline changed from 0.26% to 0.51% between 2026-03-26 and 2026-04-20 with no note on the page (raw/web-2026-arcprize-arc-agi-3-launch.md, header).
  • Live scores move; the leaderboard is at https://arcprize.org/leaderboard (not in raw).
