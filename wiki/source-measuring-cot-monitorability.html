Measuring Chain-of-Thought Monitorability

source created 2026-04-13 updated 2026-09-26 interpretability chain-of-thought faithfulness monitorability 2025

arXiv 2510.27378, October 2025

Proposes a composite monitorability score combining faithfulness (does the CoT reflect the true internal computation?) and verbosity (does the CoT externalize enough to be monitored?). Finds that models can be faithful yet unmonitorable when they silently drop key reasoning factors. Monitorability varies sharply by family; DeepSeek-R1 tops the leaderboard at 78.3%. Complementary work: OpenAI's "Monitoring Monitorability" (Guan et al. 2025) and Korbak et al.'s "CoT Monitorability: A New and Fragile Opportunity" (arXiv 2507.11473).

Neural↔symbolic bridge

Directly tests whether the CoT is the symbolic substrate of reasoning. If CoT monitorability holds, CoT text is a valid readout of the underlying algorithm — and the harness wrapping a reasoning model is a real observation window into its symbolic work. If not, the CoT is a fiction the model writes on the side while computing something else. Directly relevant to the harness-as-symbolic-substrate thesis.
