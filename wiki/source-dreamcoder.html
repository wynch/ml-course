DreamCoder

source created 2026-04-13 updated 2026-10-20 neural-symbolic program-synthesis library-learning

Ellis, Wong, Nye, Sable-Meyer, Cary, Morales, Hewitt, Solar-Lezama, Tenenbaum — 2020 (PLDI 2021) Full text: raw/arxiv-2006-08381-dreamcoder-growing-generalizable-interpretable-knowledge-with.md (v1, the only arXiv version, captured 2026-10-20). The arXiv text has unnumbered sections; "§2" below is its "Wake/Sleep Program Learning" section.

Solves problems by writing programs in a DSL it grows itself. A wake-sleep loop alternates between (arXiv 2006.08381, §2):

  • Wake: search for programs that solve the tasks, guided by the recognition model.
  • Sleep, abstraction: grow the library by refactoring solved programs (version spaces) and adding shared sub-programs.
  • Sleep, dreaming: train the recognition model on replayed solutions and on programs sampled from the library.

E-graph refactoring came later (babble; Stitch, arXiv 2211.16605).

  • Correction (partial), 2026-10-20: the full text says the abstraction step works by "manipulating the set of refactorings, combining ideas from version space algebras and equivalence graphs" ("Wake/Sleep Program Learning"). So e-graph ideas are already in DreamCoder; babble and Stitch built dedicated e-graph and corpus-guided refactoring later. The old wording "via e-graph–based refactoring" was closer than the batch-A16 fix allowed.

Given a handful of examples per task, it rediscovers the basics of functional programming, vector algebra, and physics (including Newton's laws and Coulomb's), and solves creative drawing/scene tasks. (Abstract; "Results")

Neural↔symbolic bridge

The library of lambda abstractions is the symbolic substrate — a growing typed DSL. The neural net is a learned amortized search policy over programs in that DSL. They talk via programs sampled in "sleep" that the network then learns to recognize. The closest existing analogue to Zhuge's NC "installable capabilities": every solved problem contributes a reusable symbolic routine.
