A Global Workspace in Language Models

source created 2026-07-06 updated 2026-07-06 interpretability introspection global-workspace self-explanation ai-alignment cot-monitorability consciousness anthropic

Org: Anthropic · Date: 2026-07-06 · Venue: Transformer Circuits thread Post: anthropic.com/research/global-workspace · Paper: transformer-circuits.pub/2026/workspace

Summary

Anthropic finds a privileged internal "workspace" in Claude — a small set of activation patterns they call the J-space — that behaves, functionally, like the global broadcast channel from neuroscience's Global Workspace Theory (concept-active-inference's cousin from consciousness science). The J-space emerged during training rather than being designed in. They read it with the Jacobian Lens (J-Lens): for each vocabulary word, the internal activity pattern that raises Claude's probability of emitting that word at future timesteps. This lets them "read thoughts" ahead of the output.

The claim is deliberately bounded: evidence for access consciousness (functional reportability + reasoning), explicitly not phenomenal consciousness (subjective experience).

Key claims

  • A broadcast hub exists. J-space patterns have ~100× more components reading from and writing to them than ordinary patterns — the connectivity signature of a shared workspace, not a local feature.
  • Five workspace properties hold: reportability (Claude can describe its J-space), modulation (can activate patterns on request, e.g. "think about citrus"), causal mediation (swapping J-space contents changes outputs), flexible use (one representation feeds many tasks), and selectivity (drives higher-order reasoning but not automatic processing like grammar/fluency).
  • Invisible intermediate reasoning is legible. On "the animal that spins webs has ___ legs," spider appears in the J-space though it is never written — a latent reasoning step, caught directly. Parallels the multi-hop and planning circuits in source-lindsey-2025-biology-of-llm.
  • Causal interventions transfer. Editing "France"→"China" in the J-space consistently redirects downstream answers about capital, language, and currency across prompts; "Soccer"→"Rugby" changes what Claude reports thinking about.
  • Selective ablation. Deleting J-space content removes multi-step reasoning while leaving fluency, sentiment classification, and fact retrieval intact.
  • Post-training shapes it. The J-space exists in the pretrained model but acquires "point-of-view" signatures during post-training: self-monitoring ("fictional" on roleplay), warnings on dangerous content.
  • Counterfactual Reflection Training. Teaching the model what it would say if asked to reflect — with no behavioral training — reduced dishonest outputs and raised "honest"/"integrity" activity in the J-space.

Why it matters (safety)

Direct monitoring value demonstrated: catching evaluation awareness ("fake"/"fictional" firing before a response), fabrication ("manipulation" active while falsifying scores), and latent misaligned goals in corrupted model organisms. This is a mechanistic complement to behavioral CoT monitoring — see source-measuring-cot-monitorability.
