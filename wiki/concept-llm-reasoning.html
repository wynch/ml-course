LLM Reasoning

concept created 2026-04-05 updated 2026-09-26 reasoning chain-of-thought thinking evaluation

The extent to which language models can perform genuine reasoning — logical deduction, planning, causal inference, mathematical proof — versus sophisticated pattern matching and interpolation.

The Central Debate

A recurring theme across the wiki: are LLMs actually reasoning?

Skeptical Evidence

  • source-llm-reasoning-skeptics — Hierarchical reasoning models fail on trivial puzzles (one unknown cell), suggesting guessing rather than reasoning. Grokking dynamics reveal sudden phase transitions.
  • source-llm-reasoning-skeptics — Position paper arguing LLMs compress rather than invent, using Einstein's happiest thought as case study.
  • source-llm-reasoning-skeptics — Comprehensive taxonomy: architectural limitations, application-specific constraints, and robustness inconsistencies.
  • source-what-llms-think-unprompted — Without explicit prompts, models default to training data patterns, not reasoning.
  • source-zhang-harness-blog — Transformers are "at best, unreliable at compositional generalization": a plain Transformer's train gains did not carry to longer or cross-domain tasks, while the same model in an RLM harness did (raw/web-2026-alexzhang-harness-compositional.md, §"Better scaling requires compositional generalization"; §"RLMs can train on short tasks…"; §"RLMs can generalize to unseen tasks…").

Positive Evidence

  • source-chain-of-thought-foundations — Wei 2022: few-shot CoT prompting; GSM8K 17.9% → 56.9%; emergent with scale. The paper that opened the test-time-reasoning research thread.
  • source-chain-of-thought-foundations — Wang 2022: sample N CoTs + majority vote; GSM8K → 74.4%. Sampling-as-amplification ancestor of source-pdr-rethinking-thinking-tokens.
  • source-tree-of-thoughts — Yao 2023: deliberate exploration over thoughts; Game of 24 4% → 74%.
  • source-reflexion-verbal-rl — Shinn 2023: verbal RL; 91% pass@1 HumanEval (vs GPT-4's 80%).
  • source-self-refine — Madaan 2023: iterative self-feedback; +20% across 7 tasks without retraining.
  • source-symbolica-arc-agi-2 — Neurosymbolic approach achieves 85.28% on ARC-AGI-2 using LLM + program synthesis.
  • source-pdr-rethinking-thinking-tokens — Structured inference (Parallel-Distill-Refine) yields +11% on AIME math competitions.
  • source-deepseek-r1 — DeepSeek 2025: pure RL post-training elicits o1-style reasoning. R1-Zero variant skips SFT entirely.

The emergence debate

  • source-emergence-debate — Wei 2022: emergent abilities defined as those not predictable from smaller-scale behaviour. Used to argue some capabilities only "switch on" at scale.
  • source-emergence-debate — Schaeffer 2023 (NeurIPS): >92% of "emergent" abilities are metric artefacts (Multiple Choice Grade, Exact String Match). Linear/continuous metrics show smooth scaling. The most important counter-evidence to the emergence claim.

Formal Approaches

  • source-lambda-rlm-calculus-llm — Applying lambda calculus to LLM reasoning.
  • source-subleq-transformer-computer — Transformers can be constructed as Turing-complete computers.

Causal Reasoning (formerly its own concept page)

The ability to understand cause-and-effect relationships, make predictions, and reason about counterfactuals — a specific sub-capability where current models reliably fall short.

  • source-clevrer-video-reasoning — benchmark showing models excel at perception but fail at causal reasoning (explanatory, predictive, counterfactual questions).

Improving Reasoning

Several approaches to enhancing LLM reasoning:

  • Test-time compute — see concept-test-time-compute
  • Scaffolding — see source-language-models-as-scaffolds
  • Self-improvement — see concept-self-improvement
  • Recursive decomposition — see source-recursive-language-models; trained with RL in source-zhang-harness-blog
  • Latent reasoning — overview of the whole line: concept-latent-reasoning
  • Continuous thoughts taught by written chains — see source-coconut-continuous-latent-reasoning: on ProsQA the probe-read values after each thought look like breadth-first search rather than a greedy path; but a curriculum with no thoughts at all reaches 95.5% there against Coconut's 97.0% (raw/arxiv-2412-06769-coconut-reason-in-continuous-latent-space.md, §4.3, Table 1)
  • Limits of compressed latent chains — see source-continuous-cot-two-regimes: methods near written CoT on 25-token maths chains fall to about 35% of it on 350-token MATH chains, mostly through dropped signs and numbers (raw/arxiv-2607-16972-continuous-cot-tale-of-two-regimes.md, §4.4-4.5)
  • Latent thoughts learned from answers only — see source-abstract-token-curriculum: a difficulty curriculum that appends the model's own hidden state as a thought token solves graph reachability of up to 16 hops (99.9%) where no thoughts (48.3%) and written shortest-path CoT (63.5%) fail (raw/arxiv-2609-19717-learn-your-own-thoughts-abstract-token-curriculum.md, §6.2.2, Table 1)

Open Questions

  • Is there a hard ceiling on reasoning from next-token prediction?
  • Can neurosymbolic approaches (like Symbolica) scale to general reasoning?
  • Does structured inference (PDR, scaffolding) unlock latent reasoning or just create the illusion?
    • One small-scale datum: on graph reachability neither a curriculum alone (49.0%) nor thought slots alone (47.8%) help; only both together do (raw/arxiv-2609-19717-learn-your-own-thoughts-abstract-token-curriculum.md, §6.2.2). The structure is needed, but it is learned, not written.
