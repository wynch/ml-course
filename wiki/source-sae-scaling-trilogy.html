SAE Scaling Trilogy

source created 2026-05-07 updated 2026-05-07 interpretability sae sparse-autoencoders scale hierarchy transcoders gemma anthropic deepmind features 2024 2025

Three milestones from 2024–2025 that took sparse autoencoders from "toy mech-interp tool" to "infrastructure you can run on a deployed frontier model in an afternoon": Anthropic proves dictionaries scale to Claude 3 Sonnet, the matryoshka variant fixes feature absorption and gives an explicit hierarchy, and DeepMind ships the full-stack SAE+transcoder suite for every Gemma-3 size. Read as a unit, they describe what SAEs look like when they grow up.

Scaling Monosemanticity — Templeton, Conerly, Marcus, ..., Olah 2024

Anthropic, May 2024.

Scales SAE dictionary learning from toy transformers to a production-scale model (Claude 3 Sonnet). Finds millions of features ranging from concrete ("Golden Gate Bridge," specific code variable names) to highly abstract ("inner conflict," "code bugs," "gender bias," "sycophancy"). Demonstrates features are causally meaningful — clamping the Golden Gate Bridge feature makes Claude believe it is the bridge (the "Golden Gate Claude" demo) — and that abstract multilingual / multimodal features emerge at scale.

Distinctive contribution: proves the dictionary-learning approach doesn't break at frontier scale; the symbolic readout gets richer, not noisier. Golden Gate Claude is the clearest causal evidence that an SAE feature behaves like a manipulable symbol — you can "write" to it from outside the model and watch behavior follow. The feature catalogs produced here are the nouns of LLM mech interp; later work (source-circuit-tracing-methods-anthropic) adds the verbs.

Matryoshka Sparse Autoencoders — Bussmann, Nabeshima, Karvonen, Nanda 2025

arXiv 2503.17547, March 2025.

Trains nested SAE dictionaries of increasing size so the small dictionary is forced to reconstruct inputs without help from the large one. The result is a hierarchy of features: inner rings carry general concepts, outer rings carry specializations.

Key findings

  • Dramatically reduces feature absorption (where one feature silently subsumes another) and feature splitting pathologies on Gemma-2-2B and TinyStories.
  • Small reconstruction tradeoff vs. TopK SAEs, but large wins on targeted concept erasure and sparse probing.

Distinctive contribution: gives a literal taxonomic hierarchy over learned features — the missing structural axis for treating SAE dictionaries as symbol tables with inheritance. Rather than flat bags of concepts, the matryoshka structure expresses "this feature is a kind of that feature," which is the first thing any ontology needs.

Gemma Scope 2 — Google DeepMind 2025

December 2025 release.

Open release of SAEs and transcoders on every layer of every Gemma-3 size (270M to 27B), both pretrained and instruction-tuned, trained with Matryoshka techniques (above). Over 1T parameters trained, 110PB of activation data stored. Shipped with explicit tooling for chain-of-thought faithfulness analysis, jailbreak/refusal inspection, and feature steering — accessible via Neuronpedia.

Distinctive contribution: the first full-stack, multi-scale transcoder suite on a modern instruction-tuned family. Lets the community run circuit tracing and feature-level edits on real deployed-scale models without training their own dictionaries. The existence of this infrastructure matters as much as any single finding: it turns symbolic feature manipulation into something a researcher can do in an afternoon.

Why this grouping matters

The trilogy traces the maturation arc of the SAE programme:

  1. Templeton 2024 — scale works. Frontier models contain millions of interpretable features; causal intervention behaves as predicted. The existence proof.
  2. Matryoshka 2025 — structure matters. Flat dictionaries hit pathologies (absorption, splitting); nesting fixes them and gives feature hierarchy for free. The structural correction.
  3. Gemma Scope 2 — infrastructure matters. Dictionaries and transcoders for every layer of every Gemma-3 size, openly available. The community-enablement step.

Together they answer the post-2023 question "does SAE-style dictionary learning generalise from one toy model to the field?" — yes, with the right scale, the right hierarchy structure, and the right tooling. They are the foundation downstream work like source-circuit-tracing-methods-anthropic and source-lindsey-2025-biology-of-llm stands on.

Neural↔symbolic bridge

This trilogy is where SAEs stop being a technique and become an operating system for reading and writing to LLM internals: feature catalogues you can browse, hierarchies you can navigate, transcoders you can swap in. The neural net stays neural; a symbol table sits beside it.
