SSL Decorrelation Methods — Barlow Twins + VICReg

source created 2026-05-07 updated 2026-05-07 self-supervised-learning non-contrastive vision representation-learning decorrelation information-theory foundational

Two FAIR papers, two months apart in 2021, that opened a third path to non-contrastive SSL: instead of the predictor + EMA asymmetry of source-ssl-contrastive-foundations (BYOL section), use explicit decorrelation regularisers to prevent collapse directly. Symmetric, no momentum, no stop-gradient — and the loss becomes a clean information-theoretic object.

Barlow Twins — Zbontar, Jing, Misra, LeCun, Deny (Mar 2021)

arXiv: 2103.03230.

A self-supervised loss that operates directly on the cross-correlation matrix of the two embeddings of a pair of augmented views, driven entirely by Barlow's redundancy-reduction principle from neuroscience.

Method: Two augmented views x_A, x_B passed through the same network. Compute the empirical cross-correlation matrix C between the (batch-normalised) embeddings — dimensions × dimensions. Push C toward identity:

L = Σ_i (1 − C_ii)² + λ · Σ_i Σ_{j≠i} C_ij²

  • Invariance term (diagonal): each embedding dimension agrees across views.
  • Redundancy reduction term (off-diagonal): decorrelates distinct dimensions across the batch, preventing collapse onto a low-rank subspace.

Distinctive contributions:

  • Single shared network — symmetric, no online/target asymmetry.
  • The redundancy reduction is over feature dimensions, not samples — orthogonal to contrastive losses (which decorrelate over samples).
  • Empirically, very large embedding dimensions (e.g. 8192) help — opposite of contrastive intuition.
  • Clean information-theoretic framing: under a Gaussian assumption, the loss maximises mutual information between views.

ImageNet linear-eval ≈ 73.2% (ResNet-50) — competitive with source-ssl-contrastive-foundations (BYOL section) but with a noticeably simpler training recipe.

VICReg — Bardes, Ponce, LeCun (May 2021)

arXiv: 2105.04906. Meta AI / Inria.

Non-contrastive SSL with three explicit, separately-tuned terms — variance (anti-collapse), invariance (view consistency), covariance (anti-redundancy) — that can be applied independently to each branch of a joint-embedding architecture.

Method: Two views x, x' encoded to embeddings z, z'. Loss:

L = λ · I(z, z') + μ · [V(z) + V(z')] + ν · [C(z) + C(z')]

  • Invariance I = ‖z − z'‖² — pull positive pairs together (mirrors Barlow's diagonal term).
  • Variance V = max(0, γ − std(z_d))² per dimension — hinge minimum standard deviation, prevents collapse to constant.
  • Covariance C = sum of squared off-diagonal entries of the embedding covariance — decorrelates dimensions (similar to Barlow's off-diagonal term).

ImageNet linear-eval ≈ 73% (ResNet-50), on par with the family.

Distinctive contributions:

  • The V/I/C decomposition is applied to each branch independently, so the two encoders don't have to be identical or shared.
  • Unlocks multi-modal and asymmetric joint-embedding setups (e.g. predicting one branch from a different modality) — a key design enabler for concept-jepa.
  • Cleanly separates the three failure modes a joint-embedding loss must address — making it the most "modular" recipe of the non-contrastive family.

Why this pair matters

  • Both papers showed anti-collapse can be made explicit rather than left to architectural asymmetry. This was a sanity check that the predictor + EMA of BYOL/DINO isn't strictly necessary.
  • VICReg's per-branch modularity is what made it a building block in subsequent work — VICReg-style anti-collapse regularisers are a common ingredient when training JEPA predictors (see source-ijepa-self-supervised-learning and source-vjepa-family).
  • The dimensional-decorrelation move is the conceptual move that made non-contrastive SSL a durable family rather than a curiosity.
