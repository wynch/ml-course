Predict in Latent Space — A Stealth Theme

thread created 2026-05-07 updated 2026-10-09 thread jepa world-models representation-learning self-supervised-learning active-inference cross-cutting

A bet that runs through more of the wiki than the table of contents reveals: don't predict pixels or tokens; predict a learned representation. It's the JEPA programme's headline claim, but the same idea recurs across SSL, world models, active inference, even some LLM-side architecture work. Walk the thread and the wiki's "predictive axis" sharpens.

The bet, stated cleanly

Given two views (or current state + future state, or text + image, or...), train a model to predict an encoded representation of one from the other. Critically:

  • No pixel-level / token-level reconstruction.
  • No contrastive negatives.
  • Anti-collapse comes from architectural asymmetry (predictor + EMA target) or explicit regularisation.

The pay-off: capacity goes to predictable structure, not to surface fidelity. The cost: the representation's structure is determined by the SSL objective, not by any downstream task.

Contradiction (partial): "capacity goes to predictable structure" is the intuition; a theorem that covers a random signal and nuisance together, source-infoldm-predictive-ssl-identifiability, finds that predictive objectives keep the predictable signal but are not forced to drop nuisance unless the latent is no bigger than the signal (raw/arxiv-2609-37789-predictive-ssl-identifies-stochastic-signals-under-nuisance.md, §3.2, §5). It covers objectives with an entropy term (SimCLR, VICReg, InfoNCE) and never names JEPA or the EMA-target setups listed here (same file, §2, §3).

Where it shows up

1. SSL vision — the canonical instances

  • source-ssl-contrastive-foundations BYOL section: the founding non-contrastive instance. Predict an EMA-target encoder's embedding. No negatives. No pixels.
  • source-dino-lineage: self-distillation on ViTs. Predict the teacher's distribution over multi-crop views. DINOv2 scaled to foundation-model-grade; DINOv3 added relational (Gram-matrix) prediction.
  • source-masked-image-modeling iBOT half: predict an EMA-teacher's embedding of masked patches. The hybrid MIM+self-distillation that became DINOv2's recipe.

2. The JEPA programme — explicit naming of the bet

  • source-lecun-path-autonomous-intelligence: 2022 position paper that names this the central architectural move. "Stop reconstructing input space."
  • source-ijepa-self-supervised-learning: image-JEPA proof of concept. Context block → predict target-block embeddings.
  • source-vjepa-family: video-JEPA. V-JEPA 2 + action conditioning becomes a world model: predict next latent given current latent and action.
  • source-vl-jepa: vision-language JEPA. Predict text embeddings, not tokens. 50% fewer trainable params; beats CLIP/SigLIP2 on 16 video benchmarks.

3. World models — the same idea, RL-flavoured

  • source-planet-latent-dynamics (Hafner 2018) and source-dreamer-v1: RSSM predicts in a learned latent. Not pixel rollouts, latent rollouts. The Dreamer family is built on this.
  • source-tdmpc2: reward-shaped (non-reconstructive) latent. Predict only what matters for control.
  • source-dreamerv3 (Nature 2025): single config across 150+ tasks via latent dynamics. Capstone of the predictive-world-models branch.

4. Active inference — the same idea, hand-specified

  • source-active-inference-foundations (Friston 2017 + Parr/Pezzulo/Friston 2022): predict-then-plan over a generative model. When the generative model is hand-specified, the latent is symbolic by construction.
  • source-schema-based-active-inference, source-why-learn-if-you-can-infer-active-inference — apply this to robotics, beat RL on Habitat.

source-dreamerv3 vs VERSES is the same bet at opposite ends of the learned-vs-specified spectrum.

5. Mech interp — the SAE feature space as a learned latent

  • source-sae-monosemanticity-2023, source-sae-scaling-trilogy: SAE features are exactly a learned latent space the model didn't explicitly train for. Subsequent work (circuit tracing, ROME/MEMIT, TensorLens) operates in that latent, not in pixel/token space.

A subtle case but a real one: post-hoc latent prediction is what mech interp does.

  • source-anthropic-global-workspace: the J-Lens defines an internal state by how it changes future-token probability — an interpretability probe built on the same predict-in-latent logic. The read-out target is a vocabulary-indexed "thought" (the J-space), not a token. See concept-global-workspace.

6. Diffusion — the latent target

  • source-flow-map-language-models: one-step continuous denoising in embedding space. Predicts the embedding flow, not the discrete tokens.
  • source-unified-latents-diffusion (DeepMind): joint encoder + diffusion prior + diffusion decoder all in latent space.

The diffusion side of the wiki has independently converged on "predict in latent" — not because it read LeCun, but because it works.

7. LLMs sideways — the latent that isn't pixels

  • source-engram-deepseek: explicit addressable N-gram embedding table augments the backbone. The lookup target is an embedding, not a token.
  • source-memoryllm-plug-n-play-ffn: FFN-as-retrieval-memory — same shape, different language.
  • source-codelewm: the code-domain instance — JEPA over source-code edits (state-action-state), with the sharp finding that the bet only pays when the latent substrate carries deterministic execution behavior, not textual metadata. A stress-test of what to predict in latent space.
  • source-next-latent-prediction (NextLat): the purest LLM-side instance yet. A pure-text transformer is trained with an auxiliary objective to predict its own next latent state given the next token, on top of next-token prediction. The latents provably trend toward belief states (sufficient statistics of history) — i.e. the model carries a compact world model. Gains in reasoning/planning/world-modeling plus up to 3.3× faster self-speculative decoding. This is the closest the wiki has to the "pure-text JEPA" question raised below.

8. Theory — why it finds the signal

  • source-infoldm-predictive-ssl-identifiability (Mikulasch & Zenke, 2026): the predictive objective is mutual information plus distribution matching. The first keeps what is predictable; the second makes it identifiable, so a Gaussian predictor recovers the true signal up to an affine map (raw/arxiv-2609-37789-predictive-ssl-identifies-stochastic-signals-under-nuisance.md, §3, Corollary 1). Tested on synthetic sequences, Causal3DIdent image pairs and a MuJoCo hopper with random nuisance; with a pixel decoder loss added, the hopper's state can no longer be read from the latent (same file, §4).

What the through-line implies

  1. The same anti-collapse machinery shows up. Predictor + EMA target appears in BYOL, DINO, I-JEPA, V-JEPA. See analysis-self-distillation-as-universal-lever for the deeper observation that self-distillation is the universal lever that makes predict-in-latent work.
  2. The representation is the bet. The latent's structure is what determines downstream usefulness. DINOv3's Gram anchoring (preserve patch-similarity structure) and V-JEPA 2.1's Dense Predictive Loss (loss on every token) are different bets about what to preserve in the latent.
  3. Generative is the foil. The wiki's "generative world model" branch (source-genie-2-foundation-world-model) and pixel-MIM half of source-masked-image-modeling (MAE) are the explicit counter-positions. The contrast is productive: when does rendering pay off, and when does predicting in latent pay off?

Where the thread might be heading

  • Audio-JEPA at scale. No public scaled audio JEPA yet (only source-wav2vec-2-era contrastive audio SSL). Probably 2026/2027.
  • Pure-text JEPA. source-vl-jepa uses text-embedding targets for VLM outputs. Whether a pure language JEPA — predict masked text embeddings from context — can match autoregressive LMs is the most interesting open question on this axis. source-next-latent-prediction is an early data point: rather than replacing next-token prediction, it augments it with next-latent prediction, getting belief-state latents while keeping the autoregressive backbone — and reports downstream gains, suggesting the predict-in-latent bet pays off on text even as an auxiliary.
  • JEPA + diffusion fusion. "Denoising with a JEPA" (arXiv 2410.03755) is one early thread; the wiki doesn't have it yet.
  • Hierarchical JEPA at scale. LeCun's H-JEPA (multi-time-scale prediction) is in the 2022 position paper. No scaled artefact yet.
