Latent Reasoning

concept created 2026-09-26 updated 2026-10-03 latent-reasoning continuous-thought test-time-compute chain-of-thought recurrence reasoning

Definition. A model reasons latently when it spends extra computation before answering and that computation never becomes words. Chain-of-thought gives a model more steps by making it write; latent reasoning gives it more steps, or more room, without writing. The research line asks what to give the model to compute in, and what teaches it to use that room.

The pause paper states the split that runs through the table: a written token is fed back, so M tokens give about M × L sequential steps; M pauses only widen each layer (raw/arxiv-2310-02226-think-before-you-speak-pause-tokens.md, §6, Appendix I.2). Every other row in the table adds sequential steps.

What supervises them

Nothing ever grades a latent slot directly. The methods differ in what reaches it indirectly:

  1. Only the next real tokens. Pauses are skipped by the loss (raw/arxiv-2310-02226-think-before-you-speak-pause-tokens.md, §3.1). The looped model trains on plain next-token loss with a random number of loops (raw/arxiv-2502-05171-recurrent-depth-latent-reasoning.md, §3.3).
  2. Written chains, removed step by step. Coconut swaps written steps for thoughts one stage at a time; the loss falls on the words left after the thoughts (raw/arxiv-2412-06769-coconut-reason-in-continuous-latent-space.md, §3).
  3. A teacher or the words themselves. CODI distills from a teacher reading the written chain; C-MTP predicts the words each latent averages (raw/arxiv-2607-16972-continuous-cot-tale-of-two-regimes.md, §3.2, §3.4).
  4. The answer alone, with a curriculum. ATC raises difficulty in stages and grades only the answer (raw/arxiv-2609-19717-learn-your-own-thoughts-abstract-token-curriculum.md, §2).
  5. The answer alone, by reward. Soft tokens use reinforcement learning, with noise on the embeddings as the exploration (raw/arxiv-2509-19170-soft-tokens-hard-truths.md, §3).

The recurring finding: the room alone does not help; something must teach its use.

  • Pauses help only if pretraining also had pauses; added at finetuning alone the gains are smaller and mixed (raw/arxiv-2310-02226-think-before-you-speak-pause-tokens.md, §4.3).
  • Coconut without its curriculum does no better than no chain at all (raw/arxiv-2412-06769-coconut-reason-in-continuous-latent-space.md, §5.3).
  • On shortcut-free graph reachability, thought slots alone score 47.8% and a curriculum alone 49.0% (chance 50%); both together, 99.9% (raw/arxiv-2609-19717-learn-your-own-thoughts-abstract-token-curriculum.md, §6.2.2).
  • The looped model is the case where plain next-token loss, with a random loop count in pretraining, is enough to teach the loop; at one loop it scores 0 on GSM8k CoT, so the gains live in the loop (raw/arxiv-2502-05171-recurrent-depth-latent-reasoning.md, Table 4, §3.3, §5.3).

Contradiction (partial), 2026-10-03: source-varunneal-diffusion-will-be-everywhere argues that looped models give "a compute knob over the range you trained on, not beyond it", citing Parcae's gains saturating near the mean training loop count (raw/web-2026-varunneal-diffusion-everywhere.md, "Test-time scaling"). The "Depth scales at test time" row above stays within that range: the looped model trained with loop counts around a mean of 32 (raw/arxiv-2502-05171-recurrent-depth-latent-reasoning.md, §3.3, §4.1). The essay reports no runs of its own.

Two cautions. First, on ProsQA a curriculum with no thoughts reaches 95.5% (raw/arxiv-2412-06769-coconut-reason-in-continuous-latent-space.md, Table 1), so search tasks can hide shortcuts; ATC built its benchmark to remove them (raw/arxiv-2609-19717-learn-your-own-thoughts-abstract-token-curriculum.md, §6.1). Second, the strongest results use small models (GPT-2 for Coconut, a two-layer model trained from scratch for ATC) on synthetic tasks; at 1-1.5B on realistic MATH chains, compressing methods collapse (raw/arxiv-2607-16972-continuous-cot-tale-of-two-regimes.md, Table 5).

How it changed

  • 2023: blank tokens to compute in; the gain needs pretraining (source-pause-tokens).
  • 2024: the hidden state itself becomes the token, taught by written chains (source-coconut-continuous-latent-reasoning).
  • 2025: depth instead of tokens, pretrained at scale (source-recurrent-depth-latent-reasoning); reward instead of written chains (source-soft-tokens-hard-truths); internal ticks (source-continuous-thought-machine).
  • 2026: attractors as the account of why more loops help (source-equilibrium-reasoners); answer-only training by difficulty curriculum (source-abstract-token-curriculum); a reality check on long traces (source-continuous-cot-two-regimes); the steps of a loop read as weak-to-strong guesses and used at decoding (source-loopcd-decoding-looped-transformers).

What is still open

  • Long, realistic reasoning. No method here shows a compressed continuous chain near written CoT on chains of hundreds of tokens; soft tokens get close but do not compress (raw/arxiv-2509-19170-soft-tokens-hard-truths.md, Appendix E; raw/arxiv-2607-16972-continuous-cot-tale-of-two-regimes.md, Table 5). ATC names long proofs as open (raw/arxiv-2609-19717-learn-your-own-thoughts-abstract-token-curriculum.md, §8).
  • Cost. Chained thoughts train with sequential passes; ATC and the looped model cap memory by backpropagating through the last few steps only (raw/arxiv-2412-06769-coconut-reason-in-continuous-latent-space.md, §3; raw/arxiv-2609-19717-learn-your-own-thoughts-abstract-token-curriculum.md, §6.3; raw/arxiv-2502-05171-recurrent-depth-latent-reasoning.md, §3.3). source-topological-trouble-transformers argues recurrence built into the architecture is the better route.
  • Oversight. Latent steps leave no text. Coconut reads them by decoding them to words, the looped model by plotting trajectories, ATC by replacing thoughts with noise (raw/arxiv-2412-06769-coconut-reason-in-continuous-latent-space.md, §4.3; raw/arxiv-2502-05171-recurrent-depth-latent-reasoning.md, §7; raw/arxiv-2609-19717-learn-your-own-thoughts-abstract-token-curriculum.md, §6.4). Each completed loop of a looped model can be read as a next-token guess through the model's own output layers; guesses read from layers inside a loop drift far from the final one and make poor references (raw/arxiv-2610-02185-decoding-looped-transformers-better-for-almost.md, §1, Appendix E.3). That makes the loop partly readable; the paper uses it for decoding, not oversight. See source-measuring-cot-monitorability.
  • Is the search real? The breadth-first reading of Coconut rests on probes; the ablation without thoughts nearly matches it on ProsQA.
  • Deployment. Soft-trained models do best when they think in normal words at test time (raw/arxiv-2509-19170-soft-tokens-hard-truths.md, §4), which leaves open whether the gain is latent reasoning or better training.
