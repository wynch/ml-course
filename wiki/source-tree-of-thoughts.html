Tree of Thoughts: Deliberate Problem Solving with Large Language Models

source created 2026-05-06 updated 2026-05-06 reasoning chain-of-thought search test-time-compute scaffolding 2023 neurips

Yao, Yu, Zhao, Shafran, Griffiths, Cao, Narasimhan — arXiv 2305.10601, May 2023 (NeurIPS 2023)

Generalises source-chain-of-thought-foundations from a single linear chain to a tree of intermediate "thoughts," where the model can self-evaluate partial solutions and choose which branches to expand using BFS or DFS. The reframing is small but consequential: reasoning becomes deliberate search rather than left-to-right sampling, and the tree itself is the symbolic substrate the LLM operates on.

Method

Four design choices instantiate the framework:

  1. Thought decomposition — what counts as one "thought" (a line in Game of 24, a sentence-plan paragraph in creative writing, a single word in crosswords).
  2. Thought generator — propose multiple next thoughts from a state, either by sampling i.i.d. or by prompting for a list.
  3. State evaluator — the model rates partial states (e.g. "sure / maybe / impossible") or votes across candidates, providing a heuristic.
  4. Search algorithm — BFS for breadth-limited Game of 24 / Crosswords; DFS with backtracking when a branch is judged impossible.

No fine-tuning, no external solver — the LLM plays generator, evaluator, and controller.

Key numbers

  • Game of 24: GPT-4 with standard CoT prompting scores 4%; ToT (BFS, b=5) reaches 74% — an ~18× improvement and the headline result.
  • Creative writing (5-paragraph passages constrained to end with given sentences): ToT preferred over CoT by GPT-4 evaluator and humans.
  • Mini Crosswords: ToT word-level success rises from ~16% (CoT) to ~60%.

Why it matters

ToT is one of the cleanest demonstrations that structure around the model can outperform raw scaling — a key claim of the source-language-models-as-scaffolds thesis and the broader concept-harness-as-symbolic-substrate story. The tree is the symbol; the LLM is the operator. Direct descendants in the wiki:

  • source-chain-of-thought-foundations — same idea but flat: sample-and-vote without explicit tree structure.
  • source-rap-reasoning-planning — LLM-as-world-model + MCTS, replacing BFS/DFS with more principled rollout.
  • source-lats-tree-search-agents — MCTS over tool-using agents; ToT's natural extension to interactive tasks.
  • source-pdr-rethinking-thinking-tokens — Parallel-Distill-Refine: a different topology over thoughts (parallel sampling + distillation, not tree search).

ToT also raises the concept-test-time-compute / parameter-count trade-off that source-snell-test-time-compute-scaling later formalises: in Game of 24, a much larger model running standard CoT loses badly to a smaller model with a tree-structured budget. The paper became a foundational reference for the concept-llm-reasoning camp arguing that deliberation — not just bigger weights — drives capability.

Cousin scaffolds in the wiki: source-reflexion-verbal-rl (verbal RL over episodes) and source-self-refine (single-trajectory self-feedback) — together with ToT they form the canonical 2023 scaffolding triad.
