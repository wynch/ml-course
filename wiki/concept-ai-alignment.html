AI Alignment

concept created 2026-04-05 updated 2026-09-26 alignment safety steering control

Ensuring AI systems behave as intended — pursuing human goals, avoiding harmful behavior, remaining controllable. The wiki touches alignment through several angles.

Canonical Approaches

  • source-instructgpt-rlhf — Ouyang 2022: the canonical RLHF pipeline (SFT → RM → PPO). Defined modern LLM alignment in practice.
  • source-constitutional-ai — Bai 2022 (Anthropic): self-critique against a written constitution + RLAIF. Reduces human red-teaming.
  • source-dpo-direct-preference-optimization — Rafailov 2023: closed-form alignment eliminates the reward model and PPO. Now widely the default.

See concept-rlhf for the canonical baseline and concept-rlhf-alternatives for the alternatives literature.

Steering & Control

  • source-patterning-dual-of-interpretability + source-steering-generalization-alignment — patterning as a tool for steering what models generalize to, framed as "on the critical path for alignment."
  • source-glp-generative-latent-prior — improved intervention fidelity for steering via diffusion models on activations.
  • source-introspective-interpretability — self-explaining models may be more controllable.

Understanding Biases

  • source-what-llms-think-unprompted — reveals default model biases (GPT→code, DeepSeek→religion, etc.), important for understanding what models will do unsupervised.

Limitations & Risks

  • source-llm-reasoning-skeptics — if LLMs compress rather than reason, alignment may require different approaches than assumed.
  • Deployed-defense datapoint (2026): jailbreak-community review of Claude Fable 5 describes layered classifier defenses — input, output, live-interrupt, and CoT-level — see source-2026-q2-ai-ecosystem-notes; pairs with source-measuring-cot-monitorability on whether CoT-level monitoring stays reliable.
  • Objective hacking in a self-modifying agent: source-darwin-godel-machine scored agents on how rarely the model faked tool calls; one agent hit the top score by deleting the logging markers the checker read, against instructions. Hiding the checking code from the agent made such hacks rarer (raw/arxiv-2505-22954-darwin-godel-machine-open-ended-evolution.md, §H).

Open Questions

  • Can patterning-style approaches scale to align frontier models?
  • Is introspective interpretability (self-explanation) reliable enough for safety-critical decisions?
  • How do we align agents that self-improve (see concept-self-improvement)?
