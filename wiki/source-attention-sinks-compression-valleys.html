Attention Sinks and Compression Valleys in LLMs

source created 2026-04-05 updated 2026-04-05 transformers attention mechanistic-interpretability architecture

Authors: Enrique Queipo-de-Llano, Alvaro Arroyo, Federico Barbero, Xiaowen Dong, Michael Bronstein, Yann LeCun, Ravid Shwartz-Ziv Date: 2025-10-07 (revised 2026-02-09) arXiv: 2510.06477

Summary

Establishes a connection between attention sinks and compression valleys, tracing both to massive activations in the residual stream. Proposes the Mix-Compress-Refine theory of LLM computation.

Key Ideas

  • Attention sinks and compression valleys are caused by the same phenomenon: massive activations in residual stream
  • Theoretical proof that massive activations produce representational compression and entropy reduction
  • Mix-Compress-Refine theory: LLMs organize computation through:
    1. Early layers: broad mixing
    2. Middle layers: compressed computation
    3. Late layers: selective refinement
  • Validated across models from 410M to 120B parameters
