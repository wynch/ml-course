Major Themes

1. The Reasoning Question

Skeptical: source-llm-reasoning-skeptics (HRM + failures-survey + LLMs-can't-jump), source-emergence-debate (Schaeffer half: >92% of claimed emergent abilities use one of two discontinuous metrics; re-scored continuously, the tested ones scale smoothly). Positive: source-chain-of-thought-foundations (CoT + Self-Consistency), source-tree-of-thoughts, source-reflexion-verbal-rl, source-self-refine, source-symbolica-arc-agi-2 (85.28% on the ARC-AGI-2 public evaluation set, Opus 4.6 inside Agentica, $6.94/task, 2026-02-10; self-reported, not verified). The frontier: source-arc-agi-3 (under 1% for frontier models at launch, 2026-03-25: 0.26%; Symbolica reports an unverified 36.08% on the public set), source-deepseek-r1 (pure RL elicits reasoning), source-openai-o1-system-card (hidden-CoT reasoning), source-distribution-sharpening-power-sampling (RL gains as inference-time amplification; beats one-shot GRPO out of domain, trails it on MATH500; contested by arXiv 2608.14420). The pass@k debate: source-rl-reasoning-boundary-yue (RL models beat their base at pass@1 and lose at large k) against source-prorl (long RL reaches problems the base never solves); hub concept-rlvr. ARC's dated record: source-arc-prize-2025-report and entity-arc-prize; a 7M-parameter recursive net, source-trm-tiny-recursive-model, scores 44.6% on ARC-AGI-1. Olympiad maths: source-gemini-deep-think-imo-2025. Latent thoughts (hub: concept-latent-reasoning): source-pause-tokens (blank tokens to compute in, 2023), source-coconut-continuous-latent-reasoning (hidden states as thoughts, 2024), source-recurrent-depth-latent-reasoning (a looped block, 2025), source-soft-tokens-hard-truths (continuous chains by RL, 2025), source-continuous-cot-two-regimes (compressed chains fail on realistic traces, 2026), source-abstract-token-curriculum (continuous thoughts learned from answer supervision alone, small synthetic tasks), source-loopcd-decoding-looped-transformers (the first loop's guess steers the last at decoding; half the loops match full depth on multiple choice, 2026), source-ouro-looped-lms (looped models pretrained at 7.7T tokens, 2025). A rival test-time axis, argued in an essay rather than measured: denoising steps in text diffusion (source-varunneal-diffusion-will-be-everywhere).

See concept-llm-reasoning, concept-arc-agi, concept-test-time-compute.

2. Beyond Pure Scaling — and the Emergence Debate

Foundational: source-kaplan-scaling-laws -> source-chinchilla-compute-optimal (the N:D ~ 1:1 correction). Contradiction (2026-10-20): later open models train far past Chinchilla-optimal because inference cost dominates; a refit of approach 3 disagrees with the paper's own estimates (arXiv 2404.10102, not in raw); source-reconciling-kaplan-chinchilla traces most of the Kaplan–Chinchilla gap to Kaplan counting only non-embedding parameters at small scale. Open Questions & Gaps

  1. Opened by ingests:
    • Generative vs predictive world models — source-genie-2-foundation-world-model vs source-vjepa-family (V-JEPA 2-AC): which scales better as a substrate? Now also source-genie-3 against source-dreamer-4 and source-leworldmodel.
    • VLA vs world-model planning — direct policy (source-rt-2, source-pi-zero) vs latent MPC (source-vjepa-family V-JEPA 2-AC) vs symbolic code (source-code-as-policies)?
    • Pure RL vs SFT-first — source-deepseek-r1 R1-Zero shows base+RL alone produces frontier reasoning. Where does SFT remain necessary? Superseded (in part), 2026-10-20: the released R1 kept a cold-start SFT stage (source-deepseek-r1); the question has moved to whether RL adds ability at all (source-rl-reasoning-boundary-yue against source-prorl; concept-rlvr).
    • Emergence is real or artefact — Wei vs Schaeffer: the most consequential ongoing scaling debate. Du et al. (arXiv 2403.15796) find a loss threshold even under continuous metrics; not ingested (source-emergence-debate).
    • MoE × MoD × Engram — multiple sparsity axes interact unpredictably.
    • Does looping scale past its training range? (2026-10-03) — source-varunneal-diffusion-will-be-everywhere says no and argues a loop that settles to a fixed point gains nothing more; source-loopcd-decoding-looped-transformers finds Huginn settled by step 16 of 32; source-equilibrium-reasoners ties its gains to settling into attractors. No source here clearly tests loops beyond their trained range. Partial answer, 2026-10-20: source-ouro-looped-lms trains at four loops, and its scores peak at four and fall at five to eight. Named in raw by title only: iso-depth scaling laws for looped LMs (arXiv 2604.21106) and training-free looped transformers (arXiv 2605.23872).
    • Is any decision model calibrated? (2026-10-03) — Jev's confidences are sold as calibrated; neither source-willison-jev-decision-models nor source-raschka-text-classification-to-jev checks. See concept-calibration.
  2. Still uncovered/thin (2026-07 review):
    • Diffusion image/video generation (only FMLM + UL); world-model side covered better than generation side.
    • Audio generation (TTS, music).
    • Neuromorphic / spiking nets. Candidate: compute-optimal scaling laws for spiking language models (arXiv 2610.15146, Saunders et al.; abs page checked 2026-10-20; not ingested).
    • RL foundations — now more pressing: entity-richard-sutton entered the wiki, R1/GRPO and RLVR-style training recur everywhere, but there is no PPO/GRPO/TD-learning foundational source.
