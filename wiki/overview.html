Major Themes

1. The Reasoning Question

Skeptical: source-llm-reasoning-skeptics (HRM + failures-survey + LLMs-can't-jump), source-emergence-debate (Schaeffer half: >92% of "emergent" abilities are metric artefacts). Positive: source-chain-of-thought-foundations (CoT + Self-Consistency), source-tree-of-thoughts, source-reflexion-verbal-rl, source-self-refine, source-symbolica-arc-agi-2 (85.28% on ARC-AGI-2). The frontier: source-arc-agi-3 (<1% on interactive), source-deepseek-r1 (pure RL elicits reasoning), source-openai-o1-system-card (hidden-CoT reasoning), source-distribution-sharpening-power-sampling (RL gains as inference-time amplification). Latent thoughts (hub: concept-latent-reasoning): source-pause-tokens (blank tokens to compute in, 2023), source-coconut-continuous-latent-reasoning (hidden states as thoughts, 2024), source-recurrent-depth-latent-reasoning (a looped block, 2025), source-soft-tokens-hard-truths (continuous chains by RL, 2025), source-continuous-cot-two-regimes (compressed chains fail on realistic traces, 2026), source-abstract-token-curriculum (continuous thoughts learned from answer supervision alone, small synthetic tasks), source-loopcd-decoding-looped-transformers (the first loop's guess steers the last at decoding; half the loops match full depth on multiple choice, 2026). A rival test-time axis, argued in an essay rather than measured: denoising steps in text diffusion (source-varunneal-diffusion-will-be-everywhere).

See concept-llm-reasoning, concept-arc-agi, concept-test-time-compute.

Open Questions & Gaps

  1. Newly opened by Phase 2B/3/4:
    • Generative vs predictive world models — source-genie-2-foundation-world-model vs source-vjepa-family (V-JEPA 2-AC): which scales better as a substrate?
    • VLA vs world-model planning — direct policy (source-rt-2, source-pi-zero) vs latent MPC (source-vjepa-family V-JEPA 2-AC) vs symbolic code (source-code-as-policies)?
    • Pure RL vs SFT-first — source-deepseek-r1 R1-Zero shows base+RL alone produces frontier reasoning. Where does SFT remain necessary?
    • Emergence is real or artefact — Wei vs Schaeffer: the most consequential ongoing scaling debate.
    • MoE × MoD × Engram — multiple sparsity axes interact unpredictably.
    • Does looping scale past its training range? (2026-10-03) — source-varunneal-diffusion-will-be-everywhere says no and argues a loop that settles to a fixed point gains nothing more; source-loopcd-decoding-looped-transformers finds Huginn settled by step 16 of 32; source-equilibrium-reasoners ties its gains to settling into attractors. No source here clearly tests loops beyond their trained range.
    • Is any decision model calibrated? (2026-10-03) — Jev's confidences are sold as calibrated; neither source-willison-jev-decision-models nor source-raschka-text-classification-to-jev checks. See concept-calibration.
  2. Still uncovered/thin (2026-07 review):
    • Diffusion image/video generation (only FMLM + UL); world-model side covered better than generation side.
    • Audio generation (TTS, music).
    • Neuromorphic / spiking nets.
    • RL foundations — now more pressing: entity-richard-sutton entered the wiki, R1/GRPO and RLVR-style training recur everywhere, but there is no PPO/GRPO/TD-learning foundational source. Biggest single gap.
    • Continual learning / agent memory — a cluster is forming (source-continual-interactive-causal-agents, MLEvolve's self-evolving memory, Sutton's continual backprop, MemGPT) but has no concept page to hold it. Superseded (2026-10-03): concept-continual-learning now holds it, as a stub, with source-local-support-learning as its first full-text source.
    • Adversarial robustness / jailbreaking — only an ecosystem note (source-2026-q2-ai-ecosystem-notes); no primary sources despite alignment coverage.
    • Model compression and efficient training (2026-09-26) — no page of its own on weight pruning, neural architecture search or distributed training; guide-mit-efficient-ml-6-5940 marks these gaps lecture by lecture.
    • Evaluation methodology as a topic — FrontierCode, tokenflation-style measurement, AIRS-Bench, and idea-benchmark-honest-epistemic-loop all circle benchmark validity, but no concept page unifies contamination/saturation/measurement.
  3. The three axes don't yet cross-reference deeply:
    • Neural-symbolic ↔ Predictive: when JEPA's predictor is a symbolic substrate, what changes?
    • Foundational canon ↔ Predictive: the canonical scaling/reasoning literature mostly assumes generative-text world models; JEPA/world-models force a re-examination.
  4. Temporal: Most sources are 2017–2026, with heavy weight on 2023–2026. Pre-2017 RL roots (DQN, PPO, etc.) and pre-deep-learning are absent — could matter for the world-models / active-inference threads.
  5. Author research integration: The local experiment pages are first-pass maps. They need future updates with exact artifact paths, command lines, seed/split metadata, and benchmark-truth separation whenever a local run is promoted as evidence.
  6. Sequential-measurement foundations: the corpus has a gap around proper scoring rules, prequential MDL, online expert mixtures/switching codes, causal identification, and mutually untrusted federated evaluation. These need older source/concept pages, not only recent project notes.
