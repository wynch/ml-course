Active Inference with Empathy Mechanism

source created 2026-04-05 updated 2026-04-05 active-inference empathy social-agents robotics

Authors: Tadayuki Matsumura, Kanako Esaki, Shao Yang, Chihiro Yoshimura, Hiroyuki Mizuno Published: Artificial Life, Vol 30, Issue 2, pp 277-297 (2024)

Summary

Incorporates empathy into active inference for socially adaptive robot behavior. Agents empathize with surrounding others to behave appropriately in diverse situations.

Key Ideas

  • Empathy integrated into active inference framework
  • Evaluated on autonomous mobile robot control
  • Results: reduced other agent's travel distance by 13.7%, increased margin by 25.8%
  • Trade-off: increased own travel distance by 8.2% (altruistic behavior)

Connections

  • Core example of concept-active-inference applied to robotics
  • Social behavior through predictive processing
  • Connects neuroscience-inspired AI with practical robotics
