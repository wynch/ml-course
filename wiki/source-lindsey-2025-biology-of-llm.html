On the Biology of a Large Language Model

source created 2026-04-13 updated 2026-04-13 interpretability circuit-tracing claude planning multi-hop 2025

Lindsey, Gurnee, Ameisen, Chen, Pearce, Turner, ..., Olah — Anthropic, March 2025

Companion application paper to source-circuit-tracing-methods-anthropic: uses attribution graphs on Claude 3.5 Haiku to document ~10 behavioral case studies.

Key findings

  • Multi-step factual reasoning: Dallas → Texas → Austin is an actual two-hop circuit; the intermediate "Texas" feature can be causally manipulated mid-computation to redirect the answer.
  • Forward planning in poetry: the model chooses a line-ending rhyme before writing the line, not reactively — planning is a mechanistic fact.
  • Multilingual abstraction: shared high-level features across languages, specialized surface features near input/output.
  • Refusal circuits and jailbreak dynamics, motivated / hallucinated arithmetic, and more.

Neural↔symbolic bridge

First large-scale demonstration that frontier-LM behaviors decompose into named, causally-verifiable symbolic steps. Planning and multi-hop reasoning are not folklore — they correspond to specific activated features in specific attribution sub-graphs. The "biology" framing explicitly positions these diagrams as the symbolic-level description of what the net computes.
