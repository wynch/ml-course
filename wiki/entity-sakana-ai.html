Sakana AI

entity created 2026-07-04 updated 2026-10-03 organization evolutionary self-improvement scientific-discovery japan

Tokyo-based AI lab (founded by David Ha, Llion Jones, Ren Ito) focused on nature-inspired and evolutionary methods: evolutionary model merging, The AI Scientist (automated ML research papers), ShinkaEvolve (evolutionary program search), and the evosax/gymnax JAX tooling line. Researcher Robert T. Lange is a recurring voice in the wiki's evolutionary-discovery cluster (he flagged source-mlevolve, which competes with ShinkaEvolve-style systems).

Position in the wiki

  • Anchor organization for the evolutionary side of concept-automated-scientific-discovery and concept-self-improvement — The AI Scientist is the most-cited "AI writes the paper end-to-end" system, sitting in the same Variation → Evaluation → Selective-retention template as source-alphaevolve / source-funsearch.
  • Fugu (2026): Sakana's multi-agent model line, marketed with an explicitly geopolitical angle — a Japanese frontier lab positioning itself as free of US export-control risk while claiming parity with frontier Western models; see source-2026-q2-ai-ecosystem-notes.
  • PC-ALM (2026): Jeffrey Seely and Julian Gould's local-learning alternative to backpropagation, predictive coding with a Lagrange multiplier per layer; matches backprop on residual MLPs up to depth 128 in the paper (raw/arxiv-2605-31022-augmented-lagrangian-predictive-coding.md, §5.3). The September blog adds 1000-layer MLPs on MNIST within about 2 points of backprop, shown only in a blog figure; the paper does not test them (raw/web-2026-sakana-pc-alm-blog.md, Results). A neuroscience-driven line, apart from the evolutionary one. See source-pc-alm-augmented-lagrangian-predictive-coding.
  • Note: source-ha-schmidhuber-world-models co-author David Ha is Sakana's CEO — the world-models lineage and the evolutionary lineage meet here.
