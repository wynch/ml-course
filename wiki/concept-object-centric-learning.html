Object-Centric Learning

concept created 2026-10-20 updated 2026-10-20 object-centric slot-attention perception world-models entity-persistence stub hub

A stub. Object-centric learning gives a model one vector per object (a "slot") instead of one vector per image or per patch, so that later steps can track, predict and reason about objects one at a time. Slot Attention (Locatello et al., 2020) is the usual reference: a fixed number of slots compete for image regions (arXiv 2006.15055; not in raw).

The wiki holds no primary object-centric paper. JEPA-Anything places slots in a wider family: "entity-aligned slots", like disentangled coordinates (β-VAE) and object-and-relation world models, need "corresponding inductive biases" to come out (raw/arxiv-2609-20800-jepa-anything-learning-predictive-models-across.md, §4). AXIOM (Heins et al., arXiv 2505.24784; not in raw) runs active inference over object-centric mixture models that grow as needed; source-active-inference-foundations records its game results.
