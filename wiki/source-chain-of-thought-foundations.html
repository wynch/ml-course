Chain-of-Thought Foundations

source created 2026-05-07 updated 2026-09-26 chain-of-thought reasoning prompting sampling test-time-compute foundational

Two Google Research papers from early 2022 that together founded the test-time-reasoning era. Wei et al. introduced CoT prompting; Wang et al. (same group, two months later) showed sampling N CoTs and majority-voting beats greedy decoding. Read together — Self-Consistency is structurally a strict upgrade to CoT and is almost always cited as a pair.

Wei et al. — "Chain-of-Thought Prompting" (Jan 2022)

arXiv: 2201.11903. Wei, Wang, Schuurmans, Bosma, Ichter, Xia, Chi, Le, Zhou.

Adding a few worked-example demonstrations with intermediate reasoning steps in the prompt — "chain-of-thought" (CoT) — dramatically improves arithmetic, commonsense, and symbolic reasoning. The gain is emergent: appears only at sufficient model scale (~100B params for the strong effect on math word problems) and is roughly zero or negative on smaller models.

Method: few-shot CoT prompting. Include 8 worked examples in the prompt, each showing question → step-by-step reasoning → answer. No fine-tuning, no architecture change. Tested on PaLM (540B), GPT-3 (175B), LaMDA, UL2; benchmarks: GSM8K, MultiArith, ASDiv, AQuA, SVAMP, CommonsenseQA, StrategyQA, Last Letter, Coin Flip.

Key numbers:

  • GSM8K: PaLM 540B with CoT → 56.9% vs 17.9% standard prompting (>3× improvement; >SOTA fine-tuned at the time).
  • MultiArith: 94.7% (CoT) vs ~25% (standard).
  • SVAMP: 79.0%.
  • Smaller models (PaLM 62B, LaMDA 137B) show much smaller or absent CoT gains — emergent with scale.

Wang et al. — "Self-Consistency Improves CoT" (Mar 2022)

arXiv: 2203.11171. Wang, Wei, Schuurmans, Le, Chi, Narang, Chowdhery, Zhou.

Replaces greedy decoding of a single chain-of-thought with sampling many CoTs and majority-voting their final answers. The decoding rule itself becomes the inference algorithm. Drop-in upgrade to CoT with no training — just temperature > 0 and a vote.

Method:

  • Same few-shot CoT prompt as Wei et al.
  • Sample N diverse reasoning paths (typically N=40, T≈0.7).
  • Marginalise over the reasoning by taking the most-frequent final answer.
  • Insight: the answer is the latent variable; many surface chains converge on it if the model has the right intuition; majority vote selects the high-likelihood mode.

Key numbers (PaLM 540B):

  • GSM8K: 17.9% → 56.9% with CoT → 74.4% with self-consistency. +17.5 over CoT.
  • MultiArith: 99.3%.
  • SVAMP: 86.6%.
  • AQuA-RAT: +6.9 over CoT.
  • Strongest gains on tasks with small/structured answer spaces (math, multiple-choice). Compute–accuracy curves smooth: more samples → better accuracy with diminishing returns around N=40.

Why this pair matters

CoT is the inflection point for concept-test-time-compute: spending tokens on intermediate reasoning at inference time produces measurable capability gains, with no additional training. Every modern reasoning system descends from this — o1/o3-style "thinking" models, source-pdr-rethinking-thinking-tokens (Parallel-Distill-Refine), MCTS-augmented decoding (source-rap-reasoning-planning, source-lats-tree-search-agents), the entire concept-harness-as-symbolic-substrate thesis (the substrate executes; the chain-of-thought is the program).

Self-Consistency is the cleanest demonstration that inference-time sampling = capability. Sampling diversity exposes a richer latent distribution than greedy decoding can; majority voting amplifies the modal answer. This reframes "reasoning" as a property of the distribution, not of any single chain — the lens source-distribution-sharpening-power-sampling formalises three years later: many test-time compute techniques (and even RL post-training) act as distribution-sharpening operators rather than installing new capability.

Tensions for concept-llm-reasoning

CoT's emergence-with-scale finding is one of the canonical exhibits in the source-emergence-debate. If Schaeffer is right that emergence is a metric artefact, parts of the CoT story weaken — at least, the sharpness of the transition is suspect.

Other critiques: source-measuring-cot-monitorability questions whether the visible chain matches the actual computation; source-llm-reasoning-skeptics argues "reasoning" never escapes the training-distribution manifold.
