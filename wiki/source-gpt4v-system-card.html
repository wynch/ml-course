GPT-4V — System Card for Vision-Enabled GPT-4

source created 2026-05-06 updated 2026-05-06 multimodal vision-language gpt-4v openai safety red-teaming system-card

OpenAI — September 2023

The closed-source frontier datapoint for the VLM era. The system card describes the deployment of vision capabilities on top of GPT-4 (with image input added during the same training programme that produced the text-only GPT-4 in its lineage) — heavy on safety evaluation, red-teaming, and refusal behaviour, light on architectural detail. The architectural opacity is itself a deliberate signal; OpenAI did not disclose the visual-encoder choice, training data, or fusion mechanism, in contrast to the openly-published LLaVA / Qwen-VL / Flamingo line.

What the card actually contains

  • Capabilities: image understanding for VQA, OCR, chart and diagram reading, multi-image reasoning, location identification, scientific-figure interpretation. Demonstrated on diverse domains (medical, scientific, geographic).
  • Refusal and mitigation policies: explicit refusal of person identification from photos, declining to solve CAPTCHAs, declining to interpret medical images for diagnosis, refusing to follow instructions hidden inside images (prompt-injection-via-image).
  • Red-teaming results: external red-teamers probed the model for hate-speech triggers, scientific-uplift risks (CBRN), disinformation potential, ungrounded inferences (e.g. inferring sexuality, race), and prompt-injection-by-screenshot. The report quantifies measured refusal/error rates.
  • Sensitive populations: extra mitigations around children, biometrics, person identification.

What the card omits (deliberately)

  • The vision encoder architecture. The card does not say whether GPT-4V uses a CLIP-style encoder, a VQ-tokenizer, a perceiver-style resampler, or a learned adapter into the LLM token space — all of which were live options in the concept-multimodal design space at the time.
  • Training data composition for the vision branch.
  • Number of visual tokens per image; image-resolution handling; multi-image fusion mechanism.

This opacity is the core difference from the source-llava-visual-instruction / source-qwen-vl line, which publish the recipe end-to-end.

Significance

GPT-4V is the canonical example of a deployment-grade VLM in the post-LLaVA era. Its public-facing capabilities forced the open-source community (LLaVA, Qwen-VL, MiniCPM, IDEFICS) into a chase for parity. The system-card format itself — capabilities, mitigations, red-team findings, refusal taxonomies — became the template for subsequent multimodal-frontier release notes (Claude vision, Gemini vision). Useful in the wiki as the closed-frontier baseline against which open-source VLMs are measured, and as the institutional artefact paired with entity-openai.
