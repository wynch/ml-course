A stub. A researcher in self-supervised learning, at Brown University, with Meta-FAIR on LeJEPA and AMI Labs on LeVJEPA (raw/arxiv-2511-08544-lejepa-provable-and-scalable-self-supervised.md, title block; raw/arxiv-2608-27395-levjepa-efficient-scalable-video-pretraining-without-fulltext.md, title block). Most of his papers in the wiki are with entity-yann-lecun.

The thread through them: drop the stack of tricks that keep joint-embedding training from collapsing (stop-gradients, slow-moving teachers, many loss terms) and replace it with one regularizer whose job can be proved. source-weak-sigreg tests a cheaper form of SIGReg by another author.
