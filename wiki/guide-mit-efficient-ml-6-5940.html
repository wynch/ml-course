MIT 6.5940 Fall 2026 — a tour against this wiki

guide created 2026-09-26 updated 2026-09-26 guide syllabus efficiency quantization pruning distillation inference edge-ai

MIT's efficient deep learning course, taught by Song Han with Shang Yang, Tuesdays and Thursdays, 2026-09-10 to 2026-12-10. It is a PhD-level class with five labs and one group project, and no exams; the stated goal is to understand efficient deep learning and to run an LLM on a laptop (R, "About"; "Logistics"). Prerequisites: MIT 6.191 (computation structures) and 6.390 (intro to ML) (R, "About").

R = raw/web-2026-mit-6-5940-fall-syllabus.md. The syllabus gives titles and dates only; the slides and videos behind them were not ingested. On 2026-09-26 only lectures 1-5 had slides and videos linked (R, capture note).

Each stop below is one block of lectures, in the course's order. "Wiki" names the pages to read beside it; "Course" names the matching module in course/modules/. A gap means neither has it.

Stop 0 — Introduction and basics (lectures 1-2, Sep 10-15)

  • Lecture 2, "Basics of Deep Learning"; Lab 0 out (R, "Schedule").
  • Course: 01-autograd, 02-neural-networks.

Chapter I — Efficient inference

Stop 1 — Pruning and sparsity (lectures 3-4, Sep 17-22)

  • Wiki: thin. source-sequential-attention-google lists weight pruning among the uses of a greedy selection method, in one line; no page explains pruning. The nearest idea is sparsity by routing, where each input uses a few experts and no weight is removed: concept-mixture-of-experts, source-mixture-of-depths.
  • Course: 00c-kernels-hopfield names support vectors as "the sparsity intuition behind everything that prunes" (course/modules/00c-kernels-hopfield/README.md).

Stop 2 — Quantization (lectures 5-6, Sep 24-29)

  • Wiki: source-quantization-ground-up-ngrok (symmetric and asymmetric integer schemes, 8/4/2-bit trade-offs); concept-llm-serving for why fewer bytes can mean faster decode.
  • Course: 07-inference-internals, the int8 lab (per-row symmetric int8, 3.9× smaller file, perplexity unchanged to three decimals).

Stop 3 — Neural architecture search (lectures 7-8, Oct 1-6)

  • Wiki: gap. The closest is automated search over programs rather than networks: source-harness-optimization-evolve-harness, source-rrsi-regularized-harness-evolution.
  • Course: gap.

Stop 4 — Knowledge distillation (lecture 9, Oct 8)

  • Wiki: source-hinton-knowledge-distillation (the 2015 paper); analysis-self-distillation-as-universal-lever (the same mechanism across many methods).
  • Course: gap.

Stop 5 — TinyML on microcontrollers (lectures 10-11, Oct 15-20)

  • Lecture 10 is "MCUNet: TinyML on Microcontrollers"; lecture 11 "TinyEngine and Parallel Processing" (R, "Schedule").
  • Wiki: Vincent's own edge work: experiment-edge-autoresearch-and-ane (Apple Neural Engine), idea-wavelet-substrate-for-edge-intelligence, experiment-edge-compression-federation.
  • Course: gap (the course runs on a laptop CPU, not a microcontroller).

Chapter II — Domain-specific optimization

Stop 6 — Transformers and LLMs (lecture 12, Oct 22)

  • Wiki: concept-transformer-architecture, source-attention-is-all-you-need.
  • Course: 04-attention-transformer, 05-transformers-library.

Stop 7 — LLM quantization and deployment (lecture 13, Oct 27)

  • Wiki: source-inside-vllm-gordic (paged KV cache, batching, speculative decoding), concept-llm-serving, source-quantization-ground-up-ngrok.
  • Course: 07-inference-internals (KV cache, int8, a Zig engine that matches PyTorch). Lab 5, "LLM deployment on laptop", is the course's version of that module's closing suggestion to run a GGUF model under llama.cpp.

Stop 8 — LLM post-training (lecture 14, Oct 29)

  • Wiki: concept-rlhf, source-instructgpt-rlhf, source-dpo-direct-preference-optimization, source-lora-low-rank-adaptation, concept-rlhf-alternatives.
  • Course: 06-fine-tuning (SFT, LoRA, DPO).

Stop 9 — Long context (lecture 15, Nov 3)

  • Wiki: concept-long-context, source-flashattention; chunked prefill and the KV cache's memory cost in source-inside-vllm-gordic.
  • Course: 07-inference-internals notes that the KV cache "is what makes long contexts so memory-hungry"; the module stops at 128 tokens.

Stop 10 — Vision transformers (lecture 16, Nov 5)

  • Wiki: guide-ssl-vision-tour (DINO and later ViT-based work).
  • Course: 08-vision (from convolutions to ViTs; fine-tunes ViT-tiny).

Stop 11 — Diffusion models (lectures 17-18, Nov 10-12)

  • Wiki: source-latent-diffusion-stable-diffusion, source-dit-diffusion-transformer, concept-diffusion-language-models.
  • Course: 09-diffusion.

Chapter III — Efficient training

Stop 12 — Distributed training (lectures 19-20, Nov 17-19)

  • Wiki: thin. source-deepseek-v3 (FP8 training, MoE at scale), source-frontier-model-training. Parallelism appears only on the serving side, in source-inside-vllm-gordic (tensor, pipeline, data).
  • Course: gap.

Stop 13 — On-device training and transfer learning (lecture 21, Nov 24)

  • Wiki: experiment-edge-autoresearch-and-ane (training on Apple Silicon), concept-retrofitting.
  • Course: 06-fine-tuning and 08-vision for transfer by fine-tuning.

Chapter IV — Advanced topics (Dec 1-10)

A guest lecture (Dec 1) and three sessions of final project presentations (R, "Schedule").
