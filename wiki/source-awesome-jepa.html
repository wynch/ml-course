Awesome JEPA (Resource List)

Summary

A curated, CC0 index of the JEPA (Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture) ecosystem — papers, models, code, datasets, benchmarks, talks, and explainers. It opens with a clean statement of the JEPA thesis: predict the representation of a target from the representation of a context, entirely in latent space, so the model can discard unpredictable pixel/token detail and keep structure useful for understanding and planning; three components (context encoder, target encoder, predictor) with an anti-collapse asymmetry (stop-gradient EMA teacher, or variance/covariance penalty). It frames JEPA as the centerpiece of LeCun's autonomous-machine-intelligence proposal.

The list is well beyond the canonical Meta FAIR line. It tracks the core architectures (I-JEPA, V-JEPA, V-JEPA 2 / 2-AC, V-JEPA 2.1, MC-JEPA), a substantial theory/recipes section (LeJEPA + SIGReg, EB-JEPA, IWM, VICReg, slow-features and noise-avoidance analyses, variational Var-JEPA), and — most usefully for this wiki — a sprawling variants-by-domain catalog: audio/speech, 3D/point clouds, graphs/molecules, time series/tabular, medical imaging and biosignals (EEG, ECG, brain dynamics, radiology, echo, ultrasound, genomics), Earth observation, language/recommendation (LLM-JEPA, TI-JEPA, JEPA4Rec), and generative modeling (D-JEPA, JEPA-T). The world-models/robotics/planning section carries the recent frontier: LeWorldModel (end-to-end from pixels with SIGReg), Hierarchical Planning with Latent World Models, VLA-JEPA, Causal-JEPA, and the stable-worldmodel platform.

Two entries are worth flagging as this wiki's own thematic neighbors. LLM-JEPA (Huang et al., 2025) brings the JEPA objective into large-language-model training — a bridge between the latent-prediction and LLM lines. And the maintainer's own ProvableWorldModel provides a commit-and-audit proof system for deterministic, quantized inference of a JEPA-style world model, enabling verifiable (zero-knowledge) AI inference — a novel intersection of world models with cryptographic verifiability not otherwise covered here.

Key Ideas

  • Compact, verified index of the JEPA ecosystem with a crisp architecture explainer (context/target encoders + predictor, anti-collapse asymmetry).
  • Broad domain-variant taxonomy (audio, 3D, graphs, time series, medical/biosignal, Earth observation, language, generative) — a fast map of where latent-prediction has spread.
  • Tracks the current world-model/planning frontier: LeWorldModel, Hierarchical Planning w/ Latent World Models, stable-worldmodel, VLA-JEPA, Causal-JEPA.
  • Surfaces two cross-cutting items: LLM-JEPA (JEPA objective for LLMs) and ProvableWorldModel (verifiable/ZK inference of a JEPA world model).
