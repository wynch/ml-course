V-JEPA Family — Video Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture

source created 2026-05-07 updated 2026-05-07 jepa self-supervised-learning video world-model robotics meta-fair yann-lecun

The video branch of the JEPA programme. Three generations from Meta FAIR: V-JEPA (the basic feature-prediction recipe applied to video), V-JEPA 2 (scale + action conditioning → world-model-as-planner), V-JEPA 2.1 (dense features + multi-modal tokenizers). Each builds on the same JEPA spine: predict embeddings of masked targets, never reconstruct pixels.

V-JEPA — Bardes et al., Feb 2024

arXiv: 2404.08471. Bardes, Garrido, Ponce, Chen, Rabbat, LeCun, Assran, Ballas.

Lifts source-ijepa-self-supervised-learning from images to video. Same recipe — predict masked targets in representation space — applied to spatio-temporal patches. The point of the paper is the negative result for generative video pretraining: a feature-prediction objective alone, with no pixel decoder, no text, no negatives, no augmentations, beats pixel-reconstruction baselines on motion-heavy benchmarks.

Method:

  • Clips tokenised into 3D (time × height × width) tubelets fed to a ViT.
  • 3D multi-block mask removes large connected spatio-temporal regions (a whole moving object across many frames). Critical — small random masks let the model cheat with local interpolation.
  • Two encoders (context + EMA target) and a predictor, all ViTs. L2 loss between predicted and target embeddings on masked tubelets only.
  • Trained on 2M public videos.

Key numbers (ViT-H/16, video-only training): Kinetics-400 81.9% top-1; Something-Something-v2 72.2% (motion-heavy; pixel-recon methods like VideoMAE struggle here); ImageNet-1K 77.9% — competitive image features as a side effect of video training.

V-JEPA 2 — Assran et al., June 2025

arXiv: 2506.09985.

Promotes the JEPA programme from a representation-learning method to a world-model substrate for robotic planning. Two ingredients: (i) scale aggressively — up to 1B-parameter encoder, 1M+ hours of internet video; (ii) post-train a small action-conditioned latent world model (V-JEPA 2-AC) on barely any robot data, then plan with it.

Two-stage training:

  1. Action-free pretraining. Standard V-JEPA objective on 1M+ hours of video + image set. Yields a frozen visual encoder.
  2. Action-conditioned post-training. Predictor conditioned on robot actions, trained on <62 hours of unlabelled Droid videos to predict next latent given current latent and action. → V-JEPA 2-AC.

Understanding & prediction benchmarks: Something-Something-v2 77.3 top-1 (motion); Epic-Kitchens-100 long-horizon action anticipation 39.7 Recall@5 (SOTA, beats task-specific specialists).

The robotics result: V-JEPA 2-AC deployed zero-shot on Franka arms in two different labs — no data from those robots, no task-specific fine-tuning, no reward signal. Planning by image-goal MPC in the latent space: given a goal image, search action sequences whose predicted latent rollouts terminate near the goal embedding. ~80% success on cup pick-and-place vs ~15% for Octo (a comparable-era video-language-action policy). Headline result for the JEPA-as-world-model thesis from source-lecun-path-autonomous-intelligence: world model learned from passive video, generalises across embodiments and tasks via planning.

V-JEPA 2.1 — Mur-Labadia et al., March 2026

arXiv: 2603.14482.

Refresh that fixes V-JEPA 2's persistent weakness: dense, location-aware features. Three ingredients plus pure scaling. Reported as +20pp real-robot grasping improvement over V-JEPA 2-AC, plus SOTA on Ego4D and EPIC-KITCHENS anticipation.

Three ingredients:

  1. Dense Predictive Loss. Earlier JEPAs only computed loss on masked tokens. V-JEPA 2.1 extends loss to all tokens, masked and visible. Previous setup encouraged visible tokens to act as global aggregators, hurting per-location features; loss on every token grounds each in its spatio-temporal location.
  2. Deep Self-Supervision. JEPA loss applied at multiple intermediate encoder layers, not just the output. Provides training signal throughout the network.
  3. Multi-Modal Tokenizers. Modality-specific learned tokenizers (one for images, one for videos) feed a single shared encoder. Native joint training across image and video.

Plus scaling: image data 1M → 142M, model 300M → 2B params, with monotonic gains.

Key numbers: Ego4D short-term object-interaction anticipation 7.71 mAP (SOTA); EPIC-KITCHENS high-level action anticipation 40.8 Recall@5; +20pp real-robot grasping vs V-JEPA 2-AC.

Why this matters as a lineage

V-JEPA established that video — the highest-bandwidth, most redundant signal — can be turned into useful semantic features purely by predicting in latent space. V-JEPA 2 turned that representation into a functional world model for zero-shot robot control. V-JEPA 2.1 closed the dense-features gap that had limited grounded manipulation. Each generation extends the JEPA thesis (source-lecun-path-autonomous-intelligence) one rung further.

Pairs naturally with source-why-learn-if-you-can-infer-active-inference: that paper shows hand-tuned generative models beat RL on Habitat household tasks; V-JEPA 2-AC shows learned latent generative models beat task-specific RL/imitation on real Franka manipulation. Both data points argue the concept-active-inference / concept-world-models thesis: planning under a good predictive model is a viable alternative to scaling reward-driven RL.
