Keel

source created 2026-05-06 updated 2026-05-06 transformer-architecture depth-scaling layernorm residual-connections 2026 bytedance

Chen Chen et al. (ByteDance Seed) — arXiv 2601.19895, January 2026

Replaces the standard Pre-LN residual path with a Highway-style connection on a Post-LN block, preventing signal vanishing from top to bottom layers and enabling stable training at depths exceeding 1000 layers — without specialised initialisation or optimiser tricks. The deeper the model, the more Keel pulls ahead of Pre-LN baselines.

Key Claims

  • Pre-LN's gradient stability comes at the cost of expressivity decay with depth.
  • A learned Highway gate around a Post-LN block recovers Post-LN's expressivity while keeping training stable.
  • Trains robustly at >1000 layers; consistent improvements in perplexity and depth-scaling characteristics over Pre-LN baselines.
  • Implementation: "a few lines of code" — drop-in for existing pre-training pipelines.

Method

Modifies the residual connection in Post-LN transformers with a Highway-network style learned gate, providing controlled mixing of the residual and transformed signal. This stabilises optimisation without sacrificing the deeper-information-flow properties of Post-LN.

Connections

  • Directly engages the concept-transformer-architecture depth-scaling debate alongside source-neural-depth-scaling (loss ~ 1/depth, layers as ensemble averages).
  • Complements source-residual-matrix-transformer which attacks the residual stream's representational capacity rather than depth-stability.
  • Open question: does Keel's depth advantage persist when combined with the FLOP-saving moves of source-residual-matrix-transformer or hybrid architectures like source-olmo-hybrid-rnn-transformer?
