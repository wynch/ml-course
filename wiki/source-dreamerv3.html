DreamerV3 — Mastering Diverse Domains through World Models

source created 2026-05-06 updated 2026-05-06 world-models model-based-rl generalist minecraft hafner 2023 nature

Danijar Hafner, Jurgis Pasukonis, Jimmy Ba, Timothy Lillicrap — arXiv 2301.04104, Jan 2023 (Nature 2025).

The "general world-model algorithm" claim. DreamerV3 keeps the Dreamer V1 skeleton — RSSM + actor-critic in imagination — and applies it to 150+ tasks across 8 benchmarks with a single fixed hyperparameter set: Atari 100k/200M, ProcGen, DMLab, DeepMind Control Suite (proprioceptive and visual), Crafter, BSuite, and Minecraft. No per-domain tuning. Published in Nature in 2025.

What changed: robustness tricks

The headline isn't a new architecture, it's the engineering required to make one config not blow up across reward scales of 12 orders of magnitude.

  • Symlog predictions: every regression head predicts symlog(x) = sign(x) · log(|x|+1) instead of raw values. Compresses huge rewards/values without breaking small ones, eliminating the need for reward normalisation.
  • Two-hot critic: discretised value head with twin-symlog targets, plus regression on return percentiles instead of raw returns to make the actor's update scale-invariant.
  • KL balancing + free bits: the RSSM KL term is split into a dynamics loss (prior toward posterior, scaled small) and a representation loss (posterior toward prior, scaled large), each with a free-bits floor. This stops the world model from collapsing the stochastic latent to a delta.
  • LayerNorm everywhere + small weight init: stabilises training across data regimes from 100k frames to billions.
  • EMA target networks, percentile-based actor entropy, fixed clip ranges — small fixes that compound.

Headline result: Minecraft diamond from scratch

DreamerV3 is the first algorithm to mine a diamond in Minecraft from scratch without human data, demonstrations, or curricula. This is a long-horizon (~20 minutes of play, dozens of crafted intermediate items, sparse reward) task that had resisted all prior model-free and imitation-bootstrapped approaches.

Scaling

The paper also documents monotonic returns to model size and replay ratio across all benchmarks — bigger world model + more imagination per real step ⇒ better scores, with no instability. This is the model-based analogue of the LLM scaling story and a direct rebuttal to "model-based RL doesn't scale".

Relevance

The single-config claim is what makes this a wiki anchor: it is to model-based RL what GPT-3 was to language modelling — same recipe, scaled, tuned once, then unleashed. The pairing with source-why-learn-if-you-can-infer-active-inference is striking and important for this wiki: VERSES showed a hand-tuned generative model outperforms RL on Habitat with a few hand-tuned skills; DreamerV3 shows a learned generative model generalises across 150+ tasks with one config. Same predict-then-plan structure (concept-active-inference), opposite end of the learned-vs-specified spectrum.

Connections: extends source-dreamer-v1; uses the same RSSM as PlaNet; comparable continuous-control alternative is TD-MPC2; sits in the concept-world-models hub. Symlog/two-hot are clean examples of concept-representation-learning tricks used to stabilise downstream RL signal.
