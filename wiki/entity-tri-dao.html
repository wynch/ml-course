Tri Dao

entity created 2026-05-07 updated 2026-05-07 researcher author stanford princeton together-ai transformer-architecture ssm mamba flashattention systems

Systems / architecture researcher; PhD at Stanford with Christopher Ré, then Princeton faculty and Chief Scientist at Together AI. The wiki has no dedicated org entity for any of these affiliations yet — Tri Dao's footprint is best understood as a personal research thread rather than an institutional one.

In this wiki he is the systems-side spine of post-2022 transformer architecture: every architectural conversation eventually touches a Dao kernel, a Dao co-author paper, or a Dao model. His papers sit at the bridge between attention efficiency (FlashAttention) and post-attention architectures (Mamba, Mamba-2).

Sources in the wiki

  • source-flashattention — first author of FlashAttention v1 (Dao, Fu, Ermon, Rudra, Ré, NeurIPS 2022) and single author of FlashAttention-2 (2023). IO-aware tiling of softmax-attention that turned long-context training from a memory-bound regret into routine practice. Effectively a hardware-software co-design that downstream every frontier-model report cites.
  • source-mamba-ssm — co-author with Albert Gu (CMU / Princeton, arXiv 2312.00752). Selective state-space model that made SSMs competitive with attention at sub-quadratic cost — the first credible non-attention transformer alternative at scale.
  • source-mamba-2-ssm-duality — co-author (Tri Dao, Albert Gu; arXiv 2405.21060, ICML 2024). Establishes the SSM ↔ structured-attention duality and makes Mamba-2 train with attention-friendly matmuls, recovering the throughput Mamba-1 left on the table.
  • source-m2rnn-matrix-valued-states — co-author (Mishra, Tan, Stoica, Gonzalez, Tri Dao). RNN with matrix-valued hidden states — a follow-on in the post-Mamba "structured recurrent state" design space.

Themes

The unifying theme is make sequence models actually fast on real GPUs while preserving (or replacing) attention's expressivity. FlashAttention is the kernel-level win that bought transformers another generation of scale. Mamba and Mamba-2 are the architecture-level reaction once the wall came back into view: replace the quadratic dependence entirely, but co-design the algorithm with what tensor cores are good at. M2RNN extends the pattern outside the strict SSM frame. Every paper in this thread is system-aware first: the abstraction is chosen for its hardware behaviour, not the other way round.
