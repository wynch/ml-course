Condensed Synthesis

The core design split is predict then plan versus condition then act.

World-model lines build a latent or generative simulator and choose actions through imagination, MPC, or policy gradients. VLA lines learn a direct policy from vision/language/task context into action sequences. Diffusion and flow policies sit between these poles by modeling action distributions directly.

The wiki's strongest comparison remains analysis-world-models-vs-vla:

  • Dreamer / TD-MPC2: learned dynamics plus planning or imagined policy learning.
  • Genie: render or simulate possible worlds.
  • V-JEPA: predict task-relevant latents, not pixels.
  • RT-2 / OpenVLA / pi0: train a direct robot foundation policy.
