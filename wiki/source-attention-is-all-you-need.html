Attention Is All You Need

source created 2026-05-06 updated 2026-05-06 transformers architecture attention foundational 2017

Vaswani, Shazeer, Parmar, Uszkoreit, Jones, Gomez, Kaiser, Polosukhin — arXiv 1706.03762, NeurIPS 2017 (Google Brain / Google Research / U. Toronto)

The paper that launched the transformer era. Introduces the Transformer, a sequence-to-sequence architecture built entirely on scaled dot-product self-attention and feed-forward blocks, with no recurrence and no convolutions. Eliminates the sequential bottleneck of RNNs and enables massive parallelism on TPUs/GPUs.

Method

  • Encoder–decoder stack, each layer = multi-head self-attention + position-wise FFN, wrapped with residual connections and LayerNorm.
  • Multi-head attention: h=8 heads with separate Q/K/V projections; outputs concatenated and re-projected. Lets the model attend to different representation subspaces.
  • Scaled dot-product attention: softmax(QKᵀ / √d_k) · V — the √d_k scaling prevents softmax saturation.
  • Sinusoidal positional encodings — non-learned, allow extrapolation to longer sequences.
  • Masked decoder self-attention for autoregressive generation.

Key numbers

  • Base model: 65M parameters, 6 encoder + 6 decoder layers, d_model=512, h=8.
  • WMT 2014 EN→DE: 28.4 BLEU, beating prior SOTA by >2 BLEU.
  • WMT 2014 EN→FR: 41.8 BLEU, single-model SOTA.
  • Trained in 3.5 days on 8 P100 GPUs — orders of magnitude less than competing systems.

Why it matters

The substrate everything else in this wiki is built on. Every page tagged concept-transformer-architecture inherits Vaswani's attention/FFN/residual decomposition; every paper on concept-mechanistic-interpretability reverse-engineers circuits inside this architecture. The "residual stream + per-head Q/K/V" framing that Anthropic's interpretability program uses comes directly from here (source-anthropic-mi-foundations formalises it). It's also the reference point for every architectural alternative the wiki tracks: hybrid RNNs (source-olmo-hybrid-rnn-transformer), matrix-valued states (source-m2rnn-matrix-valued-states), Post-LN highway depth (source-keel-post-ln-highway), Mix-Compress-Refine theory (source-attention-sinks-compression-valleys), and one-step continuous-flow LMs (source-flow-map-language-models) all define themselves against this baseline.

The deeper bet that turned out to matter: attention scales (in parameters, data, and compute) in ways recurrence did not — setting up concept-scaling-laws and the entire GPT trajectory (source-gpt3-few-shot-learners).
