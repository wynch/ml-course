Synthesis

The 2026 application literature is converging on a practical pattern:

raw signal -> fixed or structured wavelet transform -> compact learned module -> robust task output

The strongest overlap with the author's experiment is in:

  • audio and sensor streams
  • industrial anomaly detection
  • lightweight vision restoration
  • distribution shift and uncertainty
  • time-series dynamics where high frequencies matter

What To Learn From These Papers

The wavelet substrate should not be benchmarked only on small clean image classification. The recent papers imply the important tests are:

  • non-stationary time series
  • noisy sensors
  • anomaly detection under few labels
  • out-of-distribution confidence
  • memory-limited inference
  • high-frequency preservation

Caution

Many 2026 wavelet papers are hybrid engineering papers where wavelets are one module inside a larger neural system. They support the substrate direction weakly unless they include ablations against equally small learned baselines. The local experiment should be stricter than most application papers.
