RLHF Alternatives

concept created 2026-04-05 updated 2026-10-20 reinforcement-learning training reward-models alternatives

Methods beyond standard Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback for aligning and improving language models.

Approaches

  • source-dpo-direct-preference-optimization — Rafailov 2023: closed-form derivation eliminates the reward model and PPO entirely. The most widely-adopted alternative; arguably no longer "alternative" but mainstream.
  • source-sdpo-self-distillation-rl — self-distillation on tokenized feedback, no external reward model needed.
  • source-randopt-gaussian-noise-llms — sample Gaussian perturbations of the weights, keep the top K on the task, ensemble by vote; "competitive with" PPO and GRPO for large models, not for small ones (arXiv 2603.12228, abstract). Raises the question: is RL training just noise + selection?
  • source-intuitor-reasoning-without-rewards — model confidence as sole reward signal.
  • source-distribution-sharpening-power-sampling — argues RL gains are inference-time amplification of latent reasoning, not new capability installation. Inference-time alternative rather than training-time.
  • source-soft-tokens-hard-truths — RL on a continuous chain of thought: noisy mixtures of word embeddings give the exploration; pass@1 matches normal-token RL, pass@32 is higher, and loss on out-of-domain answers rises less (raw/arxiv-2509-19170-soft-tokens-hard-truths.md, §3, §4).
  • source-frontier-learning-edge-of-capability — RL with checkable rewards where the training problems are made during training: a generator's settings are searched by regret (solved at least once, minus success rate) so the model always trains where GRPO's gradient is not zero. Dice after 500 steps: 71.8% against 33.3% for the best fixed-pool baseline (raw/arxiv-2609-35426-frontier-learning-training-llm-reasoners-at.md, §3.3.1, Table 2).
  • source-rl-reasoning-boundary-yue — Yue et al. 2025: RL with checkable rewards wins at pass@1, the base model wins at large k; six RL algorithms (PPO, GRPO, Reinforce++, RLOO, ReMax, DAPO) hit the same ceiling, while distillation from a stronger model raises it (raw/arxiv-2504-13837-does-reinforcement-learning-really-incentivize-reasoning.md, §3, §4.2, §4.3).
  • source-prorl — Liu et al. 2025 (NVIDIA): RL for over 2,000 steps with a KL penalty whose reference is reset when validation stalls; reach (pass@128) grows most on tasks the start was weak at, and shrinks on some maths (raw/arxiv-2505-24864-prorl-prolonged-reinforcement-learning-expands-reasoning.md, §2.3.1, §4.1, §4.2). Contradiction (open) with the line above: RL can widen reach, but here only after distillation and with long training.
  • source-spurious-rewards — Shao et al. 2025: on Qwen2.5-Math-7B, RLVR with random rewards gains +21.4 points on MATH-500, near the +29.1 of true rewards; on Llama and OLMo the same rewards give little or nothing (raw/arxiv-2506-10947-spurious-rewards-rethinking-training-signals-in.md, §2.2, §3). Warning: Qwen-Math gains even under random rewards, because GRPO's clipping raises what the model already favours (same file, §4). A new reward or RL method shown only on Qwen needs other model families and a random-reward baseline (same file, §7).
  • source-rls-razor — Shenfeld, Pari, Agrawal 2025: at equal new-task accuracy, RL (GRPO) forgets old skills far less than SFT, because sampling from the model keeps it near the base model in KL; SFT on the model's own correct answers does as well as GRPO, so on-policy data matters, not negative examples (raw/arxiv-2509-04259-rl-s-razor-why-online-reinforcement.md, §3, §5.1). An argument for RL that has nothing to do with reward models.
  • source-deepseek-r1 — DeepSeek 2025: pure RL post-training (GRPO) elicits o1-style reasoning. R1-Zero variant skips SFT entirely; the proof point that base model + RL alone produces frontier reasoning.

These collectively suggest the explicit reward model in standard concept-rlhf may be unnecessary — models contain sufficient signal for self-improvement, and inference-time methods can substitute for the RL phase.

Connections
