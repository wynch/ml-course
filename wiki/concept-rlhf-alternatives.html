RLHF Alternatives

concept created 2026-04-05 updated 2026-10-03 reinforcement-learning training reward-models alternatives

Methods beyond standard Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback for aligning and improving language models.

Approaches

  • source-dpo-direct-preference-optimization — Rafailov 2023: closed-form derivation eliminates the reward model and PPO entirely. The most widely-adopted alternative; arguably no longer "alternative" but mainstream.
  • source-sdpo-self-distillation-rl — self-distillation on tokenized feedback, no external reward model needed.
  • source-randopt-gaussian-noise-llms — provocatively, single-step Gaussian noise + ensembling matches GRPO/PPO. Raises the question: is RL training just noise + selection?
  • source-intuitor-reasoning-without-rewards — model confidence as sole reward signal.
  • source-distribution-sharpening-power-sampling — argues RL gains are inference-time amplification of latent reasoning, not new capability installation. Inference-time alternative rather than training-time.
  • source-soft-tokens-hard-truths — RL on a continuous chain of thought: noisy mixtures of word embeddings give the exploration; pass@1 matches normal-token RL, pass@32 is higher, and loss on out-of-domain answers rises less (raw/arxiv-2509-19170-soft-tokens-hard-truths.md, §3, §4).
  • source-frontier-learning-edge-of-capability — RL with checkable rewards where the training problems are made during training: a generator's settings are searched by regret (solved at least once, minus success rate) so the model always trains where GRPO's gradient is not zero. Dice after 500 steps: 71.8% against 33.3% for the best fixed-pool baseline (raw/arxiv-2609-35426-frontier-learning-training-llm-reasoners-at.md, §3.3.1, Table 2).
  • source-deepseek-r1 — DeepSeek 2025: pure RL post-training (GRPO) elicits o1-style reasoning. R1-Zero variant skips SFT entirely; the proof point that base model + RL alone produces frontier reasoning.

These collectively suggest the explicit reward model in standard concept-rlhf may be unnecessary — models contain sufficient signal for self-improvement, and inference-time methods can substitute for the RL phase.
