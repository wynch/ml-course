Yann LeCun

entity created 2026-05-07 updated 2026-10-20 researcher author meta-fair ami-labs jepa self-supervised-learning energy-based-models world-models position-paper

Chief AI Scientist at Meta and lead of entity-meta-fair's long-horizon vision-SSL programme.

Superseded: LeCun left Meta in late 2025 and co-founded AMI Labs (Advanced Machine Intelligence), a world-model start-up based in Paris; he chairs its board and Alexandre LeBrun is CEO. Its seed round of about $1.03B was announced on 2026-03-10 (tech.eu, https://tech.eu/2026/03/10/yann-lecun-s-paris-based-ai-world-model-startup-raises-more-than-1bn/; not in raw). The wiki's own source-levjepa lists AMI Labs among its authors' affiliations (line 12).

In this wiki he functions as the architect of the JEPA research direction — his 2022 position paper is the framing document that the I-JEPA, V-JEPA, and VL-JEPA papers explicitly implement.

Sources in the wiki

The framing document

  • source-lecun-path-autonomous-intelligence — sole author. The 2022 OpenReview position paper that defines the architecture (perception → world model → cost → actor → short-term memory), the JEPA objective, and the multi-level abstraction stack. Every other LeCun page in the wiki references this one.

JEPA lineage (executions of the position paper)

  • source-ijepa-self-supervised-learning — co-author with Assran, Duval, Misra, Bojanowski, Vincent, Rabbat, Ballas. The image-domain JEPA that operationalises latent-space prediction.
  • source-vjepa-family — co-author (Bardes et al.) on V-JEPA; the video JEPA used downstream as a robotics world model.
  • source-vl-jepa — co-author; vision-language JEPA closing the multi-modal end of the programme.
  • source-levjepa — co-author (2026, with AMI Labs among the affiliations); video JEPA trained with SIGReg alone.
  • source-lejepa — co-author with Balestriero, equal contribution (2025; NYU and Meta-FAIR): the isotropic Gaussian as the target shape for embeddings, and SIGReg to reach it without an EMA teacher.
  • source-weak-sigreg — builds on SIGReg from LeJEPA.
  • source-llm-jepa — co-author with Hai Huang and Balestriero (2025): a JEPA term added to language-model fine-tuning and small pretraining, beside next-token loss.
  • source-codelewm — not his work: ports his "predict in latent space" idea to source code.

Joint-embedding SSL precursors (non-contrastive / decorrelation branch)

  • source-ssl-decorrelation — co-author on both Barlow Twins (Zbontar, Jing, Misra, LeCun, Deny) and VICReg (Bardes, Ponce, LeCun). These information-maximisation losses are the precursor formulations to the JEPA collapse-prevention story.
  • source-ssl-cookbook-balestriero — co-author. Frames the contrastive / distillation / decorrelation methods as variants of one joint-embedding theme — the prose version of the JEPA argument.

Adjacent / referenced

  • source-ssl-contrastive-foundations — cited as the contrastive branch flowing into JEPA, not authored.
  • source-dino-lineage — cited as a "LeCun thesis" win (vision without text supervision), not authored.
  • source-attention-sinks-compression-valleys — co-author (Queipo-de-Llano et al.); a Meta+academic geometric study of attention-sink/compression-valley structure. One of two LeCun appearances outside the SSL/JEPA programme; the other is source-pc-alm-augmented-lagrangian-predictive-coding, which builds on his 1988 result that a network's Lagrangian multipliers equal backprop's gradients.

Themes

LeCun's wiki footprint is a single decade-spanning research programme: build vision foundation models that learn predictive world models from passive sensory data, without language supervision and without pixel-space generation. The chain is Barlow Twins / VICReg (decorrelation as collapse prevention) → position paper (architectural manifesto) → I-JEPA / V-JEPA / VL-JEPA (predict in latent space, not pixel space) → V-JEPA 2 as zero-shot robot world model. The non-JEPA papers — the SSL cookbook, the attention-sinks geometry note — are sidebar consolidations of the same joint-embedding worldview.
