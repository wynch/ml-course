Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)

source created 2026-05-06 updated 2026-05-06 rlhf dpo preference-learning foundational 2023

Rafailov, Sharma, Mitchell, Ermon, Manning, Finn — arXiv 2305.18290, May 2023 (Stanford / CZ Biohub)

Eliminates the reward model + PPO machinery of source-instructgpt-rlhf by showing that the optimal RLHF policy has a closed-form relationship to its log-probability ratio against the reference policy. This lets you fine-tune directly on pairwise preferences with a simple classification-style loss — no RM, no rollouts, no PPO. Same final objective; a single supervised stage.

The derivation

Standard RLHF maximises E[r(x,y)] − β · KL(π || π_ref). The optimal closed-form policy is:

π*(y|x) ∝ π_ref(y|x) · exp(r(x,y)/β).

Inverting: r(x,y) = β · log(π*(y|x) / π_ref(y|x)) + Z(x).

Plugging this into the Bradley–Terry preference likelihood, the partition Z(x) cancels in the chosen vs. rejected difference, and we are left with a simple binary-cross-entropy-style objective on the policy:

L_DPO = −E[log σ(β · log(π(y_w|x)/π_ref(y_w|x)) − β · log(π(y_l|x)/π_ref(y_l|x)))]

— differentiable, stable, and trainable with the same infrastructure as SFT.

Key results

  • Matches or beats PPO-based RLHF on summarization (Reddit TL;DR), single-turn dialogue (Anthropic HH), and sentiment-controlled generation.
  • Far simpler and more stable than PPO; no reward-model regression, no rollouts, no clipped-ratio surgery.
  • Robust to hyperparameter choice; β controls the strength of the implicit KL.

Why it matters

DPO is the second large simplification of post-training (after source-instructgpt-rlhf). It collapses three components — RM training, RL rollouts, PPO — into one supervised loss while preserving the underlying decision-theoretic objective. Most open-weight fine-tuning since 2023 (Llama-Instruct, Mistral-Instruct, Zephyr, Qwen-Instruct, Tulu, etc.) uses DPO or one of its variants (IPO, KTO, ORPO, SimPO, GRPO).

Beyond practical simplification, DPO is the cleanest argument for the concept-rlhf-alternatives thesis: the explicit reward model is not load-bearing. The "reward" was always implicit in the LM itself; you just need the right loss to extract it. This connects to the broader pattern this wiki tracks — that what looked like architectural/algorithmic complexity often turns out to be removable:

  • source-sdpo-self-distillation-rl: self-distillation replaces RL.
  • source-randopt-gaussian-noise-llms: Gaussian noise + ensembling matches GRPO.
  • source-intuitor-reasoning-without-rewards: model confidence as the only reward.
  • source-distribution-sharpening-power-sampling: many "RL gains" reproducible at inference time.

DPO is the canonical exhibit. It replaced an RL problem with a regression and lost nothing.
