The Llama 3 Herd of Models

source created 2026-05-06 updated 2026-05-06 model-training llama meta technical-report frontier-model open-weights long-context

Authors: Meta (Grattafiori et al., ~559 contributors) Date: July 2024 (arXiv 2407.21783)

Summary

Meta's 92-page technical report for the Llama 3 family — 8B, 70B, and 405B dense transformers trained on 15T tokens. The 405B variant is the first open-weight model to credibly contest GPT-4 across most benchmarks. The report is unusually detailed about training infrastructure, data curation, and post-training, and became the default reference for "how to train a frontier dense model" in 2024–2025.

Key Claims

Architecture

  • Dense decoder-only transformer (no MoE) — a deliberate bet on simplicity and inference predictability.
  • GQA (grouped-query attention) across all sizes; 128k context via two-stage long-context training (8k → 128k with RoPE base scaling).
  • 128k vocab BPE tokenizer, ~15% more efficient than Llama 2's.

Pre-training

  • 15T tokens, ~10× Llama 2's budget. Heavy emphasis on data curation: classifier-based filtering, deduplication, domain-specific quality models.
  • Annealing on high-quality data in late training stages — the technique source-frontier-model-training later canonised as standard.
  • Trained on up to 16k H100 GPUs simultaneously for the 405B run; the report's infrastructure section is a treasure trove of failure-mode statistics (~419 unexpected interruptions over 54 days).

Post-training

  • Classical SFT → rejection sampling → DPO pipeline (no PPO). Multiple rounds of post-training.
  • Tool use (search, code interpreter, custom functions) as a first-class capability, trained via SFT on synthetic trajectories.
  • Multilinguality across 8 supported languages; code/math focus via specialised data and synthetic generation.

Multimodal (compositional)

  • Vision and speech adapters trained on top of frozen Llama 3 — a deliberately modular approach rather than the natively-multimodal route.
  • The compositional design lets the language backbone be reused unchanged.

Performance

  • 405B claimed comparable to GPT-4 (OpenAI) and Claude 3.5 Sonnet on most benchmarks at release.

Why it matters

  • The clearest single document showing what frontier dense training actually looks like end-to-end. Anyone reproducing a frontier model uses this report as a checklist.
  • Made open-weights competitive with closed frontier at scale, kicking off the post-2024 wave of strong open releases (DeepSeek-V3, Qwen2.5, etc.).
  • The compositional multimodal choice contrasts with GPT-4's natively multimodal design and with concept-jepa's representation-first vision — three different bets on how vision should attach.
