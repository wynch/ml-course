Vision-Language-Action Models (VLA) / Robot Foundation Models

concept created 2026-05-06 updated 2026-05-06 robotics vla foundation-models embodied-ai world-models

Foundation models for robotic control that take vision + language as input and emit actions. Distinct from world-model approaches (which predict state evolution, then plan against the model) — VLAs are typically direct policies trained on demonstration data.

In the wiki

VLA lineage

  • source-rt-2 — Brohan 2023 (Google): co-fine-tunes a VLM with robot trajectory data; emergent generalisation to novel objects/scenes/skills.
  • source-openvla — Kim 2024: open VLA; LLaMA 2 + SigLIP + DINOv2 backbone; OpenX-Embodiment dataset; outperforms RT-2-X with 7B params.
  • source-pi-zero — Black 2024 (Physical Intelligence): VLA foundation model with flow-matching action head; broad embodiment coverage; 6× speedup at high control frequencies.

Predictive alternative (JEPA-as-world-model)

  • source-vjepa-family — V-JEPA 2-AC: action-conditioned predictor; zero-shot Droid robot pick-and-place via image-goal MPC in latent space, no task-specific training. Predictive substrate rather than direct policy.

Symbolic precursor

  • source-code-as-policies — older pattern: LLM writes Python policies, interpreter runs. The neural-symbolic ancestor of modern VLAs.

Design space

The four sources above cover four answers to "what's the right action representation?":

The wiki's organising tension here:

  • Direct policy (RT-2, OpenVLA, π0) vs predict-then-plan (concept-world-models via DreamerV3, V-JEPA-2-AC) — the same split that runs through concept-active-inference and concept-world-models.

Recurring patterns

  1. Co-fine-tune a VLM: RT-2's recipe (joint training on web vision-language data + robot trajectories) generalises across all three direct-VLA models.
  2. Backbone choice: CLIP-style (source-clip-radford) vs DINO-style (source-dino-lineage) — OpenVLA uses both via SigLIP+DINOv2 fusion.
  3. Action chunking: emit multiple steps per forward pass for higher control frequency. π0 makes this central via flow matching.
  4. Embodiment generalisation: train on the OpenX-Embodiment cross-robot dataset to learn shared structure.

Connections

  • concept-world-models — VLAs are the policy half; world models are the model half. V-JEPA 2-AC bridges by being a predictor used as a policy.
  • concept-multimodal — VLAs are multimodal-LLMs with action heads grafted on
  • concept-active-inference — predict-then-plan parallel; AIF agents are also direct controllers when generative model is hand-tuned
  • concept-jepa — V-JEPA 2-AC is the predictive-VLA hybrid
  • concept-ai-agents — physical agents share scaffolding patterns with software agents

Open questions

  • Tokens vs flow: π0's flow-matching action head is faster than tokenised autoregression; will tokenised actions remain dominant or get displaced?
  • Direct policy vs world model: V-JEPA 2-AC's zero-shot transfer is striking — does the predictive paradigm scale better than direct VLA training as data grows?
  • Embodiment transfer: OpenX-Embodiment shows shared structure, but real cross-robot transfer is still rough. Where's the ceiling?
  • Long-horizon vs reactive: VLAs are good at reactive control; long-horizon plans currently still need an outer loop (LLM planner, MCTS). When do VLAs grow planning natively?
