Recursive Language Models (RLMs)

source created 2026-04-05 updated 2026-09-25 llm-architecture context-window recursive long-context

Authors: Alex L. Zhang, Tim Kraska, Omar Khattab Date: 2025-12-31 (revised 2026-01-28) arXiv: 2512.24601 Code: alexzhang13/rlm Via: @akshay_pachaar on X

Summary

Introduces a novel inference approach enabling language models to handle extremely long inputs by decomposing them into manageable segments. The model can programmatically examine, decompose, and recursively call itself over snippets of the prompt, processing inputs up to two orders of magnitude beyond model context windows.

Key Ideas

  • Recursive self-calling: model decomposes long inputs and calls itself on segments
  • Handles inputs 100x beyond native context window
  • RLM-Qwen3-8B achieves ~28% improvement over base model
  • Comparable to GPT-5 on certain extended-context tasks
  • Addresses "context rot" — performance degradation with long contexts

Follow-up: training through the RLM harness (2026)

source-zhang-harness-blog trains the RLM root model with RL (Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507, prime-rl). Trained only on short splits, it improves on splits 8–32× longer, where a plain Transformer trained the same way stays flat (raw/web-2026-alexzhang-harness-compositional.md, §"RLMs can train on short tasks and generalize to longer, unseen tasks"). The post names the two parts that make this work: context offloading and programmatic sub-agent calls (same raw file, §"Equivalence classes over trajectories induced by the RLM harness").
