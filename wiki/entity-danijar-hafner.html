Danijar Hafner

entity created 2026-05-07 updated 2026-05-07 researcher author google-deepmind world-models model-based-rl dreamer rssm

Model-based RL researcher; lead of the Dreamer line of world models at Google / Google DeepMind (with stretches at U. Toronto and the Vector Institute). In this wiki he is the single most prolific world-models author — he is first author on the entire PlaNet → Dreamer → DreamerV3 lineage that the wiki uses as the canonical trajectory of latent-imagination model-based RL.

Sources in the wiki

  • source-planet-latent-dynamics — first author (Hafner, Lillicrap, Fischer, Villegas, Ha, Lee, Davidson, Nov 2018, ICML 2019). Introduces the RSSM (mixed deterministic + stochastic latent transition) and drops Ha & Schmidhuber's separate controller — actions chosen by online planning in the latent model. The bridge from V+M+C to the Dreamer family.
  • source-dreamer-v1 — first author (Hafner, Lillicrap, Ba, Norouzi, arXiv 1912.01603, ICLR 2020). Adds actor-critic learning by latent imagination: the policy and value heads are trained on rollouts inside the world model itself, not the environment. Defines the Dreamer template.
  • source-dreamerv3 — first author (Hafner, Pasukonis, Ba, Lillicrap, arXiv 2301.04104, published in Nature 2025). A single set of hyperparameters across 150+ tasks, plus the symlog-prediction / two-hot trick stack that makes the recipe domain-agnostic. The Minecraft-from-scratch result that closed the "does latent imagination really generalise?" question.

Themes

A four-paper arc (Ha & Schmidhuber → PlaNet → Dreamer → DreamerV3) where Hafner is first author for three of four entries. The through-line is make Ha & Schmidhuber's V+M+C recipe actually scale: replace the separate controller with planning (PlaNet), then with actor-critic over latent rollouts (Dreamer), then with a single normalisation/loss recipe that generalises across hundreds of tasks (DreamerV3). The recurring architectural primitive is the RSSM — a mixed deterministic-stochastic recurrent latent state — which the entire downstream world-models literature (source-tdmpc2, JEPA-as-world-model claims) implicitly benchmarks against.
