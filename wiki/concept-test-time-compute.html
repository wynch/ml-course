Test-Time Compute

concept created 2026-04-05 updated 2026-10-03 inference reasoning compute-scaling thinking

Spending more computation at inference time to improve model outputs — as opposed to training-time scaling. Includes thinking tokens, iterative refinement, search, and scaffolding.

Approaches in the Wiki

Foundational

  • source-chain-of-thought-foundations — Wei 2022: the founding paper. Few-shot CoT prompting; GSM8K 17.9% → 56.9%; emergent with scale.
  • source-chain-of-thought-foundations — Wang 2022: sample N CoTs + majority vote; GSM8K → 74.4%. The sampling-as-amplification ancestor of PDR-style methods.

Search & deliberation

  • source-tree-of-thoughts — Yao 2023: BFS/DFS over self-evaluated thoughts; Game of 24 4% → 74%.
  • source-reflexion-verbal-rl — Shinn 2023: verbal RL via episodic memory; 91% pass@1 HumanEval.
  • source-self-refine — Madaan 2023: iterative self-feedback loop, no extra training; +20% across 7 tasks.
  • source-snell-test-time-compute-scaling — Snell 2024: scaling test-time compute can be more compute-efficient than scaling parameters.

Modern scaffolds

  • source-pdr-rethinking-thinking-tokens — Parallel-Distill-Refine: generate diverse drafts, distill into bounded workspace, refine. +11% on AIME 2024.
  • source-ttt-kv-binding-linear-attention — reveals TTT with KV binding is equivalent to linear attention, unifying two paradigms.
  • source-language-models-as-scaffolds — thesis that scaffolding (recursive calls, REPL environments) unlocks latent model capability without retraining.
  • source-recursive-language-models — recursive self-calling enables 100x context extension at inference time.
  • source-distribution-sharpening-power-sampling — inference-time power sampling matches GRPO post-training.
  • source-openai-o1-system-card — o1's hidden CoT + RL-trained reasoning; the productionised version of test-time compute scaling.

Latent thoughts

Hub: concept-latent-reasoning (what the tokens are, what supervises them, what is measured).

  • source-pause-tokens — the simplest form: append M copies of one learned <pause> token and read the answer after the last one. Gains (SQuAD +18% exact match on a 1B model) appear only if the model also saw pauses in pretraining; pauses added at finetuning alone give smaller, mixed gains. Pauses widen each layer; they add no sequential steps (raw/arxiv-2310-02226-think-before-you-speak-pause-tokens.md, §4.3, §6, Appendix I.2).
  • source-coconut-continuous-latent-reasoning — the last hidden state fed back as the next input ("continuous thought"), trained by replacing written reasoning steps one stage at a time. GSM8k 34.1% (written CoT 42.9%) with a third of the tokens; ProsQA 97.0% vs 77.5%. Without the staged curriculum it falls to no-CoT level (raw/arxiv-2412-06769-coconut-reason-in-continuous-latent-space.md, §5.3, Table 1).
  • source-soft-tokens-hard-truths — continuous chains of hundreds of tokens trained by RL on the answer alone; the best recipe trains soft and then thinks in normal words at test time (raw/arxiv-2509-19170-soft-tokens-hard-truths.md, §1, §4).
  • source-continuous-cot-two-regimes — the 2026 reality check: on realistic MATH chains both ways of training compressed continuous chains reach about a third of written-CoT accuracy (Qwen 1.5B: 12-15% vs 52.8%) (raw/arxiv-2607-16972-continuous-cot-tale-of-two-regimes.md, Table 5).
  • source-abstract-token-curriculum — continuous thought tokens (the model's own hidden states fed back) trained from answer supervision alone; each extra thought is one more forward pass at inference (raw/arxiv-2609-19717-learn-your-own-thoughts-abstract-token-curriculum.md, §2, §6.3).
  • source-recurrent-depth-latent-reasoning — depth instead of tokens: a 4-layer block looped r times, trained with random r and no reasoning data. GSM8k CoT (flexible) 0.00 at r = 1, 42.08 at r = 32; easy tasks saturate after about 8 loops (raw/arxiv-2502-05171-recurrent-depth-latent-reasoning.md, Table 4, §5.2).
  • source-loopcd-decoding-looped-transformers — a decoding rule for looped models: read the first loop's guess as a weak model and push the last loop's guess away from it. No training. Ouro-2.6B-Thinking AIME 2024 pass@1 61.88 → 73.33; at half the loops it matches or beats full depth on multiple choice, for 22.5% to 48.2% fewer forward FLOPs, counted, not timed (raw/arxiv-2610-02185-decoding-looped-transformers-better-for-almost.md, §4.1, §4.3, Appendix D.1).
  • source-continuous-thought-machine — internal ticks inside the network as the thinking axis.

Denoising steps

  • source-varunneal-diffusion-will-be-everywhere — an essay arguing text diffusion is a better test-time axis than loops: more denoising steps land in states seen in training, and finished steps leave nothing in the KV cache. As reported from Google DeepMind's DiffusionGemma report, its GPQA-Diamond and LiveCodeBench average climbs from about 9 to 59 with more denoising steps (raw/web-2026-varunneal-diffusion-everywhere.md, "Test-time scaling"). Second-hand; the wiki holds no copy of that report.

The bill arrives (2026)

  • source-token-purchasing-power-bigspin — extended "thinking" (1% → 18% of session tokens) is a prime driver of falling per-token output ("tokenflation"): the capability lever, priced. See concept-inference-economics.
  • source-text-optimization-yoonho proposes the mirror axis: update-time compute — compute spent converting one experience into a durable (text-layer) behavioral update, rather than compute spent per input.

Key Insight

Test-time compute and architectural choices may be two sides of the same coin — source-ttt-kv-binding-linear-attention shows TTT is secretly linear attention. The boundary between "inference technique" and "architecture" is blurring.

Relation to Training

  • source-llm-reasoning-skeptics — mitigations for reasoning failures often involve test-time techniques.
  • source-randopt-gaussian-noise-llms — provocatively, even simple noise + ensembling at test time can match trained RL.
