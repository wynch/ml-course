Dreamer V1 — Learning Behaviors by Latent Imagination

source created 2026-05-06 updated 2026-05-06 world-models model-based-rl actor-critic latent-imagination hafner 2019

Danijar Hafner, Timothy Lillicrap, Jimmy Ba, Mohammad Norouzi — arXiv 1912.01603, Dec 2019 (ICLR 2020).

The pivot from PlaNet's online search to policies learned inside the dream. Same RSSM world model, same imagination rollouts — but now an actor and a critic are trained to maximise long-horizon return entirely in latent space, with value gradients propagated analytically through the differentiable dynamics.

Three concurrent training loops

  1. World-model learning: RSSM trained on real experience to predict next latent, reward, and (optionally) image — same as PlaNet.
  2. Behaviour learning in imagination: roll the actor out for ~15 latent steps starting from each replay state. The critic regresses on a TD(λ)-style return estimate over imagined trajectories. The actor is updated by backpropagating the value through the differentiable dynamics and reparameterised stochastic states — i.e. a model-based reparameterised policy gradient. This is the key shift: the gradient of expected return wrt actor parameters flows through the world model's dynamics, not through environment samples.
  3. Environment interaction: the actor is deployed; new transitions enter the replay buffer.

Why this matters vs PlaNet

CEM at every step is expensive and re-plans from scratch. A learned policy amortises planning into network weights and can act in real-time. The compute spent on imagination becomes "training the policy", not "deciding the next move". This is the same amortisation logic that connects MCTS to AlphaZero.

Results

  • Beats PlaNet and prior model-free SOTA (D4PG, A3C, SLAC) on 20 DeepMind Control Suite tasks from pixels.
  • Substantially faster wall-clock and sample-efficient.
  • Sets the template the Dreamer family will scale: RSSM + actor-critic in imagination + replay.

Relevance

V1 establishes the recipe extended through DreamerV3: same skeleton, more domains, more robustness tricks. Conceptually, it makes explicit the trade-off between planning (PlaNet, concept-active-inference motor inference, RAP MCTS) and amortised policy (Dreamer, AlphaZero networks, CoALA-style standing skill libraries). Ties to concept-representation-learning — the latent space must be both reconstructive (so V can supervise it) and dynamics-predictable (so M can generalise rollouts).
