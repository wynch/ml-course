Dreamer 4: Training Agents Inside of Scalable World Models

Summary

Dreamer 4 learns a world model from video, then trains a policy by reinforcement learning on rollouts the model imagines, with no contact with the real environment (R, §3, Algorithm 1). It is the fourth Dreamer, and the first not built on the RSSM, the small recurrent latent model of source-dreamerv3: the authors say the RSSM is fast but "difficult to scale to diverse data distributions" (R, Appendix F).

The model has two parts, both one kind of transformer with attention causal in time (R, §3.4):

  • a tokenizer that compresses each frame into a few continuous vectors, trained as a masked autoencoder (R, §3.1);
  • a dynamics model that predicts the next frame's vectors from past vectors and actions. It is a diffusion model trained with shortcut forcing: it learns to take big denoising steps, so it needs 4 network passes per frame instead of 64, and it predicts clean vectors rather than velocities, which keeps errors from piling up over long rollouts (R, §2, §3.2).

Training runs in three phases: pretrain on video (with actions where present), fine-tune task-conditioned policy and reward heads by imitation, then improve the policy by RL in imagination with a KL pull towards the imitation policy (R, §3.3, Eq. 11).

Key results

  • Diamonds from offline data. On Minecraft with only the 2,541-hour VPT contractor dataset (raw pixels, mouse and keyboard, 60-minute episodes), Dreamer 4 is the first agent to obtain diamonds without playing the game: 0.7% of episodes; 29% reach an iron pickaxe and over 90% a stone pickaxe (R, §4.1, Figure 3). OpenAI's VPT offline agent gets as far as sticks (53%) after training on 270K hours of labelled web video, about 100× more data (R, §4.1, Table 3).
  • Against a VLA. A Gemma 3 vision-language model fine-tuned as a policy on the same data (the OpenVLA and π0.5 recipe) reaches the iron pickaxe 11% of the time; Dreamer 4 nearly triples that. Imitation on the world model's own features also beats imitation on Gemma 3's (R, §4.1, Figures 3–4).
  • Real time on one GPU. The 2B-parameter model (400M tokenizer, 1.6B dynamics) runs at 21 frames per second on one H100 at 640×360 with 9.6 s of context, against 0.8–1.6 s for earlier Minecraft world models (R, Table 1). Training used 256 to 1,024 TPU-v5p chips (R, §4).
  • Human play inside the model. People completed 14 of 16 tasks (build a wall, ride a boat, use a furnace) inside Dreamer 4, 5 of 16 inside the large Oasis model and none inside Lucid-v1 (R, §4.2, Table 1).
  • Little action data needed. With actions for only 100 of 2,541 hours, action-conditioned generation reaches 85% of the PSNR and 100% of the SSIM of training with all actions, scaled between no actions and all actions. Trained with actions only in the Overworld, it reaches 76% of the PSNR and 80% of the SSIM in the Nether and End, seen only as unlabelled video (R, §4.3, Figure 7).
  • Design ablation. From a plain diffusion-forcing transformer (FVD 306, 0.8 frames per second) to the final model (FVD 57, 21.4 frames per second), each change is measured; predicting clean vectors with the clean-vector loss cut FVD from 326 to 151 (R, §4.4, Table 2).
  • A robotics version, trained on 180 hours of the SOAR dataset, lets people steer an imagined robot arm (R, §4.2, Figure 6, Appendix A).

Caveats

  • Diamonds in 0.7% of episodes is a first, not mastery. The authors call the model "far from a full clone of the game": memory is short and inventory items blur or change (R, §6).
  • The RL updates only the policy and value heads; the transformer stays frozen (R, §3.3).
  • Rewards and the 20 tasks come from event labels in the VPT data, and a fixed prompt sequence walks the agent through sub-tasks at test time (R, §4.1).
  • "Real time on one GPU" is inference only. Training is TPU-pod scale (R, §4).
  • source-genie-3 is not compared: its action space is too narrow for Minecraft (R, §4.2). Oasis (large) speed is the authors' estimate from public information (R, Table 1).
  • The arXiv HTML of v1 stops inside §4.2; the raw file's later sections come from PDF text, so tables there are one cell per line.
