Group-Evolving Agents (GEA)

source created 2026-04-05 updated 2026-09-26 ai-agents self-improvement evolutionary swe-bench coding-agents

Authors: Zhaotian Weng, Antonis Antoniades, Deepak Nathani, Zhen Zhang, Xiao Pu, Xin Eric Wang Date: 2026-02-04 arXiv: 2602.04837

Summary

Introduces GEA, a paradigm enabling open-ended self-improvement by treating agent groups as evolutionary units with shared experiences. Achieves 71.0% on SWE-bench Verified (vs. 56.7%) and 88.3% on Polyglot (vs. 68.3%) compared to existing self-evolving approaches.

Key Ideas

  • Agent groups as evolutionary units — collective improvement rather than individual
  • Shared experience enables faster convergence (~1.4 iterations vs 5 for alternatives)
  • Strong SWE-bench results suggest practical applicability to real coding tasks
  • Open-ended: no predefined stopping criterion
