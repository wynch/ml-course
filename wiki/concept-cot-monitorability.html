Chain-of-Thought Monitorability

concept created 2026-10-20 updated 2026-10-20 chain-of-thought monitoring faithfulness latent-reasoning alignment hub

A reasoning model writes its steps as text before it answers. If those steps show what actually drove the answer, a person or a second model can read them and catch bad intent or bad reasoning. Monitorability is how far that holds. Korbak et al. call it "a new and fragile opportunity" (arXiv 2507.11473; not in raw).

Two things must both be true for a chain to be monitorable (raw/arxiv-2510-27378-measuring-chain-of-thought-monitorability-through.md, pp. 2–4; source-measuring-cot-monitorability):

  • Faithful: the text reflects what the model used. The paper tests this by planting a cue in the prompt and asking a judge model whether the chain admits using it.
  • Verbose enough: the text names every factor needed to solve the task. A chain can be faithful and still leave out the step that mattered.

The score averages the two, graded by QwQ-32B. Reasoning models score higher than their instruction-tuned versions on both; scores differ sharply across model families; GPQA-Diamond gives the lowest, BBH the highest (same file, abstract, pp. 3–4).

Latent reasoning removes the text

Several lines in the wiki move reasoning out of words and into hidden vectors (concept-latent-reasoning). Each then needs another way to see what happened:

MethodHow it reads its hidden stepsSource
Coconutforces the model to write words after a continuous thought; the first decoded thought often names an intermediate valueraw/arxiv-2412-06769-coconut-reason-in-continuous-latent-space.md, §4.3, §5.3 Figure 9
Abstract Token Curriculumreplaces thoughts with noise and watches the answer: corrupting the last thought breaks it, earlier ones do notraw/arxiv-2609-19717-learn-your-own-thoughts-abstract-token-curriculum.md, §6.4
Looped (recurrent-depth) modelplots the hidden state's path across loopsraw/arxiv-2502-05171-recurrent-depth-latent-reasoning.md, §7

None of these is a monitor a reviewer could run on a live answer. On realistic MATH chains, the continuous methods also reach only about a third of written chain-of-thought's accuracy (raw/arxiv-2607-16972-continuous-cot-tale-of-two-regimes.md, §1, Figure 1 caption, Table 5; source-continuous-cot-two-regimes), so for now the trade costs accuracy as well as visibility.

Other routes to a readout

  • Train the model to explain its own computation in words, and check the words against interpretability tools (source-self-explanation-transluce; arXiv 2511.08579; not in raw).
  • Read internal state directly: source-anthropic-global-workspace, source-anthropic-introspective-awareness, concept-activation-steering.
  • OpenAI's "Monitoring Monitorability" (Guan et al., arXiv 2512.18311; not in raw) is the companion measure; the wiki has no page for it.

Open questions

  • Does RL on outcomes keep chains faithful, or teach the model to leave things out? No source here measures a chain's monitorability before and after RL.
  • If latent reasoning closes its accuracy gap, what replaces reading the chain?
  • The judge in the measuring paper is itself a model; how often it misses an admitted cue is not reported here.
