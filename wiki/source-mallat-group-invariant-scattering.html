Group Invariant Scattering

source created 2026-05-14 updated 2026-05-14 wavelets scattering-transform representation-learning invariance stability stephane-mallat

Stéphane Mallat — Communications on Pure and Applied Mathematics / arXiv 2011, revised 2012

This is the mathematical anchor for Mallat's scattering program. It constructs operators that are invariant to translations and stable to deformations by cascading wavelet transforms with modulus nonlinearities and local integration.

Main Claim

Scattering provides a way to build invariance without throwing away all high-frequency information. The representation is not learned from examples. It is induced by a structured signal-processing architecture.

Why It Matters

For the wiki's current edge-learning question, this paper provides the strongest theoretical reason to treat wavelets as a perceptual substrate:

  • fixed filters can build useful invariances
  • stability to small deformation is a designed property
  • higher-order coefficients encode statistics beyond a power spectrum
  • the architecture already looks like a deep network, but with mathematical filters

Caveat

The paper proves and motivates stability/invariance properties, but it does not settle the engineering question of whether a scattering front-end beats small learned models under edge-device constraints. That must be tested by experiment-wavelet-substrate-network.
