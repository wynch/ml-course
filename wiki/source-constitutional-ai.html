Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback

source created 2026-05-06 updated 2026-05-06 rlhf alignment constitutional-ai rlaif anthropic foundational 2022

Bai, Kadavath, Kundu, Askell, Kernion, Jones, Chen, Goldie, Mirhoseini, McKinnon et al. — arXiv 2212.08073, Dec 2022 (entity-anthropic)

Replaces the human harmlessness preference labels of source-instructgpt-rlhf with AI-generated critiques and revisions guided by a written "constitution" of principles. Result: a helpful-and-harmless assistant trained with dramatically less human red-teaming, while remaining willing to engage with sensitive topics rather than refusing reflexively. The recipe behind early Claude.

Method (CAI = SL-CAI + RL-AIF)

  1. Supervised CAI:
    • Generate harmful/borderline responses from a helpful-only model.
    • Use the same model to critique its response against a constitutional principle.
    • Use the model to revise its response.
    • Fine-tune on the (prompt, revised response) pairs.
  2. RL from AI Feedback (RLAIF):
    • For each prompt, the model produces two candidate responses.
    • An LLM judge (instructed by the constitution) picks the better one — yielding a synthetic preference dataset.
    • Train a preference/reward model on these AI-generated preferences.
    • PPO-fine-tune the policy against that reward model (mirroring the InstructGPT third stage).

The "constitution" is a list of natural-language principles (helpfulness, honesty, avoiding harm, respecting autonomy, etc.) — including concepts drawn from the UN Declaration of Human Rights.

Key results

  • Reaches helpfulness comparable to an RLHF model with substantially less human harmlessness data.
  • Maintains engagement: significantly fewer evasive refusals than a pure-RLHF baseline at the same harm level.
  • Demonstrates a scalable oversight primitive: AI provides the labels that humans previously did.
  • Constitution is transparent and editable — alignment becomes (partly) a documentation artefact.

Why it matters

Constitutional AI sits at three intersections this wiki cares about.

  1. concept-rlhf without humans-in-the-loop for harmlessness. The labour bottleneck of source-instructgpt-rlhf was red-teaming and preference labelling; CAI shows large parts of that can be modelled by the model itself given a written spec. Analogous to self-distillation patterns in concept-self-improvement and concept-rlhf-alternatives.
  2. concept-ai-alignment as documentation. Encoding values as a natural-language constitution — versionable, debatable, auditable — makes alignment a thing you can read, not just a black-box reward signal. Foreshadows the structured-spec direction in source-claude-code-sandboxing and the steering literature (source-conditional-activation-steering-cast, source-steering-generalization-alignment).
  3. entity-anthropic's house style. The CAI methodology + interpretability program (source-sae-scaling-trilogy, source-circuit-tracing-methods-anthropic, source-lindsey-2025-biology-of-llm) form a coherent research stance: alignment via legible constraints + legible internals. Reading CAI alongside the interpretability stack shows the same bet from the policy side.

The open question is whether AI-generated critiques inherit/amplify the model's existing biases — see source-what-llms-think-unprompted for evidence that base models have strong implicit defaults the constitution must override rather than rely on.
