Training Language Models to Follow Instructions with Human Feedback (InstructGPT)

source created 2026-05-06 updated 2026-05-06 rlhf alignment instruction-tuning foundational instructgpt 2022

Ouyang, Wu, Jiang, Almeida, Wainwright, Mishkin, Zhang, Agarwal, Slama, Ray, Schulman et al. — arXiv 2203.02155, Mar 2022 (OpenAI)

The canonical recipe for turning a raw language model into a useful assistant: the three-stage RLHF pipeline (SFT → reward model → PPO). InstructGPT-1.3B is preferred to GPT-3-175B by human raters despite being >100× smaller. The blueprint behind ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and effectively every modern aligned LLM.

Method (the canonical RLHF recipe)

  1. Supervised fine-tuning (SFT): human contractors write demonstrations of desired behaviour for prompts; fine-tune GPT-3 on (prompt, demonstration) pairs.
  2. Reward model (RM): collect human pairwise preferences over model completions; train a scalar reward model RM_θ via a Bradley–Terry / pairwise-logistic loss.
  3. PPO with KL penalty: fine-tune SFT policy with PPO against RM, regularised by KL(policy || SFT) to prevent reward-hacking and capability collapse.

About 13K SFT prompts and 33K RM-comparison prompts from real OpenAI API users + labellers.

Key numbers

  • 1.3B InstructGPT > 175B GPT-3 in human preference on the API distribution.
  • Toxicity (RealToxicityPrompts) reduced; truthfulness (TruthfulQA) improved.
  • Modest alignment tax: small regressions on a few public NLP benchmarks, mostly recovered by mixing pretraining gradients into PPO ("PPO-ptx").
  • Generalises to held-out prompt categories not present in SFT data.

Why it matters

InstructGPT operationalised three claims that defined the post-2022 LLM era:

  1. Pretraining is not enough. A raw next-token predictor is not a usable assistant. Aligning to human intent requires a separate post-training stage. This is the foundation for concept-rlhf and the "post-training" half of source-frontier-model-training (SFT → preference optimisation → RL with verifiable rewards).
  2. Preference data > demonstration data, beyond a point. RM + RL provided a scalable signal because humans are better at judging than writing — a key pillar of concept-ai-alignment (steering through evaluation, not specification).
  3. Helpfulness can dominate scale. A small aligned model beating a giant base model showed that capability ≠ utility, redirecting investment toward alignment infrastructure.

The wiki's RLHF-adjacent pages frame this as the default that everything else compares to:

  • source-constitutional-ai keeps the structure but replaces human comparisons with model-generated critiques.
  • source-dpo-direct-preference-optimization eliminates the explicit RM and PPO loop entirely.
  • concept-rlhf-alternatives and source-distribution-sharpening-power-sampling question whether the RL step is doing what we think — possibly just sharpening latent capability already present in SFT.
