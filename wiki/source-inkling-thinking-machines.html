Inkling and the Tinker recipes (Thinking Machines Lab, 2026)

source created 2026-07-20 updated 2026-10-03 open-weights moe lora distillation continual-learning fine-tuning

Inkling (July 15, 2026) is Thinking Machines' first open-weights model: 975B-total / 41B-active MoE, multimodal, 1M context, Apache 2.0. It is positioned as the best open base for customization through Tinker, their hosted paid fine-tuning API. The launch demo had the model write its own training objectives and run its own fine-tuning job — a bounded showcase, not always-on learning. The model itself is far beyond laptop reach (600&nbsp;GB+ quantized).

What matters at small scale is the pair of research results the Tinker cookbook turns into recipes, both model-agnostic and open:

  • LoRA Without Regret. LoRA matches full fine-tuning when applied to all layers, with rank sized to the data's information content, batch under ~512, and learning rate ~10× the full-fine-tune optimum. Near-lossless for RL even at low rank.
  • On-Policy Distillation. The student samples its own rollouts; a teacher scores each token by reverse KL on log-probs. Dense signal: 7–10× fewer gradient steps and 50–100× less compute than RL to match performance. Demonstrated as a forgetting repair: after domain fine-tuning cut instruction-following 85%→79% (domain QA 18%→36%), distilling against the model's own pre-fine-tune checkpoint recovered IF to 83% while pushing domain QA to 41%.

Relevance

The two recipes are the whole weights-channel playbook of experiment-weights-vs-context: periodic LoRA updates plus sleep-time self-distillation against a frozen snapshot. The forgetting-repair curve is the M2 reproduction target there. Connects to concept-self-improvement (self-distillation as lever — see analysis-self-distillation-as-universal-lever) and source-lora-low-rank-adaptation. A different route to the same problem: source-local-support-learning prevents the damage instead of repairing it, by gating each adapter so it acts only on inputs like its training data (raw/arxiv-2610-02126-local-support-learning.md, §4). See concept-continual-learning. Caution: the numbers are company-reported blog results on Qwen3-8B/32B, not peer-reviewed.
