World Models (Ha & Schmidhuber 2018)

source created 2026-05-06 updated 2026-09-25 world-models reinforcement-learning generative-models mdn-rnn vae neural-architecture 2018

David Ha & Jürgen Schmidhuber — arXiv 1803.10122, March 2018 (NeurIPS 2018 as "Recurrent World Models Facilitate Policy Evolution").

The canonical modern world-models paper. Establishes a three-module decomposition that almost every subsequent world-model system can be read as a variation of.

The V + M + C architecture

  • V (Vision): a Variational Autoencoder compresses each raw frame into a small latent vector z_t. Pure perception, trained with reconstruction + KL.
  • M (Memory): a Mixture Density Network RNN (MDN-RNN) models the temporal dynamics — predicting p(z_{t+1} | z_t, a_t, h_t) as a mixture of Gaussians. The MDN matters: the world is multimodal, a single Gaussian collapses dynamics.
  • C (Controller): a tiny linear policy (a few hundred parameters) mapping [z_t, h_t] → a_t, trained with CMA-ES (evolution strategies) — explicitly chosen so that all the heavy capacity lives in V and M, not in the policy.

This separation is the load-bearing idea: representation and dynamics are learned by self-supervision; the policy is a featherweight read-out trained against return. It anticipates the modern decoupling of foundation model + thin task adapter.

Training in dreams

The headline result: once V and M are trained, the agent can be trained entirely inside the world model's hallucinated rollouts — the "dream" — and then deployed back to the real environment. They demonstrate this on the VizDoom Take Cover task: policies trained only inside the MDN-RNN's imagination transfer to the real game. A "temperature" knob on the MDN noise prevents the controller from exploiting model holes (early reward hacking of an internal model).

Domains

  • CarRacing-v0: first agent to solve the task (>900 average score).
  • VizDoom Take Cover: dream-trained policy beats real-environment baselines.

Relevance

V/M/C is a literal instance of the Neural Computer schema — perception, memory, and a thin runtime — predating it by years and grounded in pixels rather than tokens. It also shares deep structure with concept-active-inference: a generative model of dynamics + a thin policy choosing actions to minimize a cost under that model. The architectural memory ancestry runs through source-dnc-graves (DNC, also Schmidhuber-adjacent lineage via LSTM).

Earlier root in the same author's work: source-schmidhuber-compression-progress (2008-2009) casts an agent as a compressor of its history plus a reinforcement-learning controller rewarded for compression progress (raw/arxiv-0812-4360-driven-by-compression-progress.md, §1.2). V/M/C keeps that compressor-plus-controller split.

This paper is the trunk of concept-world-models; the Dreamer trilogy (source-planet-latent-dynamics, source-dreamer-v1, source-dreamerv3) is essentially "make M deeper, train C with policy gradients in latent space, scale".
