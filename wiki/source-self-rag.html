Self-RAG

source created 2026-05-07 updated 2026-05-07 retrieval rag self-reflection agents 2023

Asai, Wu, Wang, Sil, Hajishirzi — arXiv 2310.11511, Oct 2023 (U. Washington / IBM Research)

The paper that turned RAG from a fixed pipeline ("retrieve k passages, concatenate, generate") into a learned policy with self-reflection. Self-RAG trains a single LM to emit special reflection tokens that decide (a) whether retrieval is needed at all, (b) whether each retrieved passage is relevant, (c) whether the generated answer is supported by it, and (d) how useful the answer is. The model performs RAG on itself, adaptively.

Method

Four families of reflection tokens added to the LM vocabulary:

  • [Retrieve] (yes / no / continue): does the next segment require retrieval?
  • [IsRel] (relevant / irrelevant): is a retrieved passage useful?
  • [IsSup] (fully / partially / not supported): does the generation cite the passage faithfully?
  • [IsUse] (1–5): overall utility of the response.

Training pipeline:

  1. Critic LM (GPT-4 distillation) labels training data with reflection tokens.
  2. Generator LM (Llama 2 7B/13B) supervised-fine-tuned on the augmented sequences — token loss treats reflection tokens just like any other.
  3. At inference, the model can self-control retrieval: emit [Retrieve=yes], fetch passages, score each with [IsRel], generate, self-assess with [IsSup]/[IsUse]. A beam-search-style decode picks the trajectory with the highest combined score.

Key numbers

  • Outperforms ChatGPT and Llama2-chat on open-domain QA (PopQA, TriviaQA), fact verification (FEVER), long-form generation (ASQA) — at 7B/13B parameters.
  • On long-form generation, citation precision and recall improve substantially vs standard RAG: the [IsSup] token forces grounded continuations.
  • Adaptive retrieval saves compute: short factual queries trigger one retrieval; long compositional answers trigger several at the right boundaries.

Why it matters

Self-RAG operationalises three trends the wiki tracks:

  1. Retrieval as a policy, not a step. Where source-dpr-dense-passage-retrieval and source-retro-deepmind retrieve unconditionally, Self-RAG asks "should I?" before each segment. This is the natural connection to source-react-reasoning-acting / source-toolformer-self-taught-tools — retrieval is just another tool with a learned invocation policy.
  2. Generation-time critique as alignment. The [IsSup] / [IsUse] tokens are a poor man's critic model integrated into the policy itself. This is a recurring pattern: source-reflexion-verbal-rl (verbal self-critique), source-self-refine (iterative refinement), source-measuring-cot-monitorability (CoT as monitorable substrate). Self-RAG shows you can train the critic into the generator.
  3. Citations as a first-class objective. The [IsSup] head is essentially a built-in faithfulness check — close to what production assistants (Perplexity, Anthropic Claude) now post-hoc verify with separate citation models.

Self-RAG also closes the loop with concept-rlhf-alternatives: the reflection tokens are dense supervised signal that replaces the sparse preference labels DPO/RLHF rely on. You don't need a separate reward model when every span has its own [IsSup] label.

The paper's main limitation: relies on GPT-4 to bootstrap the critic — a teacher / student loop in the source-hinton-knowledge-distillation tradition rather than from scratch.
