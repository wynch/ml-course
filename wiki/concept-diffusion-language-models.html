Diffusion Language Models

concept created 2026-05-06 updated 2026-10-03 language-models diffusion generation alternatives-to-autoregressive

Generating text via diffusion-style processes rather than autoregressive next-token prediction. Two main strands: discrete diffusion (perturbation in the categorical token space) and continuous diffusion (flow over embeddings or one-hot vectors).

In the wiki

  • source-flow-map-language-models — FMLM: continuous flows over one-hot embeddings; one-step generation matches 8-step discrete diffusion (arXiv 2602.16813).
  • source-varunneal-diffusion-will-be-everywhere — a 2026 essay: block diffusion trades extra arithmetic for fewer memory reads, scales at test time with more denoising steps, and suits RL; converting a finished autoregressive model is now cheap (raw/web-2026-varunneal-diffusion-everywhere.md, Overview).
  • source-unified-latents-diffusion — jointly trains encoder + diffusion prior + diffusion decoder via noise-level alignment (DeepMind, arXiv 2602.17270). Vision-focused but the joint-training framing applies to text.

Themes

Continuous vs discrete

source-flow-map-language-models argues continuous formulations preserve geometric properties (simplex constraints) that discrete methods can't exploit. The flow-map trick makes one-step generation feasible — sidestepping the multi-step inference cost that's the standard objection to diffusion for text.

Counterpoint to test-time compute

If one-step diffusion matches multi-step diffusion, the "more inference steps = more compute = better" thesis (concept-test-time-compute) gets weaker on this axis. Worth reconciling with source-distribution-sharpening-power-sampling's related claim about RL. Contradiction (partial), 2026-10-03: source-varunneal-diffusion-will-be-everywhere reports, from the DiffusionGemma technical report, a score that climbs from about 9 to 59 as the same model takes more denoising steps, and argues extra steps are true test-time scaling (raw/web-2026-varunneal-diffusion-everywhere.md, "Test-time scaling"). The paragraph above is the wiki's own inference, not a source's claim: FMLM compares a one-step flow map with a different, 8-step discrete model, which says nothing about whether more steps help one model. Both can hold. The wiki holds no raw copy of FMLM or of the DiffusionGemma report.

Open questions

  • Does diffusion buy anything for reasoning tasks specifically, or only for raw generation?
  • How does the latent-space training of source-unified-latents-diffusion interact with mech interp — are diffusion priors more interpretable than autoregressive ones?
  • Can diffusion LMs be retrofit from autoregressive checkpoints? (See concept-retrofitting.) One answer, second-hand: DiffusionGemma starts from the post-trained Gemma 4 checkpoint, uses under 10% of its training tokens, and trades "a moderate quality cost" for a 5-7× speedup (raw/web-2026-varunneal-diffusion-everywhere.md, "Why now?").
