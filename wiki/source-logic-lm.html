Logic-LM

source created 2026-04-13 updated 2026-04-13 neural-symbolic llm solver semantic-parsing

Pan, Albalak, Wang, Wang — EMNLP Findings 2023 (arXiv 2305.12295)

Makes the LLM act strictly as a translator: natural-language problems are converted into formal statements in Pyke (Prolog-style), first-order logic, constraint-satisfaction, or SAT, then an off-the-shelf solver produces the answer. A self-refinement loop feeds solver error messages back to the LLM to fix translation bugs. Across ProofWriter, FOLIO, LogicalDeduction, and AR-LSAT, Logic-LM beats vanilla prompting by ~39% and CoT by ~18%.

Neural↔symbolic bridge

The LLM is confined to a semantic-parsing role (NL → formal language); all inference happens inside a deterministic solver. The interface is the formal language itself; mistakes are surfaced by the solver as structured errors the LLM can understand. Division of labor: neural = ambiguity resolution, symbolic = inference. Sibling paper source-satlm uses SMT in place of Prolog/FOL.
