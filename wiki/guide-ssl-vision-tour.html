Self-Supervised Vision — Reading Tour

guide created 2026-05-07 updated 2026-05-07 guide reading-list self-supervised-learning vision tour

Six stops through the vision-SSL canon, ending at JEPA as a world-model substrate. Read in order; each stop reacts to the prior.

Stop 1 — Contrastive foundations (2019–20)

source-ssl-contrastive-foundations (grouped: SimCLR + MoCo + BYOL).

  • SimCLR: NT-Xent + heavy aug + projection head. Matches supervised ResNet-50 on ImageNet linear-eval at 76.5%.
  • MoCo: momentum encoder + queue — decouples negatives from batch size. First SSL > supervised on detection transfer.
  • BYOL: the surprise. No negatives. Predictor + EMA + stop-gradient. Reset the design space.

After this, "SSL ≈ supervised pretraining" stopped being controversial.

Stop 2 — The non-contrastive split

source-ssl-decorrelation (grouped: Barlow Twins + VICReg).

Anti-collapse via explicit decorrelation instead of BYOL's predictor asymmetry. Symmetric, no momentum, no stop-gradient. VICReg's per-branch V/I/C decomposition is what made it a building block in downstream JEPA work.

Stop 3 — Masked image modeling

source-masked-image-modeling (grouped: MAE + iBOT).

MAE: BERT-for-vision via pixel reconstruction, 75% mask, asymmetric encoder–decoder. iBOT: same idea but predict in EMA-teacher embedding space + DINO global loss.

The MAE/iBOT split foreshadows the JEPA debate: pixel target vs representation target.

Stop 4 — DINO lineage

source-dino-lineage (grouped: DINO + DINOv2 + DINOv3).

  • DINO (2021): self-distillation ViTs; emergent semantic segmentation in attention maps.
  • DINOv2 (2023): 1.1B ViT-g on LVD-142M curated dataset — SSL alone matches CLIP on most vision tasks.
  • DINOv3 (2025): ViT-7B + Gram anchoring — preserves dense-feature structure through long training.

The cleanest 4-year arc in the field: SSL recipe → data curation → dense-feature stability.

Stop 5 — JEPA programme

source-lecun-path-autonomous-intelligence (the position paper).

The architectural blueprint: 6 modules (configurator, perception, world model, cost, actor, short-term memory), with JEPA at the world-model core. Polemical edge: "stop reconstructing input space."

source-ijepa-self-supervised-learning — image-JEPA proof of concept. ViT-Huge/14 in <72h on 16 A100s.

Stop 6 — JEPA as world model

source-vjepa-family (grouped: V-JEPA + V-JEPA 2 + V-JEPA 2.1).

  • V-JEPA (2024): feature prediction on video tubelets; beats VideoMAE on motion-heavy benchmarks.
  • V-JEPA 2 (2025): 1B encoder, 1M+ hours of video; V-JEPA-2-AC deployed zero-shot on Franka arms for pick-and-place via image-goal MPC.
  • V-JEPA 2.1 (2026): Dense Predictive Loss, Deep Self-Supervision, Multi-Modal Tokenizers; +20pp real-robot grasping over V-JEPA 2.

source-vl-jepa — vision-language JEPA: predict text embeddings, not tokens. Beats CLIP/SigLIP2/Perception Encoder on 16 video benchmarks at 50% fewer trainable params.

Stop 7 (bonus) — Synthesis

source-ssl-cookbook-balestriero — practitioner survey across all four SSL families. Frames collapse-prevention as the unifying lens. Good capstone read.

Side branches and counterpoints

  • source-clip-radford — the contrastive image-text alternative DINOv2 challenged. Read after Stop 4 to see what SSL had to beat.
  • source-contrastive-learning-gaussian-representations — theoretical: why contrastive objectives push embeddings to Gaussian marginals. Adjacent to Stop 1.
  • source-wav2vec-2 — the audio cousin. Contrastive SSL on raw speech.
  • source-nonlinear-feature-representations — what features can / cannot live in a residual stream. Constrains all the above.

The big argument across this tour

By Stop 6, you've moved from "SSL learns features" to "SSL learns world models for robots." The through-line is the bet on representation prediction:

  1. Contrastive (Stop 1): predict that two views agree.
  2. Decorrelation (Stop 2): predict that dimensions decorrelate.
  3. MIM (Stop 3): predict pixels (MAE) or teacher embeddings (iBOT).
  4. DINO (Stop 4): predict teacher distribution.
  5. JEPA (Stops 5–6): predict embeddings of masked targets, then condition on actions.

Each step trades surface fidelity for semantic structure. V-JEPA 2-AC is what happens when that bet pays off as a control substrate.
