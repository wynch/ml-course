Condensed Synthesis

There are two arcs in this cluster:

  1. Release norms: GPT-4 and o1 define closed-report and system-card practice; Llama 3, Mixtral, DeepSeek-V3, and DeepSeek-R1 push back through open weights and more implementation detail.
  2. Capability production: pretraining scale and MoE efficiency produce strong bases; post-training and RL with verifiable rewards sharpen reasoning behavior; distillation packages that behavior into cheaper models.

For the wiki's reasoning question, source-deepseek-r1 is the anchor because it turns "reasoning model" from a closed-lab mystery into an open training recipe. For the scaling question, source-deepseek-v3 and source-mixtral-8x7b are the sparse-efficiency anchors.
