Mixtral 8x7B

source created 2026-05-06 updated 2026-05-06 moe sparsity mistral open-weights technical-report model-training

Authors: Jiang et al. (Mistral AI) Date: January 2024 (arXiv 2401.04088)

Summary

The first widely-deployed open-weights sparse Mixture-of-Experts language model. Mixtral 8x7B has 47B total parameters but activates only ~13B per token via top-2 routing over 8 expert FFNs per layer. Released under Apache 2.0, it became the reference open MoE for over a year and reignited interest in MoE as a practical deployment architecture (rather than a research curiosity).

Key Claims

Architecture

  • Mistral 7B base, with each FFN replaced by 8 expert FFNs + a learned router.
  • Top-2 routing: each token is routed to 2 of 8 experts; outputs combined by router weights.
  • 47B total params, ~13B active per token. Same inference cost as a dense 13B, same memory footprint as a dense 47B.
  • 32k context via Mistral's sliding-window attention.

Training & balancing

  • Standard load-balancing auxiliary loss on router entropy + load (the recipe later superseded by source-deepseek-v3's aux-loss-free approach).
  • No exotic FP8 or multi-token prediction tricks — a clean baseline MoE.

Performance

  • Matches or beats Llama 2 70B and GPT-3.5 across most benchmarks at release.
  • Particularly strong on math, code, and multilingual tasks.
  • The instruct variant (Mixtral 8x7B Instruct) was the first open chat model to credibly approach GPT-3.5 Turbo on human-rated evaluations.

Licensing

  • Apache 2.0 for both base and instruct. The licence choice was as influential as the architecture — it gave the open ecosystem a viable MoE substrate to fine-tune on.

Why it matters

  • Made MoE operationally credible outside the big labs. Before Mixtral, MoE was associated with Switch / GShard at Google and not much else in production.
  • Demonstrated the headline economic argument: pay for inference like a dense 13B, get quality close to a dense 70B.
  • Set up the lineage that DeepSeek-V3 then scaled by ~14× (8 experts → 256 fine-grained experts; 47B → 671B).
