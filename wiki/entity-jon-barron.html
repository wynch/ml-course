Sources in the wiki

  • source-barron-softmax-fix — first author, with Dan Gnanapragasam and Michael Tschannen (arXiv 2610.31642, 2026). Two rounding bugs in standard softmax and cross-entropy, Softmax Collapse and Softmax Cancellation, and a log-space rewrite that fixes both; it removes slingshot spikes in grokking and stops a student drifting from an identical teacher (raw/arxiv-2610-31642-a-fix-for-softmax-and-cross.md, Abstract).

Working method, as he reports it

In the thread he says Gemini models found the bug, ran most experiments and drafted the paper, which he then rewrote twice, once through the model in his own style and once by hand (raw/tweet-barron-softmax-fix.md, post 6). Unchecked; the paper does not say so.
