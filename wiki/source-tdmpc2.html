TD-MPC2 — Scalable, Robust World Models

source created 2026-05-06 updated 2026-05-06 world-models model-based-rl mpc continuous-control multi-task hansen 2023

Nicklas Hansen, Hao Su, Xiaolong Wang — arXiv 2310.16828, Oct 2023 (ICLR 2024).

The strongest contemporary alternative to the Dreamer family for continuous control. TD-MPC2 is the multi-task scaling of TD-MPC: a model-based agent that plans short trajectories with MPPI (Model Predictive Path Integral) over a learned latent dynamics model, with a learned terminal value function bootstrapping beyond the planning horizon.

Architecture, in contrast to Dreamer

  • Latent dynamics: an encoder maps observations to a deterministic latent; a transition net predicts next-latent and reward. Crucially, no observation reconstruction loss — the latent is shaped purely by reward and value gradients (and a TD consistency term), not pixel-level supervision. This is closer in spirit to JEPA: predict in representation, not pixels.
  • Planning: MPPI samples action sequences, evaluates them via H-step latent rollouts plus a learned terminal value Q(z_H, a_H), and re-fits a Gaussian over elite returns.
  • Policy prior: a stochastic actor is trained alongside (SAC-style) to seed MPPI's sampling distribution — combining amortised policy with online search.
  • Value: a Q-ensemble with double-Q targets handles overestimation.

The TD-MPC2 contribution over TD-MPC is scaling: the same hyperparameters work across 80+ continuous-control tasks (DMC, Meta-World, Maniskill, MyoSuite) and across model sizes from 1M to 317M parameters, including a single multi-task agent trained on 80 tasks at once that matches or beats specialists.

Comparison to Dreamer family

  • Both predict in latent space and plan in imagination.
  • DreamerV3 trains an actor with reparameterised gradients; TD-MPC2 plans with MPPI then refines a sample-based actor — closer to classical MPC.
  • DreamerV3's RSSM reconstructs pixels; TD-MPC2's encoder is reward-shaped only.
  • DreamerV3 covers discrete (Atari, Minecraft, Crafter) and continuous; TD-MPC2 is continuous-control focused but with stronger results on dexterous manipulation suites.
  • Roughly: DreamerV3 = generality across modalities; TD-MPC2 = depth on continuous control with cleaner latent representations.

Relevance

Sits opposite source-dreamerv3 in concept-world-models: the same predict-then-plan core, different choices about what the latent encodes (reward-only vs reconstructive) and how actions are chosen (MPC sampling vs analytic policy gradients). The reward-only latent makes TD-MPC2 a useful comparison point for concept-representation-learning discussions about whether predictive losses must be at the pixel level — a thread shared with source-ijepa-self-supervised-learning and the predictive-vs-generative axis that becomes central in the Genie / V-JEPA 2 split.
