World Models

concept created 2026-05-06 updated 2026-10-09 world-models model-based-rl generative-models predictive-models planning dreamer genie vla

A world model is a learned generative or predictive model of an environment that an agent can plan, simulate, or act inside — a "dreamable" surrogate for the real world. The defining property is that you can roll it out under hypothetical actions and get back useful predictions of future state, observations, or rewards.

Two structural shapes show up across the lineage:

  • Generative — predict full observations (pixels, video) from past + actions; the agent literally hallucinates next frames. Examples: source-ha-schmidhuber-world-models (MDN-RNN over VAE latents), source-genie-interactive-environments, source-genie-2-foundation-world-model.
  • Predictive — predict only a learned latent / value-relevant signal from past + actions; never reconstruct pixels. Examples: TD-MPC2 (reward-shaped latent), V-JEPA 2 (JEPA-style representation prediction).

Both shapes serve the same role inside an agent: a substrate for planning (PlaNet), policy learning (source-dreamer-v1, source-dreamerv3), or interactive simulation (Genie 2).

Lineage in this wiki

Trunk: V + M + C

  • source-ha-schmidhuber-world-models (Ha & Schmidhuber 2018) — VAE + MDN-RNN + tiny linear controller; agents trained inside dreams. Establishes the canonical decomposition.

Dreamer family (latent dynamics + RL)

  • source-planet-latent-dynamics (Hafner 2018) — RSSM and pure planning via CEM; no policy network.
  • source-dreamer-v1 (Hafner 2019) — actor-critic learned via reparameterised gradients through latent dynamics. The shift from search to amortised policy.
  • source-dreamerv3 (Hafner 2023, Nature 2025) — single hyperparameter set across 150+ tasks; mines a Minecraft diamond from scratch. The "general world-model algorithm" claim.

Continuous-control sibling

  • source-tdmpc2 (Hansen 2023) — reward-shaped latent + MPPI planning + learned value; multi-task scaling on 80+ continuous-control tasks. Closest direct alternative to DreamerV3 for continuous control.

Generative foundation environments

  • source-genie-interactive-environments (Bruce et al. 2024) — 11B foundation model; latent actions discovered from unlabeled video; playable 2D environments.
  • source-genie-2-foundation-world-model (DeepMind Dec 2024) — 3D playable worlds from a single image; emergent physics, NPCs, animation; ~1-minute consistent rollouts.

Predictive sibling (cross-link)

  • source-vjepa-family — JEPA-style predictive video world model; predicts in representation, not pixels. Direct counterpart to Genie 2 in the generative-vs-predictive split.

LLM-as-world-model branch

  • source-rap-reasoning-planning — uses an LLM as the world model with MCTS as the planner. World-modelling without explicit dynamics learning; the LM's prior is the dynamics.
  • source-worldcoder — world model as an editable Python program. Crisp, symbolic, modifiable — opposite extreme of Genie 2's pixel generative model.

Key tensions

  1. Generative vs predictive — do you need to render the world (Genie 2, useful for embodied training, simulators, content creation) or just predict task-relevant latents (V-JEPA 2, TD-MPC2; cheaper, often sample-efficient, but unrenderable).
  2. Learned vs specified dynamics — DreamerV3 learns a generative model and gets generality; VERSES specifies a generative model by hand and gets sample efficiency on specific tasks. Same predict-then-plan structure (concept-active-inference); opposite ends of the learned-vs-specified spectrum.
  3. Plan vs amortise — PlaNet plans online with CEM; DreamerV3 amortises planning into an actor; TD-MPC2 mixes both (MPPI seeded by a learned actor). The same trade-off recurs in LLM agents (RAP tree search vs ReAct amortised tool use).
  4. Pixels vs program — source-worldcoder is the symbolic limit; Genie 2 is the dense-pixel limit; Dreamer/V-JEPA 2 sit in between with continuous latents.

Evidence on tension 1, small scale: source-infoldm-predictive-ssl-identifiability trains a latent predictive model of a MuJoCo hopper under random colours, lighting, camera and background. A linear probe reads the hopper's pose and velocity from its latent (velocity from the LSTM state that sums up past latents); adding an image decoder loss leaves a latent that encodes parts of the nuisance and not the state (raw/arxiv-2609-37789-predictive-ssl-identifies-stochastic-signals-under-nuisance.md, §4.2, Figure 3, Appendix Figure 4).

Connections to other concepts

  • concept-active-inference — predict-and-plan structure is shared. AIF imposes free-energy as the planning objective on a (often hand-tuned) generative model; world-model RL learns the generative model and uses task return.
  • concept-jepa — predictive world models share JEPA's "predict in representation" core. V-JEPA 2 is the explicit fusion.
  • concept-representation-learning — RSSM, VQ-VAE tokenizers (Genie), reward-shaped latents (TD-MPC2) are all latent-design choices for downstream prediction.
  • concept-neural-symbolic-bridge — source-worldcoder sits at the symbolic end of the world-model axis; programs as world models are an explicit neural-symbolic instance.
  • concept-test-time-compute — online planning (CEM, MPPI, MCTS) inside a world model is exactly test-time compute spent on imagination rather than on text reasoning.

Open questions

  • Do generative video world models like Genie 2 deliver enough physical fidelity to train embodied agents that transfer to reality, or is the predictive branch (V-JEPA 2, TD-MPC2) more pragmatic for control?
  • How far does DreamerV3's "single config" claim hold as benchmarks expand to truly open-ended worlds?
  • Where is the seam between LLM-as-world-model (RAP) and learned-dynamics world models? When the LLM is the prior, what role do explicit dynamics models still play?

VLA alternative

Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models are the direct policy counterpart to world-model approaches. See concept-vla-robot-foundation-models for the lineage: source-rt-2, source-openvla, source-pi-zero. Key bridge: source-vjepa-family is a predictive substrate used as a policy via image-goal MPC, sitting between pure world models and pure VLAs.
