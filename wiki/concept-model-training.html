Model Training

concept created 2026-04-05 updated 2026-10-03 training pretraining data optimization

Practical considerations for training language models — architecture choices, data strategies, stability techniques, and post-training pipelines.

Practical Guides

  • source-frontier-model-training — distills 7 frontier models' techniques. Key thesis: data mixture, architecture, and stability dominate algorithmic tweaks. Pipeline: SFT → preference optimization → RL with verifiable rewards.

Compute-Optimal Allocation

  • source-chinchilla-compute-optimal — Hoffmann 2022: N (params) and D (tokens) should scale ~1:1; cited by every model report since 2022.
  • source-kaplan-scaling-laws — Kaplan 2020: original empirical scaling; later corrected by Chinchilla.

Post-Training Pipeline

  • source-instructgpt-rlhf — Ouyang 2022: SFT → reward model → PPO; the canonical post-training pipeline.
  • source-constitutional-ai — Bai 2022: RLAIF variant using a written constitution.
  • source-dpo-direct-preference-optimization — Rafailov 2023: skips the reward model and PPO; now widely the default.
  • source-frontier-learning-edge-of-capability — 2026: RL on problems generated during training, at the levels where the model succeeds sometimes but not always; a fixed pool stops teaching once every problem is always solved or never solved (raw/arxiv-2609-35426-frontier-learning-training-llm-reasoners-at.md, §2, §3.3).

See also concept-rlhf for the canonical reference and concept-rlhf-alternatives for further variants.

Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning

  • source-lora-low-rank-adaptation — Hu 2021: low-rank ΔW = BA; <1% params trained, no inference latency. Precursor to broader concept-retrofitting framing.

Data Strategy

  • source-finephrase-synthetic-data-playbook — 90+ experiments → 500B curated synthetic tokens. Systematic playbook for what makes good synthetic data.
  • source-lacy-small-language-models — not all tokens deserve equal attention; grammar-guided selection for capacity-limited models.

Training Data Curation (formerly its own concept page)

Principled selection and preparation of training data — increasingly recognized as more impactful than algorithmic changes. The hypothesis: at frontier scale, data mixture and quality dominate algorithmic tweaks (cf. source-frontier-model-training's central thesis).

  • source-patterning-dual-of-interpretability — uses susceptibilities to determine what training data produces desired generalization. Data reweighting as a control mechanism.
  • source-finephrase-synthetic-data-playbook — empirical playbook for synthetic data quality.
  • source-frontier-model-training — reserve highest-quality data for late-stage training.

Efficiency

  • source-sequential-attention-google — integrate feature/component selection directly into training via attention-based subset selection.
