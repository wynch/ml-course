Self-Supervised Vision and JEPA Source Bundle

source-bundle created 2026-05-14 updated 2026-05-14 source-bundle self-supervised-learning jepa vision representation-learning world-models meta-fair

This bundle condenses the SSL / JEPA cluster. The recurring question is what target produces useful representations when labels are absent: contrast pairs, decorrelation, masked reconstruction, self-distillation, or latent prediction.

Condensed Synthesis

The cluster has two main axes:

  • Anti-collapse mechanism: negatives, EMA teachers, stop-gradient predictors, variance/covariance penalties, masked targets, dense relational anchoring.
  • Prediction target: pixels/tokens versus learned representations.

The wiki treats JEPA as a sibling to SSL rather than just another SSL variant because the JEPA program turns latent prediction into a world-model claim: the representation should be good enough for planning, not just classification.
