Why it matters here

The two posts were the whole weights-side plan of Vincent's experiment-weights-vs-context: LoRA updates plus distillation from a frozen snapshot. His 1.7B run found the distillation repair worked only on mild damage (source-inkling-thinking-machines). SDFT uses the repair as a baseline (source-sdft-self-distillation-continual).
