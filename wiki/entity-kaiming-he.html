Kaiming He

entity created 2026-05-07 updated 2026-05-07 researcher author meta-fair self-supervised-learning vision computer-vision mae moco

Computer-vision researcher at Meta FAIR (now MIT). In this wiki he is the first author of two foundational vision-SSL papers that bracket the 2019–2021 contrastive-to-masked-modelling transition: MoCo (contrastive, queue + momentum) and MAE (masked autoencoder, pixel reconstruction). The wider corpus also owes him ResNet, but ResNet has no dedicated page here yet.

Sources in the wiki

  • source-ssl-contrastive-foundations — first author of MoCo (He, Fan, Wu, Xie, Girshick, FAIR Nov 2019). Introduced the dictionary-with-a-queue + momentum-encoder pattern that decoupled negatives from batch size and produced the first SSL features to beat supervised ImageNet pretraining on detection / segmentation transfer. MoCo v2 then absorbed SimCLR's projection-head + augmentation tricks.
  • source-masked-image-modeling — first author of MAE (He, Chen, Xie, Li, Dollár, Girshick, FAIR Nov 2021). 75% mask ratio + encoder sees only visible patches + shallow pixel-reconstruction decoder. The pixel-target side of the BERT-for-vision debate that concept-jepa later reacts against.

Themes

Two papers, both first-author, that define two of the three founding vision-SSL recipes. MoCo (contrastive, asymmetric encoders, queue) is one of the templates the field still uses. MAE (masked-and-reconstruct, asymmetric encoder/decoder, pixel target) is the cleanest BERT-for-vision analogue and the explicit foil that the JEPA programme positions itself against — predict in representation space, not pixel space. The momentum-encoder pattern from MoCo also propagates downstream into BYOL → DINO → iBOT → I-JEPA's predictor design, so He's architectural primitives are load-bearing even where he isn't a co-author.
