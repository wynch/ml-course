Self-Supervised Learning

concept created 2026-04-05 updated 2026-10-09 self-supervised pretraining representation-learning contrastive masked-modeling

Learning representations from unlabelled data by manufacturing a supervision signal from the data itself. The vision-side substrate beneath the LLM stack — the equivalent role to next-token prediction in language. Now a populated hub after the May 2026 Phase 2A ingest.

Four families (per Balestriero's source-ssl-cookbook-balestriero taxonomy)

After May 2026 consolidation, the wiki collapses each family into one grouped page:

  • Contrastive + asymmetric — source-ssl-contrastive-foundations covers SimCLR (NT-Xent + aug + projection head), MoCo (momentum encoder + queue), and BYOL (predictor + EMA + stop-gradient; no negatives — opens the non-contrastive door).
  • Decorrelation — source-ssl-decorrelation covers Barlow Twins (cross-correlation → identity) and VICReg (explicit Variance/Invariance/Covariance terms; modular for asymmetric/multimodal joint-embedding).
  • Masked image modeling — source-masked-image-modeling covers MAE (75% mask, asymmetric encoder–decoder, pixel reconstruction; BERT-for-vision) and iBOT (masked patches + EMA-teacher embedding targets + DINO global loss; direct precursor of DINOv2).
  • Synthesis — source-ssl-cookbook-balestriero (collapse-prevention as the unifying lens).

Audio

Predictive (the JEPA branch)

The wiki treats JEPA as a sibling concept rather than a sub-family of SSL because of its emphasis on prediction-in-latent-space as a research programme distinct from contrastive/masked. See concept-jepa.

Cross-cutting themes

Collapse prevention is the central problem

Every method has a different anti-collapse trick: negatives (SimCLR/MoCo), EMA + predictor (BYOL/DINO), redundancy reduction (Barlow), explicit variance term (VICReg), high-mask-ratio reconstruction (MAE), latent prediction (JEPA). The Cookbook frames this as the unifying lens. source-weak-sigreg carries the covariance-toward-identity trick out of SSL entirely, using it as a supervised optimizer stabilizer (SIGReg, from LeJEPA).

Anti-collapse as distribution matching

source-infoldm-predictive-ssl-identifiability reads the entropy term behind InfoNCE, SimCLR and VICReg as one objective with two jobs: keep predictive mutual information, and match the latents to a simple learned predictor; the choice of entropy estimator (kernel density for SimCLR, log-determinant for VICReg) picks the method (raw/arxiv-2609-37789-predictive-ssl-identifies-stochastic-signals-under-nuisance.md, §3). With that, the learned latent equals the true signal up to an affine map, if the optimum is reached (same file, §3.2, Corollary 1). Results swing with the estimator, which the authors call "one of the most pressing open problems" (same file, §5).

From features to foundation models

The source-dino-lineage arc is the cleanest evolutionary thread: DINO (2021, emergent attention) → DINOv2 (2023, 1.1B params on curated 142M images — matches CLIP without text) → DINOv3 (2025, ViT-7B, Gram anchoring stabilises dense features through long training).

Bridge to language modelling

source-masked-image-modeling is BERT-for-vision; conversely, masked language modelling is MAE-for-text. Modern frontier models are blurring the line.
