Conditional Activation Steering and the Steering Ecosystem

source created 2026-04-13 updated 2026-04-13 interpretability steering alignment goodfire 2025

CAST: ICLR 2025 Spotlight. Goodfire Ember: 2025 product release. Field guide: 2026.

CAST makes activation steering conditional: instead of always-on vectors that e.g. make the model refuse everything, CAST analyzes activations at inference time and decides whether to steer. Goodfire's Ember platform productizes feature-level steering over open SAEs on Llama-3.3-70B and Llama-3.1-8B, with an "Auto Steer" that picks the right features from a short prompt. Goodfire also demonstrated SAE circuit replication and reasoning-model feature analysis ("Under the Hood of a Reasoning Model").

Neural↔symbolic bridge

Steering is the practical side of the symbolic bridge: if you can name a rule ("refuse this class of prompts," "avoid flattery") and directly manipulate the corresponding features, you have a symbolic control surface on a subsymbolic substrate. Conditional steering adds the missing "if" — turning a blunt bias vector into a miniature rule engine.
