Mathilde Caron

entity created 2026-05-07 updated 2026-05-07 researcher author meta-fair self-supervised-learning vision dino

Vision self-supervised-learning researcher at Meta FAIR. In this wiki she is the first author of DINO — the paper that lit the fuse on self-distillation as the dominant SSL paradigm — and the lead of what the wiki groups as the concept-dino-family arc. Author of SwAV in the prior generation (not yet a dedicated page).

Sources in the wiki

  • source-dino-lineage — first author of DINO (Caron, Touvron, Misra, Jégou, Mairal, Bojanowski, Joulin, ICCV 2021, arXiv 2104.14294). Showed that self-distillation with a momentum teacher + centering/sharpening on Vision Transformers produces emergent semantic segmentation in attention maps — a result that retroactively reframed what SSL features were learning. The seed paper of the DINO → DINOv2 → DINOv3 progression the wiki tracks.

Implicit / context-only

SwAV (Caron et al., 2020) — clustering-based contrastive SSL — is referenced contextually in the SSL pages but does not yet have a dedicated source page. A DINOv2 (Oquab) and DINOv3 (Siméoni) summary share the source-dino-lineage page where Caron's DINO is the founding entry.

Themes

Caron's wiki footprint is concentrated in one load-bearing paper: DINO is the bridge between BYOL-style asymmetric self-distillation and the "vision foundation model" era that DINOv2 and DINOv3 inhabit. The recurring co-author cluster — Caron, Oquab, Bojanowski, Touvron, Misra, Joulin — is essentially the Meta FAIR vision-SSL team that the wiki cites whenever DINO-family or joint-embedding methods come up. The DINO result is also one of the wiki's clearest data points for the LeCun thesis that vision representations don't require language supervision.
