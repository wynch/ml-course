OpenAI privacy-filter

source created 2026-05-06 updated 2026-05-06 open-source privacy token-classification encoder-models mixture-of-experts 2026 openai

OpenAI, Apache-2.0, 2026

A 1.5B-parameter (50M active) bidirectional token-classification model for detecting and masking PII in text. Built by post-training an autoregressive checkpoint into a bidirectional classifier — a notable architectural inversion. Uses constrained sequence decoding to produce coherent BIOES span labels rather than generating text autoregressively.

Architecture

  • Pre-norm transformer encoder, 8 blocks.
  • Grouped-query attention: 14 query heads, 2 KV heads.
  • Sparse Mixture-of-Experts: 128 experts, top-4 routing → 1.5B total / 50M active.
  • Token-classification head with constrained BIOES decoding.
  • 128k context window.

PII Categories (8)

Account numbers, private addresses, private emails, private person identifiers, private phone numbers, private URLs, private dates, secrets.

Why It's Interesting

  • Encoder-from-decoder retrofit — a clean instance of the source-retrofitting-models-minixhofer framing: take an autoregressive checkpoint, retrofit it into a bidirectional classifier with a supervised classification loss.
  • MoE in an encoder — sparse expert routing is rare in encoder-only architectures; one of the first open-source examples at this scale.
  • Practical artefact, not just a paper — Apache-2.0 weights for on-prem privacy workflows. PII tooling as a layer of the alignment stack distinct from RLHF/CAI.
