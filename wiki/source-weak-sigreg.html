Weak-SIGReg: Covariance Regularization for Stable Deep Learning

source created 2026-07-07 updated 2026-07-07 self-supervised-learning decorrelation representation-learning model-training regularization dimensional-collapse vision-transformers

Author: Habibullah Akbar (Kreasof AI, Jakarta) · arXiv: 2603.05924v1 · Date: 2026-03-06 Code: github.com/kreasof-ai/sigreg

Summary

Takes SIGReg (Sketched Isotropic Gaussian Regularization) — an anti-collapse objective from the SSL world (LeJEPA) — and repurposes it as a supervised optimizer stabilizer. The proposed Weak-SIGReg is a cheap variant: instead of matching the full empirical characteristic function of embeddings to an isotropic Gaussian, it regularizes the covariance matrix directly via random sketching (Randomized Numerical Linear Algebra), pushing sketched covariance toward identity with a Frobenius penalty. Memory drops from O(C²) to O(CK). It behaves like "soft batch normalization" with no architectural change.

Key claims

  • Rescues collapsed training. A ViT + AdamW + augmentation run stuck at 20.73% on CIFAR-100 recovers to 72.02% with Weak-SIGReg added.
  • General, not just SSL. Works as a plain supervised regularizer; a 6-layer MLP + SGD goes 26.77% → 42.17%.
  • Safe by default. Already-stable ResNet18 (~79–82%) is unharmed — argues it can be an always-on default.
  • Complementary to modern optimizers. +12.12% when stacked on the Muon optimizer; even beats expert-tuned ViT (70.76% → 71.65%).
  • Mechanism: constrains representation density toward isotropy, directly preventing dimensional collapse.

Why it matters for the wiki

This is the wiki's collapse-prevention theme (concept-self-supervised-learning — "collapse prevention is the central problem") crossing the border from SSL into supervised optimization. The decorrelation family (source-ssl-decorrelation: Barlow Twins' cross-correlation→identity, VICReg's explicit covariance term) was born to stop joint-embedding SSL from collapsing; Weak-SIGReg shows the same covariance-toward-identity trick stabilizes ordinary ViT/MLP training. It reframes an anti-collapse regularizer as an optimizer-agnostic training aid rather than an SSL-specific loss.

Connections

  • Decorrelation lineage: direct descendant of source-ssl-decorrelation (VICReg's covariance term, Barlow's redundancy reduction) — but applied as an internal regularizer, not a view-consistency loss.
  • SIGReg / LeJEPA origin: the strong form comes from LeJEPA, the LeCun-lineage JEPA programme (entity-yann-lecun, concept-jepa). The wiki did not previously cover SIGReg — this is its entry point.
  • Dimensional collapse / isotropy: concept-representation-learning — sits with source-drifting-model-representation (codebook collapse) and source-contrastive-learning-gaussian-representations (contrastive objectives → Gaussian/isotropic marginals) as another "shape the representation's density" result.
  • Training/optimization: concept-model-training — a regularizer that substitutes for careful hyperparameter tuning and complements Muon/AdamW.
  • Synthesis home: source-ssl-cookbook-balestriero (collapse-prevention as the unifying lens).

Caveats

  • Single-author, CIFAR-100-scale evidence; no ImageNet/large-scale confirmation.
  • "Beats expert tuning" is a strong framing from a narrow benchmark set.
