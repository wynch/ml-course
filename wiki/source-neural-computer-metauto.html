Neural Computer: A New Machine Form Is Emerging

source created 2026-04-13 updated 2026-04-13 neural-symbolic runtime differentiable-computer architecture anchor

Mingchen Zhuge — MetaAuto AI, 2026

The anchor paper for this wiki's thesis that neural and symbolic are two parts of a single machine-learning architecture.

Zhuge argues that AI is converging on a new machine form that is neither agent, world model, nor von-Neumann computer. A Neural Computer (NC) organizes around runtime — the persistent computational substrate that keeps a machine recognizably itself across time, internalizing capabilities rather than delegating them to external toolchains. Classical computers execute explicit programs; agents plan over environments; world models predict states; an NC absorbs all of these into a single learned runtime where inputs can become installable, callable routines.

The CNC criteria

The mature form — a Completely Neural Computer (CNC) — must satisfy four criteria simultaneously:

  1. Turing complete
  2. Universally programmable — inputs compile into reusable routines
  3. Behavior-consistent — no silent drift from ordinary use; updates leave trackable traces
  4. Machine-native semantics — its own programming paradigm, not merely neural imitation of x86

Transitional prototypes

  • CLIGen (General) — video models render terminal interfaces (~1,100 hours training) with realistic TUI dynamics.
  • CLIGen (Clean) — probes state-transition semantics where pwd, date, whoami partly behave like their referents. Arithmetic is unreliable — the honest gap.
  • GUIWorld — mouse/keyboard GUI control; finds that injecting actions inside a transformer block beats shallow conditioning, and supervised cursor visualization beats raw coordinates.

Neural↔symbolic bridge

The thesis: the split is temporary and architectural, not ontological. The symbolic substrate is the runtime's installable, trackable routines and callable sub-networks; the neural substrate is the learned dynamics that render, extend, and execute them. Zhuge speculates the hardware path isn't denser 1B–10T dense models but sparser, more addressable, more circuit-like 10T–1000T substrates — inheriting from Graves's Differentiable Neural Computer (source-dnc-graves), Schmidhuber's differentiable world, Neural Programmer-Interpreter, and HyperNetworks. The call is for learned runtimes that are more discrete and routable than today's dense transformers.
