Rethinking Thinking Tokens: LLMs as Improvement Operators

source created 2026-04-05 updated 2026-04-05 llm-reasoning inference test-time-compute thinking-tokens

Authors: Lovish Madaan, Aniket Didolkar, Suchin Gururangan, John Quan, Ruan Silva, Ruslan Salakhutdinov, Manzil Zaheer, Sanjeev Arora, Anirudh Goyal Date: 2025-10-01 arXiv: 2510.01123

Summary

Proposes Parallel-Distill-Refine (PDR), an inference approach that generates diverse drafts in parallel, distills them into a bounded textual workspace, and refines conditioned on it. An 8B thinking model trained with RL aligned to PDR yields +11% on AIME 2024 and +9% on AIME 2025 over single-pass baselines.

Key Ideas

  • Three-phase inference: parallel draft generation → distillation → refinement
  • Bounded workspace prevents unbounded reasoning chains
  • RL training aligned specifically to the PDR framework
  • Significant gains on math competition benchmarks (AIME)
