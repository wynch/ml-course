Spotlight Heist

Game · Attention ← All games

Each job is a line of word cards. The last card holds the torch and throws a beam at every card before it. Drag a card up to make its beam brighter, down to dim it. The vault reads what the light brings back.

The maths is real: each beam's share is a softmax of the brightness you set, and each lock reads the share-weighted blend of what the cards carry. The numbers in the last card come from the course's own run of an open language model on Wikipedia text.
