Lab Tycoon

Game · Growing a lab ← All games

You run a young AI lab. Each year the chips give you a budget of compute, counted in FLOP: one FLOP is one sum or one product your machines work out. Spend it on a bigger model, one with more parameters (the numbers it tunes as it learns), or on more reading: more tokens, the word pieces it learns from. Rival labs have their own ideas. Beat them.
