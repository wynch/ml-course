Neuron Detective

Game 8 · Looking inside ← All games

Lamps keep lighting up inside a small text-reading machine. Each lamp is a neuron: one number inside the machine that rises when the text holds what it looks for. Read the evidence, watch who lights up, and write down what each one is after. Then lock in your notes and face the trial: say who lights up on text you have never seen.

Suspects

Tap a suspect to rank the evidence by how bright it glows and to see which words light it.

Your notes are all you have. Tap each suspect you think lights up on this snippet, then check.

The machine is hand-built: each neuron adds up weighted clues word by word, keeps only the positive part, and squashes the total into a glow between 0 and 1, with a little fixed noise per snippet. A glow of 0.50 or more counts as lit; under 0.25 counts as dark; in between, either answer counts. To close a case, call 80% of the lamps right and catch at least 70% of the ones that light: saying "dark" to everything will not do.
